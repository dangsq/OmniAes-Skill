# Baddie

## Overview

Baddie is the house style of the 2010s attention economy: born on Instagram feeds and YouTube beauty tutorials (it is also called *Beauty Guru*), it centers on being a conventionally attractive, sexually confident woman and treating that presentation as a daily, visible craft. It is heavily influenced by African American and Latina urban style, by Kardashian/Jenner glamour, and by the commercial ecosystem of Fashion Nova, Forever 21 and Adidas. Because it is trend-driven, it absorbs whatever is current — which is exactly why its constants matter: the constructed face, the engineered hair, the streetwear-plus-glam uniform, and the snapshot-flash confidence. A baddie image is never accidental; even its "candid" frames are performances of being caught looking expensive.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette (rule, not recipe)

The core palette is six families: **black, red, camo, beige/nude, white, pink**. A image reads baddie when at least one core family anchors the outfit or backdrop, and black or red usually grounds the frame.

| Family | Hex range | Typical carriers |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `#0a0a0a–#262626` | bodysuits, graphic tees, balaclavas, leather jackets, liner |
| Red | `#b3001b–#ff2d2d` | tracksuits, sneakers, lips, heels |
| Pink (hot → baby) | `#ff4fa3–#ffc9de` | dresses, faux fur, room lighting, studio backdrops, flat lays |
| Beige / nude | `#c8a284–#e9d5c0` | mesh bodysuits, foundation-matched skin, nude nails, knit sets |
| White | `#f4f4f4–#ffffff` | fur coats, crop tops, sneakers, gloves |
| Camo olive-brown | `#5f6b4f–#8c7b5b` | cargo pants, jackets (canonical per text, low-frequency in practice) |

Secondary accents are permitted but must not replace the core: volt/neon (`#d4ff00`), magenta room glow (`#ff00cc`), rhinestone silver (`#cfd4da`), gold chain (`#d4af37`). Neon appears as a single garment or a light source, never as the whole palette.

### Signature motifs and objects (ranked by textual authority)

1. **The constructed face** — the style's key motif is "bold makeup and fashion": very thick sculpted eyebrows, dark winged eyeliner, cut-crease eyeshadow, long false lashes, heavy contour with bright highlighter, dewy skin, and glossy lips (clear lip-gloss shine is non-negotiable).
2. **Engineered hair** — long dark straight wigs, colored wigs (pink, green, orange, lavender all valid), braids, curly/wavy sets, laid edges ("baby hair"), waves, barrettes, hair scarves and du-rags. Hair is an event, never just hair.
3. **Streetwear-glam wardrobe** — crop tops, tube tops, bodysuits, tight or ripped jeans, camo pants, graphic T-shirts, sweatpants and hoodies (hoodie strings tied in a bow), tracksuits; sneakers from expensive lines (Vans, Nikes, Adidas); name-brand clothing worn logo-visible.
4. **Nails and jewelry** — long acrylic nails (chrome, glitter, french, rhinestone), hoop earrings, chunky chains, crystal chokers.
5. **The flex** — name-brand logos, designer monogram bags, and stacks or props of cash; abundance displayed casually, as evidence rather than aspiration.
6. **Peripheral, image-observed (text-silent, optional)** — rhinestone or `$$`-printed ski masks/balaclavas borrowed from trap album art; sparkle and lens-flare overlays on beauty portraits; digital-avatar illustrations of the same look; accessory still lifes and flat lays (heels, nails, money, fur) standing in for the figure.

### Materials and textures

Gloss against plush against glitter: lip gloss, vinyl and satin sheen; faux fur, feather trim and velvet/devoré; rhinestones, chrome nails and crystal pave; matte cotton fleece and jersey tees; distressed denim; sheer mesh and latex-look stretch fabric; metallic chain. A correct frame usually couples at least two of these registers (e.g., matte hoodie + glossy lips + crystal nails).

### Recurring subjects

Predominantly women posing with self-possession — chin up, direct gaze or deliberate disregard. Also: crews of friends stacked together outdoors; beauty close-ups (lips plus choker, hand plus nails); salon and mirror-selfie moments; accessory still lifes and cash flat lays; album-cover-style graphics with typographic overlays.

### Composition and lighting tendencies

Four lighting modes form the family: (a) **harsh direct on-camera flash** in dark interiors — cars, clubs, bedrooms — with blown highlights and a hard shadow; (b) **hard daylight or golden hour** on concrete walls, stoops and rooftops, casting a crisp body shadow; (c) **soft pink studio glow** for beauty-guru portraits, seamless backdrop, optional sparkle overlays; (d) **neon/magenta room light** for mirror and bedroom frames. Composition favors the outfit check (full body against a plain wall), the torso crop emphasizing waist and nails, the crouch or kneel, the mirror selfie with phone visible, and the macro of a single luxury detail (heel, nail set, bag). Crops skew square or vertical, phone-camera native; slight overexposure and snapshot grain are features, not flaws.

## Mood & Values

The infobox values are the whole ethos: **being confident, looking good, wearing makeup**. Grooming is discipline and identity, not decoration. Sex appeal is delivered with unbothered cool — the subject is never pleading for attention, only documenting that she has it. Abundance (cash, logos, fur, rhinestones) is shown with a shrug. There is crew loyalty in the group frames and a dry humor in the flex (a milk bottle held like champagne, a `$$` mask). Above all: effortlessness about the effort — hours of contour presented as a Tuesday.

## Era & Origins

2010s, United States, platform-native: Instagram models and YouTube beauty gurus, diffused from Black and Latina urban style and Kardashian/Jenner glamour into a global template, commercially serviced by Fashion Nova, Forever 21 and Adidas. It descends partly from McBling's logo love and sits adjacent to Barbiecore, Bimbocore, Cherry Emoji Twitter, Chonga, Femme Fatale, Guido, Hot Cheeto Girl and Paty de SP. Because it evolves with trends, its silhouettes update while its face craft, hair craft and flash-lit confidence persist.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Build the face first: sculpted thick brows, winged liner, cut crease, lashes, contour-plus-highlighter, dewy skin, glossy lips. No bare-face baddie.
2. Make the hair an event: long and sleek, curled, braided, or a saturated-color wig; edges laid.
3. Dress in streetwear-glam tension: pair at least one athletic/street item (hoodie, tracksuit, sneakers, ripped jeans, camo) with at least one glam item (bodysuit, fur, heels, crystals, mesh).
4. Let grooming details carry close-ups: nails, hoops, chains, gloss.
5. Choose one lighting mode from the family (flash-night, hard sun, pink studio, neon room) and commit to it.
6. Pose with ownership: chin up, gaze direct or dismissive; the flex is casual, never eager.
7. Keep the medium honest: phone-native snapshot, mirror selfie, beauty thumbnail, album-cover graphic, or avatar render — not medium-format editorial.

**Variation axes (all open)**

- *Subject*: solo portrait, friend crew, accessory still life, cash/pink flat lay, salon moment, album cover, illustrated avatar.
- *Setting*: bedroom, car interior, club, salon, kitchen, stoop, rooftop, concrete wall, seamless pink studio.
- *Composition*: full-body outfit check, torso crop, macro detail, mirror selfie, crouch, flat lay.
- *Color handling*: any weighting of the six core families; monochrome red or pink statements allowed; neon or magenta as accent/light only.
- *Lighting*: any of the four modes; flash overexposure and hard shadows welcome.
- *Mood*: flexing, casual-laughing, cold glam, playful meme.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image. A rooftop in Seoul, a kitchen in São Paulo, or an illustrated avatar can all be perfect baddie if the face craft, wardrobe logic, palette and lighting mode hold.

**Avoid**

- Bare faces, "clean girl" minimalism, natural brows, matte unglossed lips.
- Boho, cottagecore, or soft naturalist styling; linen, florals, undone hair.
- Faded film-grain vintage nostalgia; baddie flash is crisp and hot, not desaturated.
- Old-Hollywood couture gowns and noir smoky elegance (that is Femme Fatale).
- Pastel coquette bows, plush-toy infantilism and soft-focus blonde ditzy play as the dominant register (Bimbocore).
- Masculine club-frat styling: gel-spiked hair, tank-top guidos, muscle cars.
- Snack-meme props and schoolyard juvenile uniforms (Hot Cheeto Girl).
- Anonymous e-commerce mannequin flatness; the frame must feel self-shot or self-owned.
- Pre-2010s retro silhouettes (pin-up, swing dresses, rockabilly).

## Discrimination Cues

| Neighbor | The tell that separates Baddie |
|---|---|
| **Bimbocore** | Bimbocore is pastel, blonde, soft-focus, 2000s-revival cute with plush infantilism; baddie keeps a streetwear spine (hoodies, tracksuits, sneakers), contoured Instagram makeup, flash-snapshot grit and cash/logo flex. |
| **Cherry Emoji Twitter** | Cherry Emoji Twitter narrows to cherry-red accents and retro Chicana/Latina glamour with emoji playfulness; baddie's red is sportswear red (tracksuits, sneakers) and its face is contemporary contour-and-lash craft, not vintage styling. |
| **Femme Fatale** | Femme Fatale is cinematic noir: gowns, smoky eyes, muted jewel tones, filmic light, mystery. Baddie is logo-visible, snapshot-lit, platform-native, and projects confidence rather than danger. |
| **Guido** | Guido is Northeastern Italian-American 2000s club culture — tanned skin, gelled hair, Ed Hardy tees, muscle cars, reality-TV framing, mixed-gender. Baddie is feminine-centered, wig-and-lash craft, Instagram era. |
| **Hot Cheeto Girl** | Hot Cheeto Girl is a teen meme register: red snack bags, schoolyard snapshots, hoops and nails over juvenile uniforms. Baddie shares the hoops and nails but is adult glam: designer logos, cash, full makeup architecture, fashion-brand wardrobe. |

When in doubt, ask three questions: is the face visibly constructed (brows, liner, lashes, gloss)? is there streetwear-glam tension in the outfit? does the light feel like a phone flash, hard sun, or pink studio glow? Three yeses is baddie.
