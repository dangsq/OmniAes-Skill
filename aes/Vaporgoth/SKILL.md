# Vaporgoth

Vaporgoth is what happens when vaporwave's nostalgia curdles. Coined in 2015 by the artist Chinese Hackers for Bandcamp releases like `░▒▓死▓▒░` and `wiretrap`, it keeps vaporwave's materials — sampled memory, Japanese typography, early-3D virtuality, lo-fi tape hiss — but strips out the melody, the pastel, and the irony, replacing them with dark ambient dread, industrial murk and horror imagery. Alias names Deathdream, Spookwave and Blackvapor circle the same territory: eerie, death-themed, technologically haunted vaporwave. Its natural habitat is the square album cover: a degraded artifact pretending to be a recovered file, a intercepted broadcast, or a cassette pulled from a dead archive.

## Visual Identity

### Palette

The spine of the palette is monochrome: void black, ash grey, and blown bone-white. Saturated color is rationed — at most one accent hue per image, appearing as a glow, a tint, or the color of a single isolated object, never as an overall atmosphere.

| Role | Range | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Field black | `#000000`–`#0D0D0D` | Dominant ground; swallows edges and vignettes |
| Ash grey | `#2B2B2B`–`#8C8C8C` | Fog, static, concrete, photocopy midtones |
| Blown white | `#E6E6E6`–`#FFFFFF` | Overexposed skies, glowing panels, mask surfaces |
| Phosphor green | `#00C853`–`#39FF6A` | CRT wash, sickly organic tint |
| CRT cyan / pale teal | `#00B3B3`–`#BFE8E6` | Inset photo casts, title-card outlines |
| Blood red | `#8A0303`–`#D40000` | Corrupted flesh, dithered horror faces, seal marks |
| Grave violet | `#3D2B56`–`#663399` | Low-poly virtual interiors |
| Rare flares | magenta `#FF2E97`, sodium orange `#FF5A00` | One-object accents (a drowning hand, a dithered pumpkin), always contained by dark framing |

Duotone treatments (red-on-black, green-on-black, orange-on-black, violet-on-black) are fully in range. Pastel dominance is never in range.

### Signature motifs, ranked by textual authority

1. **Glitch art and corrupted imagery** — the text names it first: pixel sorting, datamosh smears, dither matrices, JPEG rot, chromatic fringing, scanline rasters. Corruption is weather, not ornament.
2. **Technological decay / "technological antiquities"** — obsolete hardware as relic: candlestick telephones, CRTs, cassettes, modems; flat-shaded low-poly rooms and beige early-CGI halls rendered like excavated futures.
3. **Horror imagery** — blank Noh-like masks, dithered screaming faces, jack-o'-lanterns, hooded processions, silhouettes with lit eyes, flesh-like organic matter mid-corruption.
4. **Cryptic text** — katakana strings, fullwidth Latin (`Ｖｉｒｔｕａｌ Ｅａｒｔｈ`), block elements `░▒▓`, runs of question marks, catalog codes (`VA:10`, `SC-106`). Set small, in thin outlined title cards or vertical spine strips; never explanatory.
5. **Dystopian transmissions and surveillance** — SSTV static, test-pattern insets, wiretap paranoia, broadcast noise. Strongly asserted by the text; in imagery it surfaces indirectly as static fields, framed "coverage" panels and intercepted-signal framing.
6. **The uncanny and surreal** — faceless doubles, mirrored kaleidoscope collages, mannequins, figures dwarfed by empty architecture, orbs hovering over barren ground.
7. **Industrial and brutalist shells** — ribbed concrete corridors, factory ridgelines with smoke, domed halls. Secondary: the text lists Industrial as a neighbor rather than a core motif, but empty machine-space recurs as a stage for the above.

### Materials and textures

Scanline raster, VHS smear, cassette J-card paper, photocopy grain, dither screens, analog noise floor, flat-shaded polygons, chrome spheres, haze and fog, corrupted-file blocking. Everything reads as a copy of a copy: contrast crushed to black-and-white extremes or washed to a single phosphor tint.

### Composition and lighting

Recurring structural habits, none mandatory: the square cover format; an inset image panel floating in a wide black or grainy margin; a narrow left obi/spine strip carrying vertical Japanese text, a small red seal and a catalog number; a centered bordered title card pairing Japanese and English; perfect mirror symmetry; a lone object or figure centered in void; horizon silhouettes against a blown-white sky. Lighting is never natural daylight: backlit silhouette against overexposed white, a single practical glow (screen, window, lamp orb, moon-sphere), flat scanner-like murk with no key light, or a phosphor tint washing the whole frame while edges drown in black.

## Mood & Values

Darkness, alienation, dread, technological horror, surrealism. Vaporgoth values the haunted over the functional: technology is remembered as corpse, transmission as ghost, memory as corruption. Anonymity is ethic as well as aesthetic — aliases, question-mark titles, faceless masks. Lo-fi degradation is treated as truthfulness: the rot *is* the content. Against vaporwave's wistful or satirical nostalgia, Vaporgoth offers nostalgia with the lights off — mourning for futures that already decayed.

## Era & Origins

Mid-2010s, born in the experimental corners of Bandcamp's vaporwave scene. A reaction against the melodic, upbeat turn of subgenres like Future Funk: artists stripped pop elements out of vaporwave and fused it with dark ambient, industrial and noise — low-octave drones, atonal melodies, heavy reverb, degraded-tape production. Chinese Hackers' 2015 releases set the blueprint; HKE's aliases (The Darkest Future, チェスマスター) contributed the language of dystopian late-night transmissions and technological antiquities; VVVX Software, 骨架的, .mp3Neptune, Humanfobia and others extended it. Deathdream later named the death-themed edge of the same current.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.** Build the image as a degraded artifact, not a clean picture: decide what medium is failing (tape, broadcast, corrupted file, early 3D render) and let that medium's defects structure the frame. Lay down a monochrome base first, then admit at most one accent hue as glow or tint. Apply two or three degradation operators (scanlines, dither, pixel sort, datamosh, grain, chromatic aberration, blocking) — enough to read as decay, not so many that subject vanishes. Include at least one carrier of the haunted: an obsolete device, an uncanny figure, an empty machine-space, a cryptic glyph-string. Keep stillness: no action, no smiles, no narrative resolution. If text appears, it withholds meaning.

**Variation axes.** Subject may be any object, figure or space that can carry a haunted-technological or uncanny charge: telephones, monitors, masks, pumpkins, reaching hands, hovering orbs, corridors, dome halls, factory ridges, low-poly rooms, robed processions, mannequins, static fields. Composition may range from full-bleed corrupted texture to inset-panel-in-void, spine-strip layouts, centered relic, mirrored symmetry, or dwarfed-figure wide shots. Lighting may range from blown-white backlight to single-glow darkness to flat phosphor murk. Color handling may range from pure monochrome through duotone to monochrome-plus-one-accent, with rare saturated flares permitted only when contained by dark framing. Mood may range from quiet desolation to overt horror. Degradation intensity may range from a whisper of grain to near-total collapse.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image. No reference composition, subject or framing is canonical.

**Avoid.** Pastel-dominant vaporwave airiness (candy pink, sky blue, teal atmospheres), palm trees, Greek busts, mall interiors, cheerful consumer irony. Synthwave chrome, neon sunset grids, hot pink-purple gradients. Busy multi-hue palettes or competing saturated accents. Clean vector crispness or pristine photorealism with no degradation pass. Golden hour, sunny daylight, warm cozy domestic light. Traditional goth subculture signifiers — blackletter, bats, coffins, Victorian mourning lace; the "goth" here is technological, not romantic. Airbrushed horror-metal illustration polish and ornate band logos. Amateur camera-flash snapshot mundanity with plain sans-serif captions. Pure formalist colorful glitch with no monochrome dread anchor. Legible friendly marketing typography, brand logos, meme text, humor, crowds, motion.

## Discrimination Cues

| Neighbor | Their tell | Vaporgoth's tell |
|---|---|---|
| **Glitch Art** | Corruption is the subject and the celebration; source imagery often colorful or neutral; formalist, gallery-clean framing | Corruption is weather over a monochrome, horror-charged scene; packaged as music artifact (spine strips, catalog codes, title cards); mood is dread, not experiment |
| **Industrial** | Machines, rust, steel, labor, documentary starkness; rust-brown and gunmetal palettes; power and force | Decay is digital — scanlines, dither, corrupted files; spaces are empty and spectral; vaporwave lineage (Japanese type, cassette spines, early CGI); horror rather than mechanical might |
| **Vaporwave** | Pastel pink/cyan/teal, daylight malls, busts, palms, wistful or satirical nostalgia | Same DNA (Japanese text, lo-fi, virtuality) inverted: black-dominant, horror-valenced, dread; shared motifs appear rotted, not fond |
| **Weirdcore** | Amateur snapshot aesthetics, camera flash, mundane liminal rooms, childlike cryptic sans-serif captions, dream-confusion | Designed cover graphics, monochrome plus phosphor, glitch and file-rot rather than snapshot blur, occult-technological horror rather than uncanny mundanity |

Quick recognition check: black field dominant? one rationed accent hue? visible medium-decay (scanlines, dither, datamosh, grain)? an obsolete technology or faceless figure? text that withholds rather than explains? stillness and dread? If most answers are yes, it is Vaporgoth.
