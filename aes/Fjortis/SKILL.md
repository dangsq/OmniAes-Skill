# Fjortis

Fjortis — from Swedish *fjorton*, "fourteen" — began as a 1990s insult for teenagers acting older than they were and hardened during the mid-to-late 2000s into a codified Swedish youth aesthetic. It is the Scandinavian cousin of the British Chav, the American Guido and Japan's Gyaru: a status performance built on artificial tanning, bleached hair and a party-first lifestyle, amplified by the early Swedish social web (LunarStorm, Playahead, Bilddagboken) and the blog boom of Kissie and Paow. Though the slur was unisex, the visual style is overwhelmingly female-coded; the male branch split off into Partille-Johnny. What makes Fjortis visually unmistakable is its *deliberate artificiality*: makeup applied not to flatter but to announce, with a high-contrast palette painted directly onto the body — orange skin, white mouth, platinum hair — and photographed with the cheap cameras of the era.

## Visual Identity

### Palette

The colour language is a body-first contrast scheme, then a wardrobe of neon and sportswear neutrals:

| Role | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fake-tan skin | `#B96A2A` – `#D98E4A` | Orange bronzer/foundation, always darker than the neck |
| Lips | `#EDE6DA` – `#F7F3EC` | Chalk-white, ointment-matte to slightly glossy |
| Hair | `#E6D9A8` – `#F2ECD8` | Platinum / "white-blonde", yellow-white, never ash-natural |
| Neon accent | `#FF3FA4` – `#FF69B4` | Hot pink tops, hearts, prints, phone shells, bedroom walls |
| Wardrobe neutrals | white, heather grey `#B9B9B9`, black | Zip hoodies, tank tops, kajal |
| Denim | light-wash `#A8C4DC` | Low-rise, distressed |

Rule: the face carries the loudest contrast (dark skin against white lips against white hair); clothing supplies one screaming neon note against otherwise casual sportswear neutrals.

### Signature motifs, ranked by textual prominence

1. **The Fjortisrand** — heavy foundation or bronzer several shades too dark, stopping in a sharp, unblended line at the jaw against a pale neck. The style's defining "error."
2. **Chalk-white lips** — a thick pale coat, canonically Idominsalva white ointment; the inverted opposite of conventional lip colour.
3. **Platinum-blonde teased hair** — dyed white-blonde, backcombed at the roots for extreme volume, long straight lengths, side-swept fringe.
4. **Duckface and mysvinkel** — pouted lips; the high-angle "cozy angle" selfie from above, enlarging eyes and cleavage, shrinking the jaw.
5. **Black kajal eyes** — heavy liner and clumped "spider leg" mascara.
6. **Visible push-up bra** — satin trim intentionally showing under tank tops and deep V-necks.
7. **Low-rise tight jeans** (Crocker-type), often distressed, worn low on the hip.
8. **Zip-up hoodies and status outerwear** — Svea, Wesc, Björn Borg hoodies; the Canada Goose expedition parka worn regardless of temperature.
9. **Party props** — cheap cider and energy drinks in hand at parties and clubs.
10. **Period camera objects** — flip phones held into mirrors, compact-digit flash blowouts.

Secondary, snapshot-corroborated props (present in period photos but not text-defining): gold hoop earrings, pearl chokers, oversized white-framed sunglasses, bunny-ear headbands, peace signs held at the cheek, drawn-on whiskers, pink-streaked extensions, pink-painted bedrooms and pink bedding as backdrop.

### Materials and textures

Cakey matte foundation with a visible tide-line; ointment-smeared pale lips; the synthetic shine of bleached or wig-like hair; fake-tan sheen on shoulders and décolletage; cotton jersey hoodies; satin bra edges; distressed denim; canvas sneakers; the plasticky glint of a flip phone. Photographically: low-megapixel sensor noise, flash-hot faces against murky rooms, slight motion blur, 4:3 crops.

### Subjects, composition, lighting

Subjects are teenage girls — alone, cheek-to-cheek in pairs, or squeezed in groups of friends; occasionally a token boy at the edges. Settings are domestic and nocturnal: bedrooms with pink walls, sofas, car and subway interiors, club corners, party kitchens, winter streets under a parka. Composition favours the face-dominant crop: arm's-length wide-angle distortion, the mirror reflection including the phone itself, tilted frames, friends crowding the edges. Lighting is amateur and direct: on-camera flash in a dark room, overexposed window daylight, dim club flash, bathroom fluorescent. The image should look like a profile picture, not a portrait sitting.

## Mood & Values

Fjortis values popularity, social status, partying, conformity and hyper-femininity. It is loud, attention-seeking and proudly artificial — effort made visible, not concealed. The mood oscillates between pouty defiance (duckface, raised chin), giggly play (peace signs, bunny ears, "grrr" captions) and party bravado (drink raised, friends piled in). Underneath sits the era's economy of validation: images made to be posted, rated and copied within a school hierarchy, carrying both the thrill of self-display and the sting of a label that was used to bully the girls who wore it.

## Era & Origins

Term coined in 1990s Sweden (canonized in the 2006 SAOL dictionary); aesthetic peak mid-to-late 2000s, tied to LunarStorm, Playahead and Bilddagboken profile culture, the blog boom, and Fjortisdunk — high-BPM Hands Up/Eurodance descendant of Basshunter with chipmunk vocals and party lyrics. Unlike Chav, it is not class-coded: it policed "bad taste" and immaturity across working-class and affluent (Lidingö) youth alike. It reacts to nothing stylistically; it *performs* adulthood and status from inside adolescence.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.** (1) Artificiality over naturalism: every element should read as bought, dyed, sprayed or smeared. (2) The inverted face: skin darker than the neck with a hard jawline edge, lips paler than everything, hair whiter than everything. (3) Amateur early-digital grammar: flash blowout, noise, mirror reflections, high-angle arm's-length framing. (4) One neon note against sportswear neutrals; brand-conscious but mid-market, not luxury. (5) Conformity in groups: matching poses, matching hair, matching pout.

**Variation axes.** Subject: solo mirror selfie, close-up duckface, two friends, group sofa squeeze, party with cider can, winter exterior in parka, transit interior, or Alternative Fjortis (black hair or "coon tail" streaks, darker clothes, emo/scene crossover — but keeping the heavy foundation and duckface). Composition: face-dominant crop, mirror-with-phone, tilted snapshot, group crowd-frame. Lighting: harsh direct flash, dim club flash, blown daylight, fluorescent. Colour: neon pink dominant, or grey/white hoodie variant, or alt-black with neon streaks — the orange-tan/white-lip/platinum-hair triad persists except in alt variants, where only the makeup grammar and pose persist. Mood: pouty, playful, brash, bored-cool.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image. A winter street, a subway car or an empty kitchen can be perfectly Fjortis if the body palette and camera grammar hold.

**Avoid:** blended professional contour or airbrushed skin; foundation matching the neck; nude, glossy-pink or red lips; natural hair colour or subtle balayage; sleek flat hair without root volume; crisp modern smartphone HDR, ring lights, portrait-mode bokeh; studio softbox lighting or analog film grain; minimalist Scandinavian interiors and muted earth tones; head-to-toe rhinestone velour logomania (McBling); male hi-vis reflex vests and waxed spikes as the focal read (Partille-Johnny); leopard print and Mediterranean glam (Cagole); Burberry-check sportswear and baseball caps (Chav); polished adult glamour styling — the subject must read as a teenager in an ordinary domestic or party space.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Pissikset** (Finnish twin): palette, tan, blonde hair and duckface overlap almost completely; evidence for visual separation is thin. Separate by provenance micro-markers — Fjortis carries the documented Idominsalva white lip and fjortisrand jaw stripe plus Swedish brand set (Svea, Wesc, Björn Borg, Crocker) and Swedish platform ephemera; without locale cues, treat as shared territory.
- **McBling**: American, glossy and consumerist — velour tracksuits, rhinestone logos, metallic bags, blended caramel tan, glossy nude lips, magazine-clean flash. Fjortis is cheaper, sportier, amateur-shot, with white lips and an unblended jaw line.
- **Partille-Johnny**: the male Swedish offshoot — towering waxed fringe, construction reflex vest, rubber bands on calves. If the image's centre of gravity is a boy in hi-vis with gelled spikes, it is Partille-Johnny; Fjortis is anchored in the female makeup grammar.
- **Cagole**: Marseille — sunbed tan but with glossy lips and smoky eyes, leopard print, white vinyl, giant hoops, scooters and Mediterranean light; older age range, no white ointment mouth, no Nordic winter parka layering.
- **Chav**: British — tracksuits, Burberry check, caps, gold rings, estate settings, both genders, makeup uncodified; class-coded sneer. Fjortis is makeup-first, hyper-feminine, status-fluid, and defined by the face triad Chav lacks.
