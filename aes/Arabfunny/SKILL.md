# Arabfunny

Arabfunny is a meme aesthetic that emerged around 2018 from the subreddit r/Arabfunny, built as an affectionate-but-hostile parody of the low-quality chain messages, blessing forwards and "boomer-esque" videos that circulated on WhatsApp and Facebook in the Arab world and South Asia during the early 2000s. Its defining move is intentional degradation: media rendered as if re-uploaded and re-forwarded dozens of times over cellular data, at 144p or 240p, then buried under a chaotic clutter of stickers, emojis, flags, download buttons and untranslated Arabic captions. The result is described as a deliberately "hostile" viewing experience — sensory overload as the joke itself. The style surged through 2019–2020, receded by 2021, and left its editing grammar to later surrealist aesthetics such as Yabujincore.

## Visual Identity

**Color palette.** The structural palette is the Pan-Arab quartet — red, green, white, black — used as flat, unblended sticker color rather than designed harmony: clickbait and flag reds around `#C8102E`–`#FF0000`; WhatsApp-and-piracy-site greens from `#00A859` to `#25D366`, spiking to neon lime `#39FF14` on FREE DOWNLOAD buttons; paper-white `#FFFFFF` collage grounds; pure black `#000000` letterbox bars, text and outlines. Emoji yellow (`#FFCC4D`–`#FFD700`) and arrow orange (`#FF6A00`) act as accent sprinkles, with incidental sky cyan/blue (`#00B7EB`–`#87CEEB`) leaking in from outdoor phone footage or fried noise fields. The rule: saturation swings between washed-out, re-compressed source footage and fully saturated flat sticker layers placed on top. No gradients, no tonal grading, no palette cohesion — colors arrive as flags, buttons, circles, emoji and outlined text.

**Signature motifs, ranked by how centrally the style documents them.**
1. Untranslated Arabic text overlays: bold, heavily outlined (white-on-black, yellow-on-red), often rotated 90° or stretched across faces and bodies.
2. Red clickbait circles and pointing arrows, borrowed from dubious thumbnail grammar.
3. Re-compression artifacts themselves: JPEG macroblocking, chroma smear, banding, soft upscale blur at 144p–240p.
4. Peter Griffin / Family Guy clips, plus a stock roster of Trollface, rage comics and Minecraft gameplay.
5. "FREE DOWNLOAD" buttons and MP3 badges reminiscent of piracy sites; Nokia and Samsung candybar phones as props joking about ringtone and camera quality.
6. Emojis, above all 😂, and generic stock smileys of the Funwaa/Freesmiley type.
7. Flags of Arab nations, most commonly Saudi Arabia or Palestine.
8. Contextless viral animal clips: camels and goats mid-stride.
9. Obscure low-quality 3D animation mascots: Hamood Habibi, Crazy Frog, Gummy Bear, Gnawty the beaver.
10. Loud audio culture (bass-boosted Nokia Arabic ringtones, sirens, autotuned baby crying) which is invisible in stills but surfaces visually as embedded player chrome, volume/speaker icons and MP3 download buttons.

**Materials and textures.** Everything reads as a file, not a photograph: re-saved JPEG and re-exported MP4 surfaces with blocky macroblocks and smeared chroma; MS-Paint cutouts with jagged edges and white halos; glossy sticker emojis; flat scanned-white backgrounds for starter-pack collages; black letterbox bars; corner handle watermarks stamped like provenance marks of re-shares. There is no lens character, no depth of field, no paper or film grain — only digital decay.

**Recurring subjects.** Cartoon fathers in school hallways and living rooms; yellow sponge or cartoon characters dressed in a red-checkered shemagh with black agal; googly-eye swaps; parking-lot and street phone footage; mosque skylines; flash-hot selfie insets; blocky Minecraft figures; Nokia phones; camels, goats; crying babies; Trollface line art; rotated polaroid-style photos scattered on white.

**Composition and lighting.** Three recurring layouts: (a) a letterboxed low-res video frame with picture-in-picture inset rectangles, corner stickers and a bottom watermark; (b) a white-background starter-pack or collage field with photos, buttons and text blocks scattered at competing angles; (c) split panels or a full-bleed fried noise field with arrows and a caption strip. Clutter is mandatory — elements overlap with no hierarchy, captions cut across faces, and empty space survives only as the white canvas of collages, itself peppered with stickers. Lighting is never designed: it is inherited and degraded — fluorescent flatness from cartoon clips, blown daylight from phone footage, flash-hot selfies, or pure scan-white. Contrast is flattened by re-compression; fried variants add noise and chroma blowout on top.

## Mood & Values

Irony, satire, absurdism and shitposting, delivered at maximum volume. The emotional core is parody of one's own parents' forwarding habits — chain messages, blessing graphics, dubious download links — pushed until the overload becomes the punchball. Contextless repetition (a goat running, a siren, a ringtone) is funny precisely because it refuses to explain itself. The community has always held an internal tension: nostalgic, culturally literate satire versus outsider mockery, and the style reads best when the joke is on the media format rather than on people or faith.

## Era & Origins

Late 2010s: r/Arabfunny founded in November 2018, golden age of growth in late 2019 through 2020, decline and subreddit locking by 2021. It reacts to early-2000s Arab and South Asian Facebook/WhatsApp chain culture (Hamood Habibi, Crazy Frog forwards) and borrows the ironic shitposting sensibility of r/DeepFriedMemes-adjacent communities, transplanted into Middle Eastern context. Its technical grammar — ear-splitting distortion, low-res compression, untranslated Arabic overlays — was later re-contextualized by Yabujincore on TikTok.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Degrade last: compose, then downscale to ~240p and re-save with visible JPEG macroblocking, chroma smear and soft blur.
2. Clutter in layers: emoji, flag, button, inset photo, red circle, arrow — several overlapping elements with no visual hierarchy.
3. Arabic text as graphic material: bold, outlined, untranslated, rotated or stretched across the frame.
4. Pan-Arab accents as flat stickers: red circle/arrow, green download button, black bars, white ground.
5. Mismatch sources visibly: cartoon clip + phone selfie + crude 3D mascot + stock smiley in one frame, each with different resolution and lighting.
6. Parody boomer ephemera: FREE DOWNLOAD / MP3 badges, chain-message sincerity, Nokia props, contextless animal clips.
7. Mark the re-share: letterbox bars, corner @-handle watermark, fragment of player or platform UI.

**Variation axes.** Subject matter may be any cartoon character, any animal, any phone-footage scene (parking lot, kitchen, street, mosque skyline), any crude 3D mascot, any selfie. Composition may be a letterboxed video frame, a white starter-pack collage, split panels, or a fried noise field, with element counts from a handful to dozens. Lighting may be flat fluorescent, blown daylight, flash-hot or scan-white — never modeled or dramatic. Color handling may range from washed re-compressed footage to saturated sticker layers to fully fried noise. Mood may run from deadpan chain-message sincerity to maximalist chaos to cursed surreal edge. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

**Avoid.** Crisp high-resolution rendering, clean vector shapes, consistent color grading, aligned grids, tasteful negative space. English-only captions or translated subtitles (Latin script only as watermarks/handles). Gradients, glassmorphism, modern corporate UI. Deep-fry saturation as the sole gesture without the Arab chain-message layer. Anime, jumpstyle or low-poly Meme Man subjects. Gaming-sponsor iconography (Doritos, MLG logos, deal-with-it sunglasses). Minimalist single-subject meme templates. Photorealistic coherence between layered sources. And avoid Islamophobic stereotype tropes or mockery standing in for satire — the parody targets media formats, not people or religion.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Deep Fried Meme:** shares compression abuse, but deep-fry is a universal treatment — glow, lens distortion, nuclear orange/cyan on any subject. Arabfunny's decay mimics re-forwarded cellular files (soft, blocky, washed) and always carries Arab-world ephemera: Arabic script, Pan-Arab flags, WhatsApp green, download buttons.
- **Surreal Meme:** quiet, cryptic, low-poly Meme Man on void backgrounds with deadpan pseudo-philosophy. Arabfunny is loud, referential and littered with real platform debris.
- **Yabujincore:** the descendant keeps the distortion and untranslated Arabic but drops the Pan-Arab parody content for anime, jumpstyle and brainrot subjects, often in vertical TikTok framing; Arabfunny keeps flags, download buttons, family-cartoon clips and chain-message sincerity.
- **Fotonight Web:** eerie early-web photo manipulation with Slavic forum nostalgia and an uncanny darkroom palette. Arabfunny is bright, sticker-loud, parody-forward and Arabic-scripted.
- **MLG:** 2014 gaming neon — airhorns, Doritos, hitmarkers, sunglasses on dark backgrounds. Arabfunny's palette is Pan-Arab on white or letterbox, its props are Nokia phones and camels, not gaming sponsors.
