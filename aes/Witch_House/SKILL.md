# Witch House

Witch House is a dark, occult-themed electronic music microgenre and internet aesthetic that emerged in the late 2000s, whose visual language is inseparable from its sound: slowed, pitched-down "chopped and screwed" samples, droning darkwave synths and trap-derived drum machine beats translated into picture form. The look aims for a dark, surreal, unsettling atmosphere built on a lo-fi, "deep web" sensibility — grainy, distorted, found-footage-style imagery borrowed from horror cinema (the handheld dread of *The Blair Witch Project*, the dreamlike uncanny of *Twin Peaks*) and fused with glitch art and retro computer graphics. It is a deliberately obscure, DIY scene aesthetic: artwork, promo shots and label collages (Phantasma Disques, Mater Suspiria Vision and peers) function as half-hidden ritual documents rather than commercial packaging.

## Visual Identity

### Palette

Black is not an accent but the ground: most of the frame sits in void black and ash charcoal, with every other hue arriving desaturated, as if photocopied or shot on expired film. Keep saturation low (roughly under 40%) except for intentional glitch intrusions.

- Void black and charcoal: `#050505`–`#101014`, `#1c1c1f`–`#3a3a40` — the dominant mass of cloth, shadow and background.
- Bone, dead grass, xerox white: `#b9b3a6`, `#d8d4cc` — flash-blown skin, overcast winter grass, granite, photocopy highlights.
- Oxblood and dark red: `#3d0a0d`–`#7a1220` — stained-glass glow, backlit haze, small stains of color.
- Grave purple and violet: `#241436`–`#5b2a86`, with glitch magenta `#a449c9` reserved for digital error, crystal fields and collage borders.
- Cold blue-teal: `#14262e`–`#3e6b74` — duotone casts, night shade, mirrored-label-art toning. (The text lists blue as a key color; in practice it reads as cold teal-grey cast rather than true blue.)
- Rare phosphor green (`#7fd6a4`-ish) only as a laser beam, pixel-sort streak or corrupted patch — never as a field color.

Rule of thumb: one murk hue (red, purple or teal) may tint a black-dominated scene; bright multicolor palettes break the style.

### Signature motifs (ranked by how firmly the text documents them)

1. **Triangles** — upright △ and inverted ▼, as overlays framing a face or creature, as outline geometry printed on robes, as logo cores of labels and collectives.
2. **Crosses** — ✝ and the inverted †, worn as pendants, drawn over collages, set as lone white glyphs on black fields.
3. **Unicode-obfuscated text** — artist and title names rendered unsearchable with △, †, ╪, Ø, Я, ∆; glyphs used as texture and identity, not as legible messaging.
4. **Occult and religious imagery** — pentagrams, alchemical signs (named in the text, seldom legible in practice), sigil circles, hooded celebrants, animal skulls (ram and goat above all), candles, mirrored angel statues, occult tapestries.
5. **Distorted, lo-fi visuals** — grain, compression artifacts, scan lines, pixel sorting, xerox contrast, low-resolution upscale; the image should look recovered, not produced.
6. **Horror-cinema framing** — found-footage distance, surveillance-like candidness, eerie childhood (robed children, playgrounds), Lynchian domestic uncanny.
7. **Glitch art and retro computer graphics** — datamoshed portraits, pixel-sorted owls and cats, early-CGI roughness, triangle-framed screen artifacts.
8. **Mirrored / kaleidoscopic symmetry** — twin or duplicated figures, reflected hooded celebrants, bilateral collage layouts; frequent in label and promo artwork.
9. **Funerary and ritual space** — cemeteries with granite headstones, dim churches with stained glass, bedrooms turned into shrines with tapestries and candles.

### Materials, textures, medium

Film grain and ISO noise; VHS haze; JPEG crush and banding; photocopy and halftone contrast; cut-and-paste collage edges; draped matte cloth (robes, hoods, hanging fabric); granite and dry winter grass; brick walls; animal bone; cheap printed tapestries; cassette/CD label furniture (obi strips, catalog numbers, logo blocks). Surfaces are never glossy or clean-rendered.

### Recurring subjects

Androgynous figures in layered all-black clothing — hoodies, long draped garments, track jackets and athletic pieces, corsetry over streetwear — with long hair veiling the face, dark sunken eye makeup, shaved-side or spiked hair; duos and twins; groups walking at night; robed children; hands over synths or ritual objects; ram/goat skulls held over faces; lone girls among headstones; crouched figures before occult tapestries; pure glyph graphics (a white inverted cross on noise-black).

### Composition and lighting

Composition behaves like evidence or ritual, never like advertising: off-center snapshot candidness, flat frontal distance, lone centered figure dwarfed by environment, strict bilateral mirroring, sigil overlaid at dead center, or dense collage grids with borders and vertical text strips. Lighting is either flat and colorless (overcast daylight, harsh direct on-camera flash at night crushing shadows to black) or monochromatic and directional (a single red or magenta wash, a green beam, candle or screen glow in a black room). No three-point beauty lighting, no golden hour.

### Typography

Elongated blackletter / "Gothic" letterforms and Unicode-substituted glyphs; text is often intentionally hard to read. Label-artifact type — small caps catalog lines, Japanese obi-strip verticals, triangle-flanked logotypes — is legitimate collage furniture.

## Mood & Values

Dread, sluggishness ("zombie rave"), ritual solemnity colliding with teenage ennui — smoking, loitering, staring at the camera without smiling. Surreal dream-logic and uncanny innocence sit beside overt occultism. The governing values are DIY ethos and internet-fueled mystery: deliberate unsearchability, anonymity, anti-commercial obscurity, and belonging signaled through glyph literacy and label affiliations rather than through polish or budget.

## Era & Origins

Late 2000s United States, pioneered by the electronic trio SALEM; the name was coined jokingly in December 2009 by Travis Egedy (Pictureplane) and stuck. The scene peaked 2009–2011 on SoundCloud, Myspace, Bandcamp and Tumblr, then bled into cloud rap (Clams Casino) and shares sample-processing roots with Vaporwave. It reacts against bright rave culture and glossy commercial photography, answering them with slowed tempo, black cloth and degraded signal. Hype faded by the mid-2010s; a cult underground persists.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Black first: let void black and ash hold most of the frame; color arrives as tint, backlight or stain.
2. Degrade the signal: apply at least one lo-fi process — grain, compression crush, scan lines, pixel sort, xerox contrast, low-res upscale.
3. Stamp one sigil: a triangle frame or overlay, a cross, a pentagram, an alchemical glyph, or obfuscated Unicode text.
4. Keep identity half-hidden: hoods, hair over the face, flash-blown features, turned backs, mirrored duplicates.
5. Shoot like evidence: snapshot candidness, found-footage distance, flat overcast or direct flash; or commit fully to ritual symmetry.
6. Collage like a label artifact when collaging: borders, obi strips, catalog type, mirrored fragments, triangle logotypes.
7. Dress the body in layered black streetwear-goth: hoodies, draped robes, track jackets, minimal geometric occult prints.

**Variation axes**

- *Subject*: lone portrait, twin/duo, night crowd, robed children, skull still life, cemetery/wood/playground landscape, church interior, bedroom shrine, album-cover collage, or a pure glyph on a noise field.
- *Composition*: centered sigil, bilateral mirror, off-center snapshot, collage grid, or minimal black field with one white mark.
- *Lighting*: overcast flat daylight, direct night flash, single-color gel wash (red/magenta/green), candle or screen glow, or full monochrome.
- *Color handling*: pure black-and-white, cold desaturated film, violet or teal duotone, black-plus-one-accent, or rare glitch-pastel intrusion treated as error.
- *Mood*: dread, boredom, solemn ritual, uncanny innocence, surreal dream.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules — darkness, degradation, sigils, anonymity — not any reference image. A still life of a rusted drum machine under flash grain with a triangle overlay is as valid as a cemetery portrait.

**Avoid**

- Full-frame saturated neon or candy gradients; neon may appear only as a glitch error, never as the palette.
- Clean glossy commercial finish, crisp high-resolution clarity, softbox beauty light, high-key daylight cheer.
- Cozy boho witchcraft props: bundled sage, sun-and-moon tapestries in honey light, crystals on sunny sills.
- Victorian mourning frills, lace, parasols, polished cathedral editorial styling.
- Aquatic CGI: dolphins, water chrome, aqua gradients, bubble renders.
- Vaporwave pastel grids, classical busts, consumer-retail irony (unless re-darkened into occult collage).
- Legible standard sans/serif headline typography as the main text.
- Literal gore or blood splatter; the style implies dread, it does not depict viscera.
- Smooth clean 3D renders with tidy reflections.

## Discrimination Cues

| Neighbor | Their tell | Witch House difference |
|---|---|---|
| Nu-Goth | Same black wardrobe, but shot clean: sharp editorial contrast, styled hair, smooth finish | Witch House adds grain, glyphs, athletic streetwear layers and snapshot awkwardness |
| Seapunk | Glossy aquatic cyan, 3D water, dolphins, Y2K CGI optimism | Witch House keeps early-internet roughness but inverts mood to occult dread and desaturation |
| Trillwave | The closest twin: identical △† Unicode syntax over hot pink/cyan neon, chrome type, rave glow | Witch House keeps the glyphs in black, ash, oxblood and violet murk; glowing pink triangles over sunset palms are Trillwave |
| Witchcore | Cottage/nature witchcraft: herbs, candles, forest, warm amber handmade comfort | Witch House is urban, digital, industrial, cold-lit; it unsettles rather than comforts |
| Comfy Synth | Cozy nostalgic synth warmth, pastel dusk, domestic softness | Witch House grain signals dread, not comfort, and carries sigils and all-black dress |

Quick check: black-dominated frame + degraded lo-fi signal + at least one occult geometric glyph + anonymous black-clad figure or ritual object = Witch House. Remove the glyph and add neon and it drifts to Trillwave; remove the degradation and it drifts to Nu-Goth; add warmth and herbs and it drifts to Witchcore.
