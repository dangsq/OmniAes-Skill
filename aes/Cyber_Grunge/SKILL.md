# Cyber Grunge

Cyber Grunge is an outfit-first aesthetic observed from roughly 2022 onward, blending Grunge, streetwear and Y2K Futurism with a slight military inflection. Its doctrine is the "subversive basic": a simple jersey top or tank interrupted by cut-outs, mesh panels or bolted-on pockets, worn inside a layered, androgynous, oversized-meets-distressed system of clothing. Bold silhouettes carry utilitarian features — cargo pockets, straps, buckles — while Y2K-era prints, accessories and proportions supply the nostalgia. The style documents itself the way street fashion always has: lookbook full-bodies, sidewalk snaps, mirror selfies, flat-lay kit grids, zine collages, and muted anime-style illustration in the lineage of *Serial Experiments Lain* and *Ghost in the Shell*.

## Visual Identity

### Palette

One rule governs color: everything is desaturated, dusted, sun-faded or concrete-stained. Build any image from these bands:

- **Washed black / charcoal:** #141416 – #26262A
- **Concrete and heather gray:** #6E7276, #8A8D8F, #B9BCBD
- **Olive, sage, moss:** #4A5240 – #788069
- **Khaki, taupe, deep brown:** #8B7D6B, #A79A87, #4E4237
- **Washed denim and slate blue:** #5A6B7C – #7C8BA1
- **Bone / off-white** for layered tees: #D8D5CE

Camo and star graphics appear as contained patches or prints, never as field color. Saturation stays low across the frame; a single muted family (gray-led, olive-led, khaki-led or black-led) usually dominates while the others support. Neon, candy pastel, saturated primaries and chrome are outside the palette entirely.

### Signature motifs (ranked by how centrally the style documents them)

1. **Subversive basics** — plain tops broken by chest cut-outs, sheer mesh sleeves, asymmetric hems, added pockets; the style's stated core.
2. **Utilitarian detail** — cargo pockets, straps, buckles, zippers, belt-on-belt at the hip.
3. **Layered androgyny** — cropped jacket, cardigan or vest over a tank; oversized over cropped; long maxi skirt over short top; gender-coding deliberately mixed.
4. **Distress** — faded washes, raw hems, worn denim, pilled knit.
5. **Y2K silhouettes and prints** — low-rise flared cargo or track pants, baby tees with photo or graphic prints, mini and maxi skirts, star graphics and sparkle clipart in collage work.
6. **Futuristic accessories** — oversized over-ear headphones, goggles pushed onto the head or hat, visor-ish eyewear; the tech-melancholy of Lain and the Matrix.
7. **Headwear** — bucket hat, knit beanie, baseball cap; occasionally a knit balaclava-style mask.
8. **Chunky footwear** — thick-soled platform boots and shoes in the New Rock / Demonia / Buffalo lineage; heavy soles anchor every silhouette.
9. **Soft counter-details** — pearl and handmade necklaces, stockings; a deliberate tenderness borrowed from Fairy Grunge.
10. **Military inflection (variation)** — camo patches, olive drab, field pockets; seasoning, never full costume.
11. **Urban decay context (variation)** — brick walls, utility boxes, parking stripes, concrete as backdrop.

### Materials and textures

Washed cotton jersey, distressed denim, mesh and sheer synthetics, nylon ripstop for bags and parachute bottoms, wool or acrylic knits, canvas, matte leather on boots, dull metal hardware, pearl bead. Surfaces read faded, pilled, raw-edged and dust-matte — never glossy. In photographic registers expect digicam grain or zine-scan softness; in illustration registers, flat cel shading with muted fills and paper grain.

### Recurring subjects

The dressed figure is the subject: a solo lookbook model, a street-snapped pedestrian, a mirror-selfie diarist, a loosely coordinated crew, a flat-lay outfit arranged like kit, a collage of cut-out figures among star sparkles, or an anime-style character portrait. Androgynous presentation is common. Poses are deadpan — arms crossed, hands in pockets, weight on one hip, gaze flat or averted.

### Composition and lighting

Vertical head-to-boot framing is the default; torso and waist crops that foreground the low-rise waistband, belt and hip chain are equally native. Flat lays, collage grids with white margins, and character-portrait illustration complete the range. Backgrounds run from seamless studio white to sidewalk, brick alley, fitting room, or cluttered bedroom. Light is flat and diffuse — even studio light, overcast daylight, or harsh on-camera flash indoors — with low contrast, desaturated grade, neutral-to-cool temperature. No golden hour, no glossy strobe glamour. (The wiki anchors "urban photography" as related media; the specific lighting habits above are distilled from the style's photographic practice rather than dictated by text.)

## Mood & Values

Urban edge, rebellious style, utilitarianism, Y2K nostalgia, societal critique, disillusionment. The feeling is armored casualness: clothing as soft gear for a city whose future never arrived, deadpan alienation stared through a screen. Nostalgia is handled ironically — a childhood's chrome future remembered in faded olive and heather gray. The style values function and refusal alike: pockets that work, layers that shed, a smile withheld.

## Era & Origins

A 2020s online fashion culture (observed from ~2022), synthesizing 90s grunge's earthy palette, streetwear utility, the Y2K revival, and slight military borrowing. Its spiritual media anchors are 1998–2005: *Serial Experiments Lain*, *Ghost in the Shell*, *The Matrix*, 90s/2000s alternative music, and the street-snap tradition of *Fruits* magazine carried into zines and blogs. It reacts against both polished minimal Y2K revivalism and costume-grade cosplay futurism by dragging the future back to street level, distressed and wearable.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

- Treat the image as outfit documentation: figure and clothing carry the style; setting only supports.
- Layer in tiers: subversive-basic base (cut-out, mesh, sheer) → utilitarian bottom (cargo, track pant, strapped maxi skirt) → outer shell (cropped jacket, cardigan, vest) → headwear → chunky sole.
- Play proportion: cropped over oversized, long over short, flare from the knee, low-rise waist visible.
- Include two or three utilitarian or tech details (pockets, straps, buckles, hip chains, headphones, goggles) plus one soft counter-detail (pearl strand, handmade necklace, stocking).
- Keep saturation low; let one muted family lead.
- Prints stay contained and faded: stars, photo graphics, camo patches, distressed graphics.
- Pose deadpan; groups stand as loose crews, not choreographed lines.

**Variation axes.** Subject: solo, duo, crew, flat lay, collage, illustrated character; any gender presentation, indoors or out. Setting: seamless studio, sidewalk, brick alley, fitting room, bedroom, crosswalk — or no setting at all. Composition: head-to-toe vertical, waist crop, mirror-selfie rectangle, collage grid, character portrait. Lighting: flat studio, overcast, direct flash, dim room lamp. Color handling: gray-led, olive-led, khaki-led, black-led, denim-blue accent. Mood: aloof cool ↔ melancholic dissociation ↔ ironic collage play. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules — palette band, layering logic, utilitarian-plus-Y2K detail, deadpan mood — not reproduce any reference image's subject, framing or composition.

**Avoid**

- Neon, candy pastel, saturated primaries, holographic chrome, glossy latex or vinyl shine.
- Pristine, hardware-free, preppy or clean-minimal clothing with no distress or wash.
- Head-to-toe tactical gear, gas masks, harness rigs, scorched-rubble narratives.
- Empty ruined interiors with no dressed subject.
- Woodland romance: florals, mushrooms, cottage textures, wings.
- Glossy optimistic Y2K futurism: chrome logos, iridescent puffers, candy visors.
- Golden-hour warmth, high-gloss editorial strobe, saturated sunset grades.
- Smiling commercial catalog posing or dynamic action-sport poses.
- Maximalist clashing prints, rave fluff, kawaii decals beyond faded star/sparkle clipart in collage register.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Acubi** shares Y2K silhouettes and baby tees but stays clean, slim and soft-neutral, without distress, straps or chunky soles; Cyber Grunge adds grunge dirt, wash and hardware.
- **Avant Apocalypse** shares straps and dystopia but is costume-grade survivalism — masks, harnesses, ruined settings, catastrophic narrative; Cyber Grunge remains wearable streetwear in an intact city, nostalgic rather than apocalyptic.
- **Cyber Swag** shares Y2K futurism but is glossy and optimistic — chrome, iridescence, brights, logo shine; Cyber Grunge is matte, faded, earthbound, disillusioned.
- **Fairy Grunge** shares pearls, handmade necklaces and distressed layering but turns toward woodland — florals, moss, romantic softness; Cyber Grunge turns toward the city — tech accessories, cargo utility, military seasoning.
- **Industrial Decay** shares urban decay backdrops but makes decay the subject (empty rusted spaces); Cyber Grunge keeps the dressed figure as subject, with decay only as context.
- Against parent **Grunge**: if an outfit lacks Y2K tech accessories, mesh subversion or low-rise flare, it reads as plain 90s grunge, not Cyber Grunge.

**Quick recognition test:** muted earth-and-concrete palette + subversive basic or utilitarian layering + chunky platform sole + one Y2K-futuristic accessory + deadpan stance; three or more of these, in any medium from digicam photo to cel-shaded illustration, place an image firmly in Cyber Grunge.
