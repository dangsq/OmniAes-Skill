# Dead Inside

Dead Inside (дед инсайд, also "zxc ghoul") is an internet-born subculture of Russian-, Ukrainian- and Belarus-speaking teenagers who self-describe as "ghouls" after *Tokyo Ghoul*, grind through Dota 2 matches, and wear their depression, misanthropy and self-importance as a uniform. It began as a gamer's throwaway line of indifference and hardened into a full visual identity: avatars of Kaneki-style ghouls, all-black wardrobes, dyed hair falling over the eyes, and a monochrome-plus-blood-red image language borrowed from manga panels and pushed through inversion, glitch and grain. By 2022 the style circulates as much as meme as confession, so a deadpan ironic register sits just under every genuinely bleak image.

## Visual Identity

### Palette
The palette rule is two values plus one wound: an absolute monochrome of black and paper white, with red as the only permitted accent. Blacks run `#000000–#111111`; whites run `#eeeeee–#ffffff`; mid-greys (`#555555–#999999`) appear only as desaturated photographic tones, never as a designed color. The red family spans dried blood `#6e0000–#8b0000`, signal crimson `#a31621–#c1121f`, and luminous eye-red `#e60000–#ff2020`. Red may be used sparingly (a pair of irises, a scatter of specks, a slash across a panel) or may flood the frame as a red-black duotone; what never appears is a third hue. Any image where blue, purple, pastel, gold or neon registers has left the style.

### Signature motifs, ranked by textual documentation
1. **The ghoul avatar.** The text documents *Tokyo Ghoul* — Kaneki Ken above all — as the subculture's primary self-image. Visually this means a manga-drawn youth with white or black hair, a long asymmetric bang, and eyes that carry the whole color budget: black sclera with a glowing red iris, or a fully inverted face (black skin, white hair, red gaze).
2. **The uniform.** Black clothes, explicitly: oversized black hoodies and coats, Drain-style graphic prints on black cloth, plus — as the photographic evidence extends the text — checkered/plaid trousers, fingerless gloves, chains and rings.
3. **The hair.** Dyed black, white or red, with a bang that covers half the face; split-dye half-red/half-black heads occur. The obscured face is both fashion rule and compositional device.
4. **Gaming-nihilism markers.** Dota 2 affiliation and the slang ledger — "1000-7", "zxc", "dead inside" as tattoo-style or handwritten lettering, sometimes in Cyrillic — surface as scribbled text, prints and avatar handles.
5. **The depression/misanthropy posture.** Hunched shoulders, head tilted or bowed, arms or palms blocking the face, groups huddled with heads buried in each other's shoulders. The single permitted "open" expression is the unhinged grin: wide, teeth showing, eyes voided or glowing.
6. **The ironic meme layer.** The text notes elderly people parodying the persona; this is a documented but rare, jokey variant, not the core look.

### Materials, textures, medium
Texture does the emotional work. Manga screentone and halftone dot fields, pencil crosshatch and scratchy hatching, ink blots and splatter, speckle noise like dust or dead stars on a black field, glitch slices and cut-panel collage, and lo-fi phone grain on photographs. Mediums range across digital manga illustration, fragmented panel collages, abstract ink-and-blot compositions, and candid or mirror-selfie phone photography. Surfaces should never look smooth, airbrushed or vector-clean.

### Composition and lighting
Composition favors the intimate and the obstructed: extreme close-ups on eyes or a grinning mouth, a hand reaching into the lens, heads canted off-axis, lone full-body figures dwarfed by an empty expanse (snow field, fluorescent mall corridor), and group lines where every face is buried. A defining move is negative-space inversion — the whole image flipped as if a film negative, white figure and white hair burning against a black void. Lighting is anti-naturalistic by default: in illustration there is no light source at all, only value contrast and the self-lit red of eyes and slashes; in photography the light is flat and joyless — overhead fluorescents or overcast daylight, desaturated, with no golden hour and no studio glow. (Photographic lighting is documented by only a handful of candid shots in the evidence set, so treat "flat, cold, ambient" as the rule rather than a detailed schema.)

## Mood & Values
Dead Inside performs emotional numbness as identity: apathy toward the game, toward school, toward everything, paired with a fierce self-importance about that very apathy. Misanthropy and loneliness are worn proudly; aggression (toxic chat, aggressive phonk) and indifference are two faces of the same coin. Loyalty runs to the in-group — the zxc crew, the Dota stack, the anime canon — while irony buffers sincerity, so images can be simultaneously devastating and self-aware. The mood to hit is not gentle sadness but bleached-out dread with a smirk under the bangs.

## Era & Origins
Emergent in the 2010s across Ukraine, Russia and Belarus. The phrase crystallized in 2014 when Ukrainian Dota 2 professional Ilya Korobkin (ALOHADANCE) declared himself "dead inside" and indifferent to the game, then tattooed the line; his fanbase turned it into a creed of nihilistic play. A 2021 resurgence broadened it into general pessimism about a declining world, and by 2022 it circulated chiefly as meme. The style reacts to post-Soviet teenage ennui and competitive-gaming burnout, filtered through the *Tokyo Ghoul* edgelord wave and dark phonk/hyperpop playlists; it inherits from emo, goth and Drain but strips their color and ornament down to monochrome plus blood.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Hold the palette to black, white, desaturated grey and red — nothing else enters the frame.
2. Push value contrast to the cliff edge; full negative inversion is always available as a compositional mode.
3. Obscure the face (bang, palm, forearm, hair curtain, mask) or make the eyes the only light source in the image.
4. Texture everything: screentone dots, crosshatch, ink blot, speckle, glitch slice, phone grain.
5. Dress live subjects in the uniform (black oversized hoodie, plaid trousers, dyed bangs) or draw the ghoul (white hair, voided or red-glowing eyes).
6. Keep photographic light flat, cold and ambient; keep illustrated light graphic — glow emanates from red elements, not from skies or lamps.
7. Use red as injury, alarm or glow: eyes, slashes, splatter, duotone flood. Never as balanced decorative color.
8. Lettering is optional and handwritten or tattoo-like: "dead inside", "zxc", "1000-7", Cyrillic permitted.

**Variation axes.** Subject matter may be a manga ghoul portrait, a cut-panel collage, an abstract ink/blot field, a horned game-like silhouette with burning eyes, a mirror selfie, a group huddle, a lone figure in snow or under fluorescent ceilings, or the ironic elderly parody. Composition may run from eye-filling close-up to distant full body, from a single figure in clean negative space to a dense fragmented collage. Color handling may be pure monochrome, monochrome with sparse red accents, or a red-dominant duotone. Medium may be screentoned manga, scratchy pencil, glitch collage or lo-fi phone photo. Mood may slide between numb apathy, quiet dread, aggressive sneer, manic grin and deadpan irony. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

**Avoid.**
- Any hue outside black/white/grey/red: pastels, neon, gold, teal-orange grading, purple or blue tints.
- Warm sunlight, golden hour, sunset gradients, romantic bokeh, studio glamour light.
- A fully visible, relaxed, cheerful face with ordinary bright eyes (the manic grin and the red glow are the exceptions).
- Clean vector minimalism, glossy 3D renders, airbrushed smooth skin, untextured flat design.
- Colorful streetwear, visible commercial logos, chrome/Y2K futurism, cottagecore florals, goth lace and velvet, scene-emo rainbow hair.
- Explicit gore or viscera; menace in this style is implied through eyes, slashes, inversion and contrast, not depicted anatomically.
- Center-parted curtain hair and layered colorful grunge (that is E-Boy territory).

| Animecore | Anime imagery | Animecore uses saturated, nostalgic, bright-sky or pastel-tinted screenshots; Dead Inside desaturates, inverts and screentones its anime into grim monochrome |
| E-Boy | Dyed hair, chains, online teen fashion | E-Boy wears center-parted curtain hair, layered colorful streetwear and polished TikTok grading; Dead Inside wears shaggy face-covering bangs, an all-black wardrobe and lo-fi grain |

Against its older ancestors (emo, goth, Drain), the decisive cue is subtraction: Dead Inside keeps the despair but deletes the color, the ornament and the romance, leaving black cloth, halftone dots, a red gaze and a shrug.
