# Trillwave

Trillwave — also called the Trill Era or Swag Era — is the visual language of the underground internet hip-hop that emerged in the late 2000s and peaked between roughly 2010 and 2014. It was born on SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Tumblr and YouTube, where cover art and free-floating "trill edits" (digital collages unattached to any release) carried the atmosphere of the music before a single bar played. Sonically and visually it welds 1990s Southern hip-hop — Memphis horrorcore tape covers and Houston chopped-and-screwed psychedelia — onto early internet culture: anime, Nintendo 64 and PlayStation renders, Web 2.0 graphics, Old Web typography. It is deliberately syncretic, absorbing Vaporwave statues, Seapunk water, Cleancore whiteness and Y2K futurism but always filtering them through a lo-fi, hip-hop lens. It is the transitional aesthetic between 90s Southern rap and SoundCloud Rap, and the feedstock for Cloud Rap, Phonk and Seapunk.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette

The rule, not the exception: a neutral xerox base (black, white, grey) invaded by **one** saturated override family, applied as a global grade, duotone or tinted void. Naturalistic color never survives untouched — it is screwed, tinted, dithered or voided.

| Role | Range |
|---|---|
| Xerox base | blacks `#0a0a0a`–`#1e1e1e`, paper whites `#f5f5f5`–`#ffffff`, cold greys |
| Screwed override (core) | violets and magentas `#3b0057`, `#6a0dad`, `#a64dff`, `#ff4fd8`, lean pink `#ff9ecf` |
| Holy / aquatic override | pool cyan `#00b7d4`, `#00e5ff`, teal `#40e0d0`, fog pale `#d7ecea` |
| Kawaii / otaku lane | pastel pink `#ffd1dc`, baby blue `#bfe9ff`, mint `#cdfbe3` |
| Metallic type | gold `#b8860b`–`#ffd700`, chrome silver gradients `#8f8f8f`–`#ffffff` |
| Signal green (sparse) | digital-rain / night-vision `#00ff41` |

Black, purple, gold and white are the scene's declared key colours; cyan-aqua and pastel pink are documented lane variants (Holy/Seapunk and Otaku/Kawaii respectively).

### Signature motifs, ranked by how centrally the scene documents them

1. **Pen & Pixel / Memphis tape grammar** — the foundational layer: high-contrast black-and-white imagery, xeroxed contrast, dithering, heavy grain, ornate borders, chrome or gold bevel lettering, tracklist blocks, parental-advisory stickers.
2. **Mirrored symmetry** — horizontal or vertical mirroring of photos and renders into psychedelic, Rorschach-like kaleidoscopes.
3. **Chopped-and-screwed grading and lean iconography** — heavy purple/pink color grading over anything; syrup cups, Sprite bottles, smoke, marijuana.
4. **The "Holy" classical lane** — Greco-Roman statues (broken, crying, glowing), marble columns, temples, monumental pools; fog and mist over stone.
5. **White Space isolation** — subjects cut out as hard-edged transparent-PNG shapes (statues, luxury items, dolphins, bottles) floating on a stark white void; Cleancore-adjacent.
6. **Jungle and nature in digital voids** — high-resolution rainforest, ferns, dense woodland, or pools sunk into darkness; trees digitally warped to look unnatural; organic matter against synthetic emptiness.
7. **Otaku / kawaii artifacts** — 90s–2000s anime screenshots, low-poly N64/PS1-era 3D renders, pixel-font hashtags, pastel palettes.
8. **Street and luxury signifiers** — firearms, fanned cash, chains, sneakers and apparel (Nike, Gucci, Adidas), Backwoods, lowriders and boxy sedans.
9. **Corporate coldness** — glass skyscrapers, architectural blueprints, office plazas, overcast skies; sterile interiors with glowing cove lighting.
10. **Terrorwave edge (rarer)** — balaclavas, camouflage, tactical gear, night-vision green.

### Typography as a foreground object

Type is never a caption; it is collage material. Expect gothic blackletter and ornate serif display faces, chrome/gold gradient bevels, pixel fonts, and heavy Old Web Unicode abuse: fullwidth letters (ｅｙｅｂｒｏｗ ｂｏｙｓ), dingbats and glyphs (✦, ༄, ♒, ◑‿◐), kaomoji, katakana and kanji blocks, dollar signs for S (DRUG$), `.exe` suffixes, １-８００ phone numbers, words like *trill, based, cloud, drip, squad, holy*.

### Materials, textures, subjects, composition, lighting

Textures are digital-degradation textures: dither patterns, xerox scan grain, JPEG compression artifacts, pixelation, low-poly geometry with vertex-lit textures, chrome and gold gradient fills, marble, still pool water, fog, hard cutout edges. Subjects range across statues, pools, columns, jungle foliage, dolphins and water, low-poly game characters, anime stills, hooded or masked figures, cash, guns, bottles, sneakers, cars, skyscrapers, clouds. Composition tends to bilateral mirror symmetry, centered isolation on a void, stacked collage with ornate borders and corner repeats, or type slammed over a photograph; the square 1:1 cover format dominates, with occasional landscape "screenshot" frames and checkerboard grid floors. Lighting is flat scan/flash harshness for black-and-white portraits, foggy diffuse light for holy and architecture lanes, unnatural saturated glow for pools and neon interiors, overcast grey for corporate, night-vision green for terrorwave.

## Mood & Values

"Trill" means true + real: underground authenticity performed through deliberately amateur, DIY means — degradation is credibility. Over that sits a melancholy euphoria: screwed psychedelia, sadboi vulnerability, "holy" mysticism and quasi-occult dread (Xpiritualism) coexisting with meme irony and kawaii playfulness. Nostalgia targets childhood technology and the early web, not analog decades. Luxury aspiration (Gucci, chains, pools) is processed through xerox poverty, producing aspirational imagery that looks photocopied. A constant undercurrent is urban isolation and "digital coldness" — empty plazas, fog, voids.

## Era & Origins

Roots: 1990s Memphis horrorcore (Three 6 Mafia, DJ Squeeky) and DJ Screw's Houston chopped-and-screwed technique; proto-trillwave production circa 2008. Codified around 2011 by SpaceGhostPurrp and Raider Klan, who coined "Phonk"; mainstreamed the same year by A$AP Rocky's "Purple Swag" and "Peso"; evolved in 2013 by Yung Lean and Sad Boys merging it with Web 2.0 aesthetics. Peak 2010–2014, with a Vaporwave feedback loop 2013–2016 and White Space work persisting to 2016. Later subsumed by Cloud Rap and Phonk; legacy offspring include SadTrap and Trap Metal.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.** (1) Build the image as an early-2010s internet artifact, not a photograph: something scanned, mirrored, tinted, compressed. (2) Choose one lane — Memphis xerox, holy statue, white space, jungle void, kawaii render, corporate cold, terrorwave — but keep the shared grammar: degradation, override color, foreground type. (3) Apply the palette rule: neutral base plus at most one saturated override family, metal reserved for lettering. (4) Let type behave as collage: blackletter, chrome bevel, pixel font, fullwidth Unicode, katakana, dingbats. (5) Keep hip-hop signifiers present somewhere — cash, lean, firearms, brands, screwed tint — even in the prettiest holy or jungle lane. (6) Prefer square cover format and symmetrical or centered composition.

**Variation axes.** Subject: any of statues, pools, jungle, dolphins, low-poly renders, anime stills, hooded figures, skyscrapers, cars, cash, bottles, balaclavas. Composition: mirror kaleidoscope, white-void isolation, bordered collage stack, type-over-photo, landscape screenshot. Lighting: flash-flat, foggy diffuse, neon glow, overcast, night-vision. Color: purple/magenta screw, cyan/aqua holy, pastel kawaii, pure greyscale xerox. Mood: dread, holiness, melancholy, irony, coldness. **A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.**

**Avoid.** Clean contemporary design (smooth vector gradients, kerned geometric sans, 4K product polish, flat icons/emoji); naturalistic warm photography with no grade or degradation; handcrafted analog materials (paper craft, ink, art-film grain — Trillwave grain is dither, xerox and JPEG); Drift Phonk neon night-city car culture; Frutiger Aero glossy optimism (bubbles, lens-flare auroras, sincere eco-utopia); mainstream hip-hop billboard luxury and minimal modern trap type; Nerdcore cartoon mascots and sincere fandom illustration; pure Pen & Pixel replication (airbrushed portrait plus chrome bevel with no internet-age layer); Vaporwave emptiness with no hip-hop content; coherent photoreal scenes with no digital intervention.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Vs. Drift Phonk:** Drift Phonk (post-2016, TikTok) is neon-lit night streets, JDM drift cars, motion blur, aggressive red-black manga energy; Trillwave is slower, xeroxed, purple-screwed, statue-and-jungle haunted, with 2011-era web type.
- **Vs. Frutiger Aero:** Aero is glossy, skeuomorphic, hopeful corporate eco-futurism with bubbles and clean blues; Trillwave may borrow water, dolphins and early 3D renders but degrades, tints and ironizes them, adding rap iconography and grain.
- **Vs. Hip-Hop (mainstream):** mainstream rap visuals are crisp, expensive, cleanly lit and minimally typeset; Trillwave is amateur collage, degraded fidelity, Unicode clutter, melancholy or occult undercurrent.
- **Vs. Nerdcore:** Nerdcore covers illustrate nerd life sincerely (cartoons, games as narrative); Trillwave uses anime and game artifacts as sampled texture inside trap posturing, keeping street signifiers.
- **Vs. Pen & Pixel:** Pen & Pixel is the 90s source: airbrushed studio portraits, chrome bevels, ornate borders, glossy finish. Trillwave quotes it but adds the internet layer — dithering, mirroring, purple grading, white space, anime/low-poly renders, Old Web typography. Presence of that web-native layer is the deciding test.
