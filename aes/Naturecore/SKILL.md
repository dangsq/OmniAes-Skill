# Naturecore

Naturecore is a parent aesthetic: an umbrella term under which Cottagecore, Goblincore, Mori Kei, Adventurecore, Mushroomcore, Prairie and Dark Naturalism each sit as specialized children. What the parent keeps is the common core — an idyllic, often romanticized depiction of the natural world valued for serenity, solitude and peace, consumed as escapism from modern urban life. Because it is an umbrella, its visual language is broad but not unlimited: everything must read as *the outdoors appreciated, not the outdoors used*. The wild is the subject; the viewer is a guest.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette

Green owns the frame. Browns anchor it. Whites punctuate it. Blues and pastels are guests, confined to sky and water. Saturation is allowed to swing from vivid botanical (wet moss, fern fronds) to fog-muted near-grey, but synthetic brightness never enters.

Rule of thumb: if you mentally subtract the greens and the image still holds its identity, it is not Naturecore. White appears as *points* (blossoms, a moth, a distant house, a cloud), never as a field. Black is only shadow-depth inside foliage, never a graphic element.

### Signature motifs, ranked by textual prominence

1. **Forests** — the defining motif. Moss-laden trunks and fallen logs, fern understory, misty woods, dense conifer stands, swamp forest with draped moss and duckweed-skin water.
2. **Mountains and tree-covered ridges** — valleys, scree slopes, snow-dusted peaks under cumulus, fog-layered conifer hillsides.
3. **Water bodies** — lakes, rivers, tannin-stained streams, ponds, cypress swamp; water as calm surface or gentle flow, never as surf or rapids spectacle.
4. **Plants and flowers** — ferns, moss, lichen, wildflower meadows (daisies, lupines, dandelion clocks), flowering branches framing a view.
5. **Wild animals** — deer, moths, birds, small fauna; shown undisturbed, tender or translucent, never hunted or posed.
6. **Natural landscapes as vast fields** — prairie horizon, meadow under big sky.
7. **The darker untamed edge** — decay as quiet fact: mossed fallen logs, mushrooms on litter, cut seed cross-sections, the cycle of life and death handled contemplatively (the seam with Dark Naturalism).
8. **Minimal human trace** — a white cottage small across a lake, a lone figure from behind before a great tree, hands in stream water or reaching toward canopy. Humans are scale-givers and touch-proxies, never protagonists.

### Materials and textures

Moss velvet, fern fractals, lichen crust on bark, furrowed trunk skin, matte duckweed film, silk-and-glass stream water, paper-thin petals and moth wings, damp stone, needle litter, wool and linen weave when clothing appears. The aesthetic is tactile: many images invite touch as much as sight (hands submerged, palms upturned). Text describes fashion in natural fibers — cotton, linen, wool, oversized jumpers, collared shirts, handmade wood/stone/pressed-flower accessories — though the visual corpus is landscape-dominant; treat garment imagery as a minor, text-sanctioned branch obeying the same earthy palette.

### Composition and lighting

Recurring structural tendencies, none mandatory: layered depth planes (foreground foliage or branch framing, midground water or trunks, background ridge or fog); immersion framing with no visible horizon inside forest; high or absent horizon in woodland, low horizon under big sky in meadow shots; a portrait/vertical bias inherited from phone-video culture, balanced by horizontal vista options; macro as an equal citizen beside panorama — a moth on lichen carries the same weight as a valley. Light is always *weather*, never lamp: diffuse overcast and canopy shade as the default, mist as atmospheric softener and depth-cue, dappled sunbeams and godrays through trunks, backlight making leaves, wings and seedheads glow translucent, flat documentary daylight on vast terrain, soft peach-lilac dusk at the calm extreme. Shadows stay open and soft; highlights bloom gently; film grain or clean phone-photo truthfulness both fit, glossy CGI render does not.

## Mood & Values

Tranquility, solitude, peace, reverence. Escapism for the urban scroll-weary; environmentalism and sustainability as lived values rather than slogans; a yearning for connection to the earth sharpened by pandemic-era confinement. Gentle melancholy is welcome — fog, dusk, rust, decay — because the cycle of life and death belongs to the romance. Solitude, not loneliness; awe, not adrenaline. The mood test: standing inside the image should feel like exhaling.

## Era & Origins

Emergent in the early 2020s, surging on TikTok as serene landscape videos marketed as a "break from scrolling"; the COVID-19 pandemic amplified it, binding nature-comfort to sustainability consciousness. It reacts against urban modernity and screen fatigue — with the noted irony that it is consumed on screens. Its deeper lineage is Romantic landscape painting and the natural-history documentary (Planet Earth as the spirit-capturing touchstone), with folk music as its tonal neighbor. As an umbrella term it deliberately holds contradictory children together, which is why its own center is mood and palette rather than a fixed object list.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Budget the frame green-first; browns ground it, whites punctuate, blues/pastels stay in sky and water.
2. Light as weather: choose mist, overcast, canopy filter, low sun or dusk — never studio or flash logic.
3. Let one texture carry the image: moss cap, lichen map, duckweed skin, fern lattice, petal field.
4. Pick a rung on the scale ladder — macro, arm's-reach immersion, middle-distance woodland, panoramic vista — and commit to it.
5. If a human appears, make them a guest: back turned, hands only, or a speck-scale dwelling nestled in growth.
6. Serenity is the default register; mystery and quiet decay are seasoning, dread is off-menu.
7. Keep photographic truth: natural lens behavior, soft bloom, optional film grain or phone-snapshot candor.

**Variation axes (the acceptable space)**

- *Subject*: any wild natural subject — alpine valley, swamp, conifer stand, prairie, meadow, stream, lake, macro fauna or flora, fungi, seed pods, seasons from spring bloom to autumn larch rust to fog-muted winter; text-sanctioned natural-fiber clothing details.
- *Composition*: vertical or horizontal; horizonless immersion or horizon-led vista; framed-through-foliage or open; macro to panorama; human absent, partial, or tiny.
- *Lighting*: mist, flat overcast, dappled beams, backlight translucence, documentary noon, pastel dusk.
- *Color handling*: vivid wet-moss saturation ↔ fog-desaturated grey-green; dusk may add peach/lilac veils.
- *Mood*: serene ↔ contemplatively melancholic ↔ quietly awed ↔ softly mysterious.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image. A sun-baked grassland, a frozen creek, or a single moth wing can be perfect Naturecore with none of the reference scenes repeated, so long as palette, light logic, texture-forwardness and reverent mood hold.

**Avoid**

- Neon, candy or synthetic color; saturated HDR skies; black graphic contrast.
- Urban infrastructure in foreground: roads, signage, power lines, vehicles, fences as subject.
- Plastic, glass, metal gear, electronics, screens of any kind.
- Action-adventure narrative: climbing, fording, summit poses, backpacks, ropes, boots-as-props.
- Staged domesticity: picnic spreads, baking, laundry lines, garden borders, vintage dresses as focus.
- Horror register: bones as focal point, carrion, predation mid-kill, menacing fog, near-monochrome dread.
- Harsh flash, studio strobes, oversharpening, glossy 3D-render sheen.
- Text overlays, UI elements, watermarks; cartoonified or anthropomorphic animals.

## Discrimination Cues

**Adventurecore** shares terrain but adds exertion and equipment: packs, boots, tracks, triumph poses, nature as obstacle or course. Naturecore removes the gear and stills the body; the landscape is beheld, not conquered.

**Cabincore** lives indoors: woodstove glow, enamel mugs, knit blankets, window light seen from inside. Naturecore stays outside; its dwelling, if any, is a white speck across water or beneath trees, never a room.

**Cottagecore** is cultivated pastoral domesticity — fenced gardens, chores, baking, floral dresses, tamed animals, vintage kitsch. Naturecore's flowers are wild meadow, its labor absent; it romanticizes untouched growth, not homestead routine.

**Cozy Gamer** is an interior tech nest of monitors, RGB and headphones. Naturecore contains no screens or devices whatsoever; the two never overlap in a single frame.

**Dark Naturalism** walks the same woods but leads with dread: bone, decay-as-horror, black fog, desaturation toward menace. Naturecore admits decay only as quiet cycle — a mossed log, a bolete on litter — with life-giving light and peace-first mood. If the image frightens instead of settles, it has crossed the border.

Against its other children (Fairycore's magic glitter, Goblincore's muddy hoarded curios, Mushroomcore's fungal obsession, Prairie's grassland minimalism), Naturecore is the balanced parent: no magic props, no clutter fetish, no single-species focus — just the whole wild world, green-first, breathing slowly.
