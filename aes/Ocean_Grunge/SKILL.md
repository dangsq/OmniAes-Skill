# Ocean Grunge

## Overview & Cultural Context

Ocean Grunge is a music-centric aesthetic that began in 2014 as the darker version of Seapunk. It inherits Seapunk's and Vaporwave's motif pool — ocean water, classical statuary, early-3D digital gloss — but strips away their candy color in exchange for a restricted palette of blacks, greys and muted blues. Musically it descends from early Vaporwave, drone, Nu-Metal and Grunge, with a family resemblance to Witch House; where Witch House is spooky and ethereal, Ocean Grunge is hollow and depressing. The visual medium carries the same weight: colors meant to evoke being stranded in open ocean with no land in sight, hope of rescue draining away, before ultimately plunging into the depths. A secondary strand abandons Seapunk's psychedelia entirely for the industrial sea: oil rigs, deep-sea drilling, commercial maritime ventures, submarines. Scene roster names themselves are often corrupted glyph strings (section signs, filled blocks, triangles), which sets the tone for the style's typography.

## Visual Identity

### Colour Palette

The palette rule is: desaturated depth first, saturation only as wound or glow. Most of any image sits in the first four bands below; yellow and purple act as tints and point-lights; magenta and mint appear solely as intrusions — a bioluminescent flare in blackness, a corrupted smear across a degraded frame — never as overall coloring.

| Band | Role | Hex range |
|---|---|---|
| Abyssal black / blue-black | water mass, night, void | #04070A–#101418 |
| Charcoal & slate grey | overcast sky, basalt, fog, murk | #2B3438–#556065 |
| Muted sea blue / teal | water midtones, never turquoise-bright | #16323B–#3C6B72 |
| Foam & bone white | breaking surf, highlight, statue plaster | #C7D2D4–#E8EDEA |
| Sickly lure yellow | anglerfish lure, sodium lamp, fire on water | #C9B458–#E3D27E |
| Aubergine / violet plum | shadow tint, dusk water, deep-space backdrops | #241A2E–#43284A |
| Interference magenta | glitch bands, corrupted vertical smears | #B22A63–#E14E86 |
| Bioluminescent mint | creature glow, submerged screen glow | #3FD9AE–#8CF2D4 |

Monochrome and duotone treatments are fully acceptable; a single deep saturated blue or teal may stand in for "the water itself," but pastels and candy tones are outside the style.

### Signature Motifs (ranked as the text documents them)

1. **The ocean itself** — open water, swelling waves, foam, weather; often no land in sight.
2. **Deep-sea fauna** — anglerfish, eels, jellyfish, squid, coelacanths; creatures of the lightless zone.
3. **Bioluminescence** — the style's characteristic light source: a cold point-glow in darkness.
4. **Statues of Poseidon** and classical sculpture, drowned, duotoned, or mirrored into symmetry.
5. **Sunken ships** and drowned objects settling into murk.
6. **Deep-sea mermaids** — pale, luminous, unwelcoming.
7. **The industrial sea** — oil rigs, deep-sea drilling, commercial maritime vessels, submarines; the strand that drops Seapunk imagery entirely.
8. **Treatment-layer motifs** from the scene's cover art: datamosh smears, compression blocks, scanline offsets, inset rectangular frames, mirrored/kaleidoscopic symmetry, low-poly 3D geometry, corrupted glyph typography. (The wiki prose specifies palette, motifs and mood; this degradation layer is documented by the scene's cover-art practice and corrupted artist names rather than by prose — treat it as the style's characteristic signal-processing, not an optional extra.)

### Materials & Textures

Churning foam and particulate murk; wet basalt and rock shelves; corroded metal, barnacle crust, oil sheen on water; matte stone and plaster on statuary; wet drapery; and on the digital side, VHS/datamosh artifacts, blocky compression noise, vertical color smears, flat low-poly facets, xerox grain over photography.

### Composition & Lighting

Emptiness is compositional: vast negative space of water, fog or black; subjects small, sinking, tilted, or absent altogether. Recurring structures include horizonless open water or a high, heavy horizon; frames-within-frames (porthole or grid windows, inset rectangles floating over the scene); mirrored bilateral symmetry; tilted planes and objects half-submerged; god-rays descending through particulate water. Lighting is low-key and cold: flat overcast scatter on the surface, silhouette backlight, shafts through murk, or a single point source in darkness — an anglerfish lure, a submerged screen, a flare burning on water at dusk. Direct sun, golden hour and high-key clarity do not occur.

## Mood & Values

Nihilism, emptiness, depression. The style's stated emotional core is the hollow feeling of stranding: no land, no rescue, slow descent. It is dreary rather than scary — ambient dread, not jump-scare horror — with a funerary undertone ("what awaits you in your oceanic grave"). Values: indifference of deep water, decay of signal and memory, melancholy drone-like stasis.

## Era & Origins

2010s, internet-native, from 2014. It reacts against Seapunk's bright psychedelia and Vaporwave's pastel nostalgia by draining them; it absorbs Grunge and Nu-Metal's nihilism and drone's stasis. Touchstones across media include deep-sea documentary imagery, the game *Narcosis*, *Disaster Log C*, *Splatoon 2: Octo Expansion*, League of Legends' Forgotten Depths material, and the novel *Fractured Tide*.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Drain before you add: build the image in black, grey and muted blue; treat saturation as an intrusion (one glow, one smear), never a base.
2. One light in the dark: a single lure, screen, flare or shaft against overwhelming darkness or overcast flatness.
3. Empty the frame: water, fog or void dominates; the subject sinks, tilts, or is missing.
4. Degrade the signal: datamosh smears, compression blocks, mirrored halves, inset frames, corrupted glyphs — the image should look like a transmission from depth.
5. Obey cold water physics: foam, murk, refraction, weight; water presses down on everything.
6. Mythic or industrial, never cozy: Poseidon and anglerfish share the style with oil rigs and submarines; harbors stay closed.
7. Mood check: hollow, not spooky; depressive, not dramatic.

**Variation axes.** Subject may range across open sea states, underwater fauna, classical statuary, wrecks and drowned objects, deep-sea mermaids, maritime industry, submerged technology and screens, abstract glitch fields, or rare still-life studies of sea pigment. Composition may range from horizonless water to framed windows, mirrored symmetry, tilted sinking geometry, or close murk. Lighting may be overcast flat, bioluminescent point-light, shafts, screen-glow, or dusk fire on water. Color handling may be full monochrome, duotone, desaturated-with-magenta-smear, or purple-black-with-mint-glow. Medium may be photo manipulation, low-poly 3D render, digital painting, underwater photography, or glitch collage. **A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.**

**Avoid**

- Candy-bright Seapunk cyan/magenta or pastel Vaporwave skies as the base palette.
- Sunny tropical beaches, palms, surf lifestyle, clear turquoise shallows.
- Cheerful nautical décor: anchors, ship wheels, red-white stripes, gleaming brass.
- Golden hour, warm cozy interiors, hopeful or triumphant tone.
- Romantic period tall ships or pirate folklore; wrecks belong in murk, not in glory.
- Neon city streets, cars, signage (After Hours territory).
- Crisp commercial HDR clarity, clean product photography, white-gallery minimalism.
- Gore, jump-scare monsters, occult spookiness (Witch House territory).
- More than one competing saturated accent; overall high saturation.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Vs Seapunk:** same motif pool (ocean, statues, 3D gloss), but Seapunk is vibrant, glossy, playful. Test: if the water is candy-bright and the surfaces clean, it is Seapunk; Ocean Grunge drains and corrodes the same ingredients.
- **Vs Nautical:** Nautical is heritage maritime — brass, rope, stripes, lighthouse postcards, warm wood, clear daylight. Ocean Grunge has no cozy harbor and no cheerful signage.
- **Vs Dark Nautical:** Dark Nautical is painterly storm-folklore — omens, period sailors, lighthouse beams, narrative romance. Ocean Grunge is digital-native, abstract-nihilist, and includes modern industry (rigs, submarines) and glitch degradation rather than folk tale.
- **Vs After Hours:** After Hours locates loneliness in the nocturnal city — neon signs, empty streets, parking lots. Ocean Grunge's loneliness is pelagic or underwater; its only infrastructure is maritime.
- **Vs Grunge:** Grunge is 1990s xerox photocopy, flannel, urban decay in warm dirty olives and sepias. Ocean Grunge shares the degradation and nihilism but transposes both into cold water and digital datamosh.
- **Borderline neighbors:** Witch House shares corrupted typography but is spooky-ethereal and occult; Liminal Space shares emptiness but sets it in architecture. Ocean Grunge's void is always water.
