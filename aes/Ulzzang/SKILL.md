# Ulzzang (Eoljjang)

Ulzzang (얼짱, "best face") is a South Korean online subculture and beauty-fashion aesthetic of the early 2000s that grew out of photo-voting contests on Cyworld, where users posted portraits to be judged and the winners became the country's first internet celebrities. What began as a title became a replicable visual code: a youthful, doll-like, seemingly effortless "perfect face," achieved through specific makeup techniques, understated casual fashion, and codified selfie angles ("selcas"). The aesthetic is fundamentally face-first and self-shot — amateur intimacy rather than studio production — and it later fed mainstream K-beauty standards and K-pop visuals while spreading globally via Instagram. Both young women and young men practice it; the male variant keeps the same soft skin, tinted lip and selca grammar.

## Visual Identity

**Palette.** A low-saturation field of warm neutrals and muted pastels: cream and ivory (#F5EFE6–#EFE6DA), oatmeal and beige (#D9CBB8–#EAE0D5), blush pink (#F2C9C4–#E8B4B8), butter yellow (#E8C468 softened toward #F0D080), dusty navy and charcoal as the occasional dark anchor (#2E3A59–#1F1B18). Skin reads pale with a peach undertone (#F3E3D3 range), and the lips carry the single strongest color note in the frame — a rose-coral band roughly #C4524E to #E08A7A, always diffused at the edges. Saturation stays subdued everywhere; a faint film-like desaturation or hazy wash is acceptable, but never neon, never candy-primary brightness.

**Face grammar (defining motifs, in order of authority).** The makeup is the style's core: a flawless pale dewy "glass skin" base; straight, minimally arched eyebrows; "puppy dog" eyeliner drawn downward to round and enlarge the eye; highlighted aegyo-sal (the under-eye fat band) for a cheerful eye-smile; circle lenses enlarging the iris; and the signature gradient lip, color concentrated inner-center and blended outward to a soft just-bitten haze. Grooming observed across the corpus supports this: see-through air bangs or soft curtain/comma fringes, dark brown-to-black hair in loose natural fall or gentle C-curl, and on male subjects the same smoothed skin, soft fringe and faint lip tint.

**Wardrobe, materials, textures.** Simple, casual, understated pieces that keep the face the focal point: oversized sweatshirts, hoodies and t-shirts, often partially tucked into skinny jeans or tennis skirts — the proportion play of loose top over fitted bottom is a recurring formula. Knitwear dominates the photographic record: fuzzy angora/mohair sweaters, cable and patterned cardigans, cream fleeces; also sailor-collar and school-uniform echoes, berets and simple beanies, round wire glasses, delicate minimal jewelry. Textures are matte and soft-touch — wool, fleece, cotton — with the only sheen reserved for the skin's dewiness.

**Composition.** Selca grammar: arm's-length close-ups from a slightly high angle, head tilted, a hand resting on chin or cheek or half-covering the face; mirror selfies with the phone partially obscuring the torso or face; occasional relaxed full-body poses seated against a plain wall. The face sits central and large; backgrounds are plain walls, soft-focus cafe interiors, bedrooms, or blurred everyday street scenes. Crops trend square or portrait; negative space is quiet, never cluttered.

**Light and medium.** Diffused daylight and window light, high-key and low-contrast, with gentle wraparound softness; a warm golden-hour variant exists but stays hazy rather than dramatic. The medium reads as a beauty-filtered smartphone photo: smoothed skin, brightened eyes, slight soft-focus, mild desaturation in some grades. Hard direct flash, deep shadow and chiaroscuro are absent.

## Mood & Values

Cuteness, innocence, and "natural beauty" that is in fact carefully curated — effort concealed behind effortlessness. The image projects approachability and warmth (the eye-smile) or a soft pensive melancholy, never confrontation or overt sexiness. Underlying values are online popularity and community validation: the look was built to be voted on, emulated and shared, a democratized celebrity where the face itself is the content.

## Era & Origins

Early 2000s South Korea, on Cyworld; ulzzang contests created feedback loops of emulation that codified makeup, fashion and selfie angles into a replicable style, spreading through East and Southeast Asia and later worldwide via Instagram. Associated commercial wave: Stylenanda, Etude House, Innisfree. The aesthetic reacts against nothing explicitly but stands apart from professional entertainment polish — it is user-generated, bedroom-and-cafe native, and its lasting legacy is the global K-beauty makeup canon.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Face first: the face is the subject and the focal point; clothing and setting recede in scale, color and detail.
2. Curated naturalness: makeup effects (gradient lip, straight brow, doe eye, aegyo-sal) must read as fresh skin, not paint.
3. Soft light only: diffused, high-key-leaning, low contrast; no flash, no hard shadow.
4. Muted palette: warm neutrals and pastels at low saturation; lips as the sole saturated accent.
5. Loose-over-fitted silhouettes in soft matte fabrics; minimal accessories.
6. Selca intimacy: self-shot framing, high angle, hand-to-face gestures, mirror-selfie variants, plain or blurred everyday backdrops.
7. Phone-camera finish: smoothed skin, brightened eyes, slight haze or mild film desaturation — not DSLR editorial crispness.

**Variation axes.** Subject: female or male, teen to young adult, solo (a pet or small prop like an umbrella or drink is permissible). Setting: bedroom, cafe, school corridor, plain wall, quiet street. Composition: extreme close-up selca through three-quarter to full-body mirror or seated shot. Lighting: cool window daylight through warm golden hour, always diffused. Color handling: cream/oatmeal neutrals through blush, butter, mint or dusty navy accents. Mood: bright eye-smile cheer through soft pensive calm. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

**Avoid.** Hard direct flash and deep shadows; heavy contour, bronzer or matte full-coverage Western makeup; upturned winged cat eyeliner; fully painted, dark or matte lips (the gradient is non-negotiable); thick high-arched brows; tanned or heavily textured skin; neon or saturated candy colors; loud graphic prints, logos and streetwear branding; bodycon or sexualized styling; goth/alt hardware (chains, fishnets, stacked piercings); cluttered maximalist sets; studio editorial lighting and poses; heavy grain or high-contrast noir grading.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Baddie:** matte sculpted contour, winged liner, long acrylic nails, bodycon clothing, confrontational gaze, saturated or moody grade. Ulzzang swaps all of it for dewy skin, downward liner, oversized knits and a soft, inviting gaze.
- **E-Girl:** heavy nose-spanning blush, graphic liner marks, split-dyed hair, chains and fishnets, sticker-cluttered frames. Ulzzang grooming reads "natural-curated" with no alt subculture hardware.
- **Hallyu:** industry-produced idol and drama polish — stage costumes, editorial lighting, high-gloss production. Ulzzang is user-generated: phone-camera softness, everyday clothes, domestic backdrops.
- **Kawaii:** Japanese candy-colored costume cuteness — bows, prints, posed childlike theatrics. Ulzzang is muted, casual and understated; the face, not the costume, carries the cuteness.
- **Kkonminam:** the "flower boy" pretty-male ideal, often styled editorially with richer wardrobe and coiffed hair. Male ulzzang overlaps in grooming but stays selfie-native: plain walls, casual knitwear, arm's-length intimacy rather than produced portraiture.
