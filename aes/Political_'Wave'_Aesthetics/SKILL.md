# Political 'Wave' Aesthetics

Political 'Wave' Aesthetics is not one look but a family of looks: internet-born visual dialects, emerging in the late 2010s, that borrow the retrofuturistic kit of Vaporwave and Synthwave and repurpose it as ideological packaging. The family is defined by *purpose* (online propaganda or polemic aimed at young audiences) rather than by music, and every lane in it performs the same operation: take found political imagery — a portrait, a flag, a procession, a tank, a bust — and run it through the degraded dream-machine of 80s/90s digital nostalgia: neon grids, sunset glows, VHS tracking tears, glitch displacement, scanlines, and caption typography lifted from meme culture. The result reads simultaneously as reverence and as irony: propaganda wearing the costume of a remembered future.

## Visual Identity

**Palette rule.** Two color systems always collide: the *neon substrate* inherited from Synthwave/Vaporwave, and the *source material's own colors* (flag reds, archival greys, gilded icon gold), unified by one dominant grade. The substrate range runs magenta/hot pink (#FF71CE–#C42B6E), cyan-turquoise (#01CDFE–#39E6C0), violet grid purple (#7A3FF2–#B967FF), and sunset amber/signal yellow (#FFB347–#FFD24A, up to a full monochrome amber field around #FFC400). The ideological layer supplies crimson (#D22630–#7A0C14) for stars, sickles and crosses; archival silver-gelatin greys (#D8D8D8–#141414) for historical photographs; desaturated olive-slate and gunmetal (#4B5320–#2F3A3A) for militaristic lanes; and pure red/green (#FF0000 / #00FF00) as chromatic-aberration fringe on black-and-white duotone work. A correct image may sit anywhere in this range — full neon saturation, a single-hue monochrome field, or near-monochrome duotone — as long as one unifying grade welds the found image to the neon layer.

**Signature motifs, ranked by how centrally the documentation treats them.**
1. *Retrofuturistic substrate*: neon perspective grid floors, horizon suns or glowing roundel emblems behind the subject, wireframe domes, palm silhouettes, scanline fields.
2. *Ideological emblems*: hammer and sickle, red star, circled-A, NATO four-point compass rose, St George's cross, royal cyphers and national flags, religious icons; extremist lanes additionally use fascist symbology and black-sun geometry, which are recognition markers only and never generation targets.
3. *Political portraiture*: theorists (Marx, Lenin, Mao), monarchs and emperors, saints and madonnas, contemporary politicians, soldiers — almost always duotoned, pink-washed, or glitched.
4. *Glitch and degradation*: RGB split, datamosh smears, displacement tears, heavy grain.
5. *Slogan typography*: black rectangular caption bars with lowercase pixel/monospace text, small corner slogan boxes, full-width stretched titles, date stamps, and (in the fascist lane) Fraktur blackletter.
6. *Japanese text*, typically vertical kana/kanji columns at the margins.
7. *Classical and religious art*: marble busts, paintings of saints, procession photography.
8. *Military hardware and combat-footage captures*: jets, tanks, formations, video-game war captures.
9. *Obscured faces*: balaclavas and skull masks in the low-fi insurgent lane.

**Materials and textures.** The base layer is always *found media*: a news photograph, archival studio portrait, painting reproduction, procession snapshot, or footage still. Onto it go VHS tape grain and tracking error, CRT scanlines, JPEG compression crunch, prismatic light-leak streaks, halftone or scanline monochrome fields, and neon glow bloom. Nothing looks shot-for-camera; everything looks *recovered and re-processed*.

**Composition tendencies.** Emblem-as-halo: a glowing roundel, sun or symbol centered behind a figure or family group. Flag-as-backdrop: a full-bleed national or movement flag behind a cropped subject. Moodboard grid: multiple tiled panels, each a mini-edit, forming one plate. Margin typography: vertical Japanese columns or stacked titles along the edges. Centered caption bar slicing the image like a meme label. Wide crowd or procession shots with the horizon glitched. Paired historical portraits with date stamps and epithets.

**Lighting.** Source lighting is preserved (news flash, archival studio light, overcast procession daylight) and then contradicted by added light: neon rim glow, sunset backlight, prismatic rainbow leaks — or replaced entirely by a flat single-hue scanline field with no naturalistic light at all.

## Mood & Values

Ironic reverence: the image treats its ideology with the solemnity of a shrine and the detachment of a meme at once. Nostalgia is a recruitment tool — politics packaged in the comforting, haunted grammar of remembered tape decks and arcades. Depending on lane the mood slides between sincere revival (spiritual resurgence, royalist longing, alliance pride), populist optimism, satirical embrace, and transgressive "edgy" provocation. The shared value is *aestheticization of ideology*: the belief that a cause can be made desirable by making it look like a remembered future.

## Era & Origins

Emerges mid-to-late 2010s from Vaporwave's own ambiguous politics (its satire of consumerism and late capitalism seeding the leftist lane) and from Synthwave's 80s retrofuturism; the first widely reported derivative appears around 2016, with meme lanes dating from October 2016 and a reclaiming centrist lane around 2021. Distribution is native to Reddit, YouTube and TikTok, and the family's growth tracks the political polarization of the 2020s.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Begin from a found-media base: archival portrait, news still, procession or crowd photograph, hardware photo, painting or bust scan.
2. Apply one unifying grade: magenta/pink wash, single-hue monochrome field, or B&W duotone with colored fringe.
3. Add one retrofuturistic substrate layer: grid floor, horizon sun/roundel, wireframe dome, palms, scanline field.
4. Add a coherent ideological emblem set and, usually, a portrait or institution tied to it.
5. Add a typography layer: pixel/monospace slogan in a black bar, corner slogan boxes, vertical Japanese caption, full-width title, or date stamp.
6. Degrade last: chromatic aberration at edges, tracking tear, grain, compression crunch.

**Variation axes.** Subject: any ideology's emblem set across the spectrum, any figure from 19th-century theorist to living politician, any institution — church, army, union, monarchy, protest movement, alliance headquarters. Composition: single centered portrait, emblem-halo group, flag backdrop, crowd wide shot, moodboard tile grid, paired portraits. Color handling: full neon magenta/cyan, monochrome amber/red/yellow field, duotone with red-green split, desaturated militaristic with a single neon accent. Lighting: flat archival plus neon rim, sunset backlight, prismatic leak, or scanline-flat with no natural light. Mood: sincere revival, meme irony, or satire. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

**Avoid.** Clean vector flatness with no grain, glitch or scanlines; natural-color photography without a unifying grade; corporate sans-serif or handwritten typography; apolitical Vaporwave props (busts with operating-system windows, soda cans, storefronts) with no emblem or slogan — that is the parent style, not this one; cinematic high-fidelity grading; chrome Y2K blob renders; mixing opposed ideologies' emblems in one plate (the documented exception is the comparative "political compass" moodboard format); glossy modern poster layout with generous white space. **Content guardrail:** extremist lanes (fascist symbology, black-sun geometry, terror-glorifying masked imagery) are documented here for recognition only; generation should remain in non-extremist lanes and never reproduce hate symbols or terror romanticization.

## Discrimination Cues

**Against Hyperborean.** Hyperborean is a 2020s TikTok/4chan evolution of the fascist lane alone: it narrows the family to a single extremist lineage and drifts toward esoteric, archaic-monumental framing native to vertical short-video. The umbrella style, by contrast, is ideologically plural — red stars, circled-A, NATO roses, Catholic icons and royal cyphers all belong to it — and keeps the explicit Vaporwave kit (grid floors, sunset roundels, VHS tears, meme caption bars, Japanese margin text, moodboard grids). An image carrying any non-far-right emblem, or the overt meme-caption/moodboard grammar, is this style, not Hyperborean; evidence on Hyperborean's precise visual delta is thin, so treat genealogy and ideological narrowing as the primary cues.

**Against parent Vaporwave/Synthwave.** The parent styles stay apolitical or consumer-satirical; the presence of an explicit ideological emblem, a named movement in the typography, or a political figure's portrait is what converts retrofuturism into political 'wave'.

**Against adjacent looks.** Royalcore shares monarchist subject matter but lacks VHS degradation, grids and neon; raw combat or phonk edits lack the Synthwave grade and VHS filter that define the military lane; generic glitch art lacks the found-political-media base and slogan layer.
