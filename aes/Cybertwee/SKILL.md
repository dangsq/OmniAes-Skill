# Cybertwee

Cybertwee is an art movement and aesthetic founded in 2014 by Gabriella Hileman, May Waver and Violet Forest as a deliberate counterpoint to cyberpunk's masculine, cynical starkness. It reinterprets technology through softness, cuteness and femininity: pastel pink and purple, glitter, holographic sheen and kawaii imagery welded onto microchips, reclaimed electronics, wearable tech and LEDs. Its manifesto opens with "The singularity is dear," and everything visual follows from that sentence — sincerity, empathy and sweetness treated as strength inside digital space. The look is DIY by conviction: emoji-stickered scans, gentle selfies, hand-glued craft objects, virtual galleries on Newhive and Tumblr, and conceptual gestures like the 2015 Dark Web Bake Sale of rosewater cookies. Expect a frame where a flip phone sits on taffeta, a crystal grows out of glitter glue, or a low-poly moon rises over a pastel render — never irony, never dread.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette

The rule: pastel pink and lilac carry the majority of frame area; saturated color enters only as *light* (LEDs, glow, bioluminescence) or as an *iridescent shift* across a surface, never as flat graphic blocks; chrome and silver appear only as hardware trim.

| Role | Range |
|---|---|
| Base wash | pastel pink `#FFD9E8`–`#F7B8D4`, lilac `#E3D3F7`–`#C9A7EB` |
| Holographic shift | mint-teal `#8FE3D9`, orchid `#B79CED`, magenta `#FF9EE3`, gold `#F2D16B` sliding across one surface |
| Hardware trim | chrome / silver `#C9CCD6`–`#E8EAF0` |
| Light-only saturation | LED magenta `#FF3EC8`, cyan `#4DE3FF` as emitters, not pigment |
| Night sky (virtual renders) | deep indigo `#2E2A5E`–`#43357E` |
| Sweet neutrals | cream, milk white `#FFF6F9`, `#FDF0F4` |

Holography is a surface treatment, not a hue: any material — plastic shell, film, dome, feather-swirl render — may catch a rainbow caustic gradient while staying pastel overall.

### Signature motifs (ranked by how firmly the text documents them)

1. **Pastel pink/purple fields** with **glitter** and **holographic effects** — the core triad, named everywhere.
2. **Kawaii imagery, emojis, gentle selfies** — integral self-expression, sticker-grade hearts and smileys on scans and collages.
3. **Feminine fabric signifiers**: lace, ruffles, taffeta, flounces, flowers.
4. **Technology fused in**: microchips, reclaimed electronics, plastic, wearable tech, illuminated elements (LEDs, LED nail inlays, metallic makeup). Retouched pink Motorola flip phones and pink Nintendo GameCube controllers are the canonical fusion objects.
5. **Sweetness**: cookies and icing (the rosewater cookies of the Dark Web Bake Sale), candy-soft food styling.
6. **Soft natural motifs**: butterflies, hearts, flowers, seashells (seashell beds in imagined interiors).
7. **Imagined virtual spaces**: sleek minimalist rooms with natural light, bioluminescent algae tanks, isolation tanks; low-poly pastel landscapes under moons and striped planets (Sailor Moon-era magical-girl nostalgia).
8. **Image-corpus extension, thin in text**: quartz crystal points and geodesic domes recur as DIY craft objects and renders, extending the glitter-mineral-holographic logic; treat them as legitimate but secondary.

### Materials and textures

Glitter glue with visible hand-made seams; raw quartz and craft crystals; holographic film and cellophane throwing rainbow caustics; translucent pastel plastic console shells; chrome trim; lace, ruffles, taffeta, crushed velvet platforms, cable knit; satin bedding; royal icing on shortbread; paper scans with sticker edges; soft-shaded or low-poly 3D surfaces.

### Recurring subjects

Hand-made jewel-crafts and crystal objects; retouched vintage consumer tech; fashion details (platform heels, floral socks, LED nails, metallic makeup, "flirty but techy" lines); gentle selfies and soft portraits, face sometimes hidden behind bouquets; LED-lit bedrooms and studios; virtual galleries, domes and night landscapes; baked sweets; manifesto zines and emoji-decorated letters; thumbnail-grid moodboards.

### Composition and lighting

Centered single object on a reflective iridescent or satin ground; intimate crops of hands, feet, garment detail; room-wide interiors bathed in pink LED strip and ring-light haze; horizon-based renders with a moon or planet high in an indigo sky; grid collages and scanned pages. Light is emissive and tender: LED strips, neon rings, bioluminescent jars, overexposed daylight, on-camera flash grain with soft focus. Never chiaroscuro, never hard editorial strobe.

## Mood & Values

Sincerity over irony. Tenderness as power, sentimentality as resistance, empathy against mechanical efficiency. An anti-sexist, technofeminist project writing femme-identified people into futures as creators, not subjects; open-source collaboration; humans as "emotional cyborgs" blurring real and digital life. Nostalgic optimism for late-1990s utopian tech, filtered through 1980s twee music's soft answer to punk. Gentle provation: cookies sold on the dark web, glitter inside encryption culture.

## Era & Origins

Founded 2014 as a collective out of Hileman's thesis on feminine cyborgs in cyberpunk and cyberfeminism (with lineage to VNS Matrix). Modeled on late-1980s twee music as punk's softer counter-response; visually nourished by late-1990s utopian tech imagery, Sailor Moon, and the nostalgic optimism of Seapunk and Vaporwave. Lives on Tumblr, Newhive, closed Facebook groups; fashion touchstones include McQueen-era Givenchy (A/W 1999). Key project: the 2015 Dark Web Bake Sale.

## Generation Guidance

Principles:
1. **One frame, two vocabularies.** Pair at least one soft/feminine signifier with one technological signifier — or render the technology itself soft (pastel plastic, lace-wrapped circuits).
2. **Pastel carries the frame;** saturation arrives as glow or holographic shift.
3. **Light is emotion:** LEDs, ring lights, bioluminescence, overexposure, flash grain.
4. **Keep the DIY hand:** glue seams, sticker emojis, scan edges, amateur tenderness.
5. **Sincere, never ironic:** no satire, no dystopia, no menace.
6. **Hardware vintage ≈ 1997–2005:** flip phones, cube-era consoles, CRT towers, microchips.
7. **Kawaii as language, not branding:** hearts, butterflies, flowers, emojis as decoration.

Variation axes: subject (craft object, tech still life, fashion detail, portrait/selfie, lit interior, virtual render, baked goods, zine scan, collage grid); composition (centered object, intimate crop, horizon render, grid); lighting (LED glow, flash snapshot, soft daylight, luminescent night); color handling (full pastel wash, pastel plus one LED accent, holographic-dominant, indigo-night render); medium (photo, 3D render, digital collage, illustration, paper scan); mood (sweet sincerity, dreamy melancholy, playful sparkle). **A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.**

Avoid: black-dominant noir, rain-slick streets, acid-green terminal glow; cynicism, violence, horror dread; cold steel-blue chrome dominance without pastel; crisp white-and-primary corporate futurism; ocean-only aqua palettes with marine fauna as subject (seashell accents are fine); analog folk twee with zero technology; glossy commercial kawaii merchandising and mascot logos; hard-surface mechs, weapons, tactical gear; high-contrast editorial strobe; desaturated beige minimalism; grunge dirt; ironic vaporwave parody grids.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Cyberpunk:** shares chrome and tech, inverts everything else. Dark, rainy, cynical, neon-on-black = cyberpunk; pastel, glowing, sincere = Cybertwee.
- **Cyberprep:** both optimistic futurisms, but Cyberprep is crisp white, primary colors, preppy corporate leisure with no glitter, lace or DIY hand; Cybertwee is handmade, glittered and feminist.
- **Kawaii Gamer:** overlaps in cute pastel peripherals, but stays at cozy consumer desk setups with current-gen gear; Cybertwee uses vintage tech, expands into virtual renders, fashion, zines and conceptual art, and carries technofeminist intent.
- **Seapunk:** shares early-2010s holographic 3D-render lineage, but Seapunk is aqua/teal with dolphins, water and liquid chrome; Cybertwee is pink/lilac with flowers, hearts and cookies.
- **Twee:** shares softness and handmade charm, but Twee is analog indie-folk — cardigans, ukuleles, hand-drawn animals, mustard and cream — with no technology; add LEDs, microchips and holography and it crosses into Cybertwee.
- **Vaporwave (nearby):** ironic magenta-cyan nostalgia with busts and grids; Cybertwee's nostalgia is sincere, pink and craft-based.
