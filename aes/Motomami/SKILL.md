# Motomami

Motomami is a conceptual aesthetic born in March 2022 with ROSALÍA's album *MOTOMAMI*, spread from Spain through TikTok into street style and high-fashion runways (Miu Miu, Chanel, Balmain, Dior; worn by Bella Hadid and Kim Kardashian). Its entire visual logic is a named duality: "moto" — hard, masculine, protective, mechanical — against "mami" — soft, vulnerable, feminine, exposed. Every image in this style stages that collision: a cat-ear helmet over bare skin, a racing jacket over a sheer top, ballpoint love-notes scrawled around a nude figure, pink heart nail art under a liquid-chrome gothic logotype. It quotes 1950s biker leather, 2000s pop, Japanese manga/anime (Akira) and cyberpunk, but always through a minimalist, gallery-clean art direction of white voids, torn paper and handwritten text.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette

The field is almost always black-and-white; red is an *event* inside that field, and pink is a whisper against it.

| Role | Hex range | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Paper / void white | #FFFFFF – #F2EEE6 | Dominant background: seamless studio, scanned paper, poster ground |
| Moto black | #0B0B0B – #1C1C1E | Leather, bodysuits, boots, sunglasses, ink |
| Lipstick / racing red | #C8102E – #E60023 (chrome highlights to #FF1E3C) | Logotypes, silkscreen prints, gloves, dresses, nails, lips |
| Soft "mami" pink | #F6C9D4 – #FF9EC4 | Small accents: nail art, hearts, helmets, dresses under black jackets |
| Ballpoint ink | #2E3A8C, violet #4B3FA5 | Handwritten marginalia over paper and skin |
| Chrome silver | #B9BDC4 | Jewelry, grillz, liquid-metal type highlights, hardware |

Rule: red never becomes an ambient wash (no red rooms, no red fog); it appears as discrete objects — type, garment, glove, nail, lip — on a black/white ground. A single off-palette garment (mustard yellow, silver-grey, olive) is acceptable if the red-black-white scaffolding still holds.

### Signature motifs (ranked by how centrally the style documents them)

1. **Leather biker jacket** — the iconic garment; oversized or cropped, often with racing sponsor patches and quilted padding.
2. **Motorcycle helmet, cyberpunk-styled with cat ears** — the defining accessory; worn as portrait framing, sometimes as the only garment.
3. **Racing silhouettes** — racing-style pants and full racing suits, leather miniskirts, pleated "schoolgirl" skirts as playful juxtaposition.
4. **Butterfly** — emblem of transformation; appears as jewelry, prints, ink illustrations (often pierced by a dagger or wrapped in a banner).
5. **Handwritten, scribbled text and symbols** — the album artwork's graphic language: confessional Spanish fragments, tracklist-style word columns, doodled hearts, stars, spirals, arrows, written in marker or ballpoint across paper, posters, skin and negative space.
6. **Oversized Y2K sunglasses** — flat rectangular shields, worn indoors and outdoors.
7. **Heavy footwear and gloves** — thigh-high leather boots, chunky moto boots, long opera gloves; chunky silver jewelry, grillz.
8. **The motorcycle itself** — as silhouette in silkscreen/stencil prints and as implied presence (gloves, helmets, hazard stripes) more often than as a ridden machine.
9. **Liquid-gothic / tribal chrome logotype in red** — the melted, spiky letterform treatment of the word MOTOMAMI, stamped across bodies, posters and CD faces.
10. **Macro fragments of the body** — mouths with jeweled teeth and a cigarette, manicured hands, nails painted with hearts and stars; treated deadpan, airbrushed smooth.

### Materials and textures

Glossy and matte leather, quilted protective padding, mesh and sheer chiffon, torn and scanned paper, taped collage edges, silkscreen and stencil ink on poster stock, ballpoint and marker strokes, liquid-chrome type, airbrushed skin, polished silver, plastic helmet shells, vinyl CDs, chunky rubber boot soles, hazard-stripe metal.

### Recurring subjects

Full-body fashion figures (posed or walking); cropped or hair/arm-concealed nudes presented clinically, not erotically; helmeted portraits on seamless grounds; hands, nails and mouths in extreme close-up; deadpan object stills (helmet, CD in a player, racing gloves); torn-paper zine collages of snapshots; street candids at crosswalks, shutters and striped poles; runway and fashion-week looks.

### Composition and lighting

- **White-void centering:** a lone figure or object on bright paper/seamless white, with negative space activated by handwritten marginalia rather than left empty.
- **Collage asymmetry:** torn edges, layered snapshots, scribbles crossing photo boundaries, logotypes stamped over bodies.
- **Deadpan object framing:** centered, flat, product-shot stillness for helmets, CDs, gloves.
- **Street flash / overcast candid:** full-body walking figures, harsh on-camera flash or flat daylight, urban asphalt and metal backgrounds.
- **Studio single-source:** clean seamless grey-white with crisp shadow for helmet portraits.
- **Airbrushed macro:** gradient-soft, poreless light for mouth/hand close-ups.
Lighting is never golden-hour, never moody neon night; it is flat, hard, or clinically soft.

## Mood & Values

Transformation, strength, vulnerability, self-confidence, sexual empowerment, feminine power. The mood is confrontational tenderness: aggression (leather, helmets, glare, grillz) held in the same frame as intimacy (handwritten confessions, pink hearts, bare skin, butterflies). There is humor and self-awareness — scribbled jokes, crossed-out words, tracklists as decoration — and an unapologetic ownership of the body. Rawness and polish coexist: ballpoint mess inside gallery minimalism.

## Era & Origins

2020s, Spain and the internet (TTok), crystallized March 2022 with the album campaign — cover, music videos, tour wardrobe — and peaking as a trend through 2022–2023. It descends from 1950s biker leather and Yves Saint Laurent's 1960s interpretation, updated through Y2K pop, Japanese manga/anime (Akira) and a cyberpunk sensibility. It reacts against both pure masculine biker realism and against glossy 2000s bling by inserting avant-garde minimalism, paper-collage conceptuality and feminist duality into moto iconography.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Stage the duality: pair at least one "moto" element (leather, helmet, racing patch, boot) with one "mami" element (sheer fabric, pink, butterfly, handwritten tenderness, bare skin).
2. Keep the ground minimalist: white paper, seamless studio, or flat urban daylight — never a busy atmospheric environment.
3. Let text be image: handwritten scribbles, doodles or liquid-gothic red type occupy negative space or cross the subject.
4. Red is an object, not an atmosphere; pink is an accent, not a field.
5. Treat the body and objects deadpan — clinical, confident, slightly ironic.

**Variation axes.** Subject may be any of: fashion figure, concealed nude, helmeted portrait, body macro (mouth/hands/nails), object still, collage poster, street candid, runway shot. Composition may range from centered void to torn collage asymmetry to diagonal walking candid to extreme macro. Lighting may be scan-flat, hard flash, overcast daylight, single-source studio, or airbrushed soft. Color handling may shift red between type, garment and props, and may admit one off-palette garment; mood may slide from aggressive to tender as long as both poles remain visible. **A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.**

**Avoid.**
- Neon cyan/magenta night cities, rain-slick streets, glowing techwear (cyberpunk drift).
- Garage/asphalt realism, denim-and-chrome vintage patina, all-male biker gangs (pure Biker).
- Velour tracksuits, pastel logo-mania, rhinestone sparkle, glossy consumer bling (McBling).
- 3D chrome renders, UV club gradients, perreo dancefloor neon (Neoperreo).
- Tracksuit sportswear with heavy gold and loud brand logos as the lead read (Choni).
- Golden-hour bokeh, cottagecore softness, beige quiet-luxury minimalism, clean corporate sans-serif branding, ornate maximalist backgrounds.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Vs Biker:** Motomami keeps the leather but adds the conceptual layer — white paper voids, handwritten text, pink accents, butterflies, sheer/schoolgirl juxtaposition; Biker stays in masculine garage realism with denim, chrome and earth tones.
- **Vs Choni:** Choni is Spanish working-class street loudness (tracksuits, gold, animal print, slicked hair); Motomami is art-directed minimalism with avant-garde helmets and collage graphics.
- **Vs Neoperreo:** Neoperreo is digital-native Latin club gloss (chrome 3D type, neon gradients, rendered bodies); Motomami is analog-paper, ballpoint-ink, red/black discipline with biker hardware.
- **Vs Cyberpunk:** Cyberpunk's futurism is environmental (night city, circuits, neon); Motomami's futurism is a single quoted object — the cat-ear helmet — inside daylight or white void.
- **Vs McBling:** McBling's pink is the whole outfit (velour, rhinestones, logos); Motomami's pink is a nail, a heart, a helmet stripe against dominant black leather and matte paper.
