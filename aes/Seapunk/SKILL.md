# Seapunk

Seapunk is an internet aesthetic and electronic music microgenre that emerged on Tumblr and Twitter in 2011, beginning as an inside joke before crystallizing into a visual language. Its core operation is a **utopian, glossy filter applied to early cyberpunk and 1990s cyberculture**: the aquatic dream of the decade's first consumer 3D software, net art, and video games like Sega's *Ecco the Dolphin*. The visual side always outweighed the music; even after the scene burned out post-2012, its imagery fed Vaporwave and spawned named offspring like Slimepunk and Icepunk. Think wet chrome, kind dolphins, and a screensaver that achieved enlightenment.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette

The palette is cool-aquatic dominant with pastel-warm accents, always bright and never muddy. A working rule: roughly two-thirds of any image sits in the cyan–aquamarine–turquoise band, with lilac and pastel pink carrying skies, highlights and accents; deep teal or ocean blue supplies contrast, and occasional acid green or hot magenta appears as a sky gradient or emissive horizon band.

| Role | Range |
|---|---|
| Core water / shader | cyan `#00FFFF` → aquamarine `#7FFFD4`, turquoise `#30D5C8–#40E0D0` |
| Secondary fill | seafoam green `#9FE2BF–#B2E9D0`, deep teal `#008B8B–#006677` |
| Sky / accent | lilac `#C8A2C8–#B57EDC`, pastel pink `#FFD1DC–#F4A7C3` |
| Optional spikes | acid green `#66FF66`, hot magenta `#FF6EC7` (gradients, flares, horizons) |

Gradients between these hues are airbrushed and dithered, never flat vector fills.

### Signature motifs (ranked by how firmly the text documents them)

1. **Bodies of water as subject and shader** — oceans, swimming pools, tropical coastlines; water appears both as scene and as mirror-reflective surface treatment.
2. **Early-3D CGI grammar** — low-poly faceted geometry, chrome and glass shaders, dithered gradient skies, pixelated horizons; the look of 1990s net art and game graphics.
3. **The dolphin–pyramid–palm tree trio** — CGI dolphins, floating or half-submerged pyramids/prisms, and palm trees populate neon-pastel seascapes.
4. **Rotating geometric shapes and reflective surfaces** — the aesthetic's native format is the animated GIF loop: spinning primitives over aquatic backdrops.
5. **Yin-yang symbols and smiley faces** — 1990s pop-culture sigils, on artwork and on clothing alike.
6. **Nautical-rave fashion kit** — hair dyed turquoise, lilac, sea blue or aquamarine; transparent plastic jackets, tie-dyed shirts, tartan overshirts; plastic circle-lens sunglasses, shell jewelry, baseball caps.
7. **1990s computer UI framing** (image-corroborated extension of the net-art lineage) — wireframe grids, desktop taskbars, letterboxed screenshot frames, tiny pixel HUD text.
8. **Collage guests** (present in the visual record, not named in the text): classical or Buddhist statue cut-outs, seahorses, coral branches, handheld game consoles, gem crystals. Treat these as permitted collage vocabulary, not required elements.

The infobox's motif tag "Silicon Dreams" sums up the attitude: digital render as dreamscape.

### Materials and textures

Liquid chrome and pearlescent iridescence; glass, crystal and ice-like refraction; transparent PVC; mirror-flat water; faceted low-poly surfaces; airbrushed smooth gradients with visible dithering; soft JPEG-smear glitch; matte organic counterpoints of shell, coral and tie-dye cloth. Everything reads either wet, polished, or rendered.

### Composition and lighting

Favored structures: centered mandalic symmetry (a yin-yang, a pyramid, an enthroned figure); bilateral mirror symmetry across a water axis; horizon-split scenes with a low camera over a reflective plane; objects floating weightless in gradient voids; stacked cut-out collage layers; radial arrangements implying rotation. Lighting is sourceless and ambient: airbrushed gradient skies, glowing horizon bands, lens flares and pale sun discs, specular hotspots on chrome. Naturalistic shadowing is absent or soft; the world is lit by its own gloss.

## Mood & Values

Utopian, playful, sincerely kitschy. Seapunk applies optimism and gloss to cyberculture instead of dystopia — a screensaver paradise with rave energy and new-age calm at once. It values the communal in-joke, DIY meme craft, and affectionate nostalgia for the dawn of consumer 3D. Its history also encodes an underground ethic: when mainstream pop co-opted the look in 2012, the community reacted with backlash and disavowal, so the style carries a faint streak of ironic self-awareness beneath the shine.

## Era & Origins

Coined June 1, 2011 by Brooklyn DJ Julian Foxworth (Lil Internet) in a tweet about a "seapunk leather jacket with barnacles where the studs used to be"; grown on Tumblr and Twitter around artists like Ultrademon (Coral Records Internazionale). Peak mainstream exposure in 2012 via Azealia Banks's *Fantasea* / "Atlantis" and Rihanna's SNL "Diamonds" stage of floating pyramids and chrome waves — the appropriation that helped dissolve the scene. Precedents: 1990s net art, *Ecco the Dolphin*, the film *Waterworld*. Legacy: absorbed into Vaporwave; parent of Slimepunk and Icepunk.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Render as if with 1990s consumer 3D software: low-poly meshes, chrome/glass shaders, mirror water planes, dithered sky gradients, pixel-soft horizons.
2. Water is mandatory as subject or as shader — even a portrait should feel wet, glossy, or aquatic.
3. Hold palette discipline: cool aquatic dominance, pastel lilac/pink accents, gradient blending.
4. Apply the utopian gloss: no dirt, no decay, no dystopia; kitsch embraced sincerely.
5. Design for the loop: rotational symmetry, spinning primitives, floating repetition suggest GIF motion even in stills.
6. Collage freely: cut-out photo elements (dolphins, statues, palms, shells) layered over rendered gradients is authentic method.
7. Season with UI artifacts — a taskbar, a wireframe grid, a letterbox, pixel HUD type — when a cyber-desktop framing suits the piece.
8. Set any type in plain 90s sans-serif caps or chrome WordArt effect; keep it sparse.

**Variation axes.** Subject may range across open ocean, pool, aquarium, tropical shore, underwater, digital desktop, fashion portrait, album cover, or abstract collage. Composition may be symmetric-icon, horizon-split, floating-void, collage-stack, or UI-framed. Lighting may run from calm gradient glow to flare-heavy sunset to flat neon emissive. Color handling may lean pastel-dominant, neon-dominant, or admit the acid-green variant. Mood may slide from new-age serenity to rave exuberance to ironic kitsch. **A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image** — a seapunk piece with no dolphin and no pyramid is still seapunk if the water, the 90s-CGI gloss and the palette hold.

**Avoid**

- Grime, rust, murk, desaturation, decay (breaks the utopian gloss).
- Dystopian cyberpunk night cities, rain-slick neon noir.
- Photoreal modern CGI with heavy ray tracing or subsurface scattering; the low-fi render look is intentional.
- Earth tones: browns, ochres, autumn palettes, matte paper or hand-drawn illustration.
- Aggressive datamosh/glitch destruction; seapunk's glitch is a soft smear, not wreckage.
- Modern flat vector minimalism and clean corporate design.
- Gothic darkness or kawaii-only candy pastel with no aquatic content.

## Discrimination Cues

| Neighbor | Tell that separates it from Seapunk |
|---|---|
| Cybertwee | Soft kawaii pastel, flowers, pearls, fairy gentleness; lacks seapunk's mandated water iconography, rave-surfer edge and 90s render shaders. |
| Icepunk | Frozen climate: snow, ice fields, crystalline winter whites; seapunk's ice appears only as a glass shader, never as weather, and keeps palms, dolphins, tropical pastels. |
| Ocean Grunge | Murky, polluted, seaweed-tangled, desaturated deep-sea melancholy; seapunk is bright, glossy, utopian. |
| PC Music | Hyper-glossy corporate-pop parody, chrome logos, candy primaries, consumer irony; no aquatic mandate, no 90s net-art nostalgia. |
| Slimepunk | Viscous dripping goo, acid-green slime, industrial tubing; seapunk water is reflective surface and gradient, never viscous sludge. |
| Vaporwave (downstream heir) | Ironic consumer critique, Greco-Roman busts, mall muzak dusk, checkerboard plazas; seapunk is pre-ironic oceanic play with rave fashion and yin-yang sigils. |

**Evidence note:** the fashion-photography record is thinner than the digital-art record, and statue/coral collage elements rest on images rather than text; treat them as secondary vocabulary. The infobox motif "Silicon Dreams" is named without elaboration, so it is used here only as a summary tag for the style's digital-dreamscape sheen.
