# Sadboi

Sadboi (Sad Boy) is an internet aesthetic and masculine archetype that emerged on Tumblr and SoundCloud in the early 2010s, named after the "Sad Boys" music collective founded by Swedish rapper Yung Lean in 2012. It visualizes loneliness and anxiety by fusing oversized minimalist streetwear, early-2000s raster graphics, and Cloud Rap iconography into a single degraded image language. Its defining trick is tonal: genuine vulnerability delivered through heavy irony, so that a crying-face emoticon, a wilted rose and a corporate soft-drink logo can occupy the same frame without contradiction. Where the parallel Sadgirl aesthetic descends from feminine literary melancholy, Sadboi descends from hip-hop posturing turned inward — depressive ideation paired with trap bravado, commercial logos reappropriated as anti-materialist jokes, and broadcast-cartoon characters edited into avatars of despair.

## Visual Identity

### Palette

The color rule is monochrome-plus-one: a near-black and grey base cooled by desaturated blue-purple midtones, with white reserved for typography, and at most a single saturated note per image. Neon and pastel hues exist only as digital overlay layers — glitch fringes, emoji, vaporwave-tinted collage backgrounds — never as ambient light on subjects.

| Role | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Void black (backgrounds, hoodies, night) | `#060606`–`#161616` | dominant ground |
| Xerox grey (midtones, anime stills) | `#2a2a2a`–`#585858` | flat, grainy |
| Paper white (type, manga backgrounds) | `#e6e6e6`–`#f4f4f4` | never warm cream |
| Desaturated blue | `#3c4658`–`#5a6a86` | walls, dusk, denim |
| Muted purple / lavender | `#4a3f60`–`#8d80a8` | Simpsonwave-tinted edits |
| Rose / blood red accent | `#6f1120`–`#b0263c` | roses, petals, glitch hearts |
| Chromatic aberration fringes | cyan `#00e0ff`, magenta `#ff2d55` | hairline offsets only |
| Vaporwave pastel overlay (rare variant) | `#a98ff0`, `#7fe3e0`, `#ff9ad5` | collage layer, not lighting |
| Rare garment accent | orange `#c94f1d` | one jacket, one back-print |

An image rendered fully greyscale is equally correct; greyscale is inside the palette, not a departure from it.

### Typography and graphic layer

Text is a structural element, not a caption. Japanese katakana or hiragana strings run alongside short introspective or self-deprecating English lines ("I'll stay alone again", "FAKE SMILE", "2 SAD 4 U"), set in clean sans-serif or pixel/monospace faces, usually white on black. The standard frown emoticon ☹ is embedded in wordmarks ("SAD ☹ BOYS"). Emoji debris — broken hearts, crying-laughing faces, frowns in circles — floats over the image. Early-raster UI fragments appear: fake buttons, timestamps, playback counters, year stamps like "2K14", dashed rules. Thin wireframe grids (graph-paper lines), scanlines and halftone panels overlay backgrounds, recalling early-2000s raster graphics and zine xerox.

### Signature motifs, ranked by textual prominence

1. **Lo-fi degradation**: VHS tracking distortion, scanlines, pixelation, severe JPEG compression artifacts, chromatic aberration, data-bending — the style's first-listed trait; every clean image is treated as a tape copy of a copy.
2. **Censored or concealed faces**: black bars over the eyes of anime characters and photos, hands covering the face, bowed heads, scribbled cross-outs over portraits. The bar often carries ironic text claiming happiness.
3. **Japanese typography + frown emoticon + introspective one-liners**, as above.
4. **Isolated broadcast-animation characters**, predominantly Bart Simpson, subjected to VHS distortion, "ERROR" bars and broken-heart emoji; anime and manga figures (black-haired boys bowed in grief, ink-hatched figures clutching their heads) serve the same role.
5. **Streetwear**: monochrome Nike/Adidas windbreakers and tracksuits, oversized tees, the bucket hat as primary accessory; merch language of FTP, Anti Social Social Club, Vlone, Thrasher, Trash Gang; slogan prints on garment backs.
6. **Reappropriated consumer brands**: Arizona Iced Tea, Fiji Water, corporate logos deployed ironically (a Vaporwave inheritance). The text documents this strongly; in imagery it surfaces as logo play and branded text on clothing, cars and collage.
7. **Roses, often withered**: deep red roses held in front of the face, clutched in a fist, or shed as petals on pavement.
8. **Emoji and UI debris**, broken hearts foremost.
9. **Cloud imagery and dusk skies**: clouds as emblem; silhouettes against fading sunset gradients with power lines.
10. **Melancholy merchandise slogans**: anti-depressant references, crying faces, self-deprecating phrases printed on streetwear — documented by the style's own criticism as central enough to be parodied.

Minor, image-corroborated habits: cigarettes at the mouth in dark close-ups; first-person downward views of own sneakers and pavement; mundane urban terrain (parking lots, apartment blocks, graffiti walls, bedrooms); subtitle text baked under anime stills.

### Subjects

A lone young masculine figure or his anime/cartoon stand-in; back turned, head bowed, face hidden. Equally valid subjects: a still life of roses, sneakers or a branded drink; a dusk landscape with a small silhouette; a zine-collage page of torn paper and halftone panels; a garment seen from behind carrying a slogan; a grey-scale anime frame with broken-English subtitles. Company, when present, is disconnected — two bodies lying apart, faces scribbled out.

### Composition and format

Vertical phone-wallpaper proportion (9:16) is the native frame, with square Tumblr crops and 4:3 VHS frames common. Typographic lockups sit centered above or across the figure; figures are often small in frame, cropped at the chin or mouth, or pushed to the lower third beneath sky or grid. Collage variants arrange panels in flat grids with torn edges. Depth is shallow and accidental: flash-flattened rooms, wall-backed portraits, pavement seen from standing height.

### Lighting and texture

Lighting modes: harsh direct camera flash at night with black falloff; underexposed interiors lit by one cool window; flat overcast daylight; twilight backlight producing silhouettes; directionless flat light in greyscale anime stills. There is no warm golden glow and no studio polish. Textures are media textures: compressed JPEG mush, VHS noise, xerox grain and halftone dots, manga screentone and scratchy vertical hatch lines, scanline overlays, graph-paper grids, flash-snapshot blowout on skin and white cotton.

## Mood & Values

Sadboi expresses emotional vulnerability, irony, nostalgia and self-expression through hip-hop and trap, and the reclamation of male sensitivity against traditional stoicism. The mood is performative melancholy with a wink: anguish is sincere enough to hurt and staged enough to quote itself with a crying-laughing emoji. Nostalgia targets childhood broadcast media and dead digital formats — cartoons, VHS, raster UI, 2000s video-game graphics. Loneliness is spatial: night streets, empty rooms, dusk rooftops, a single figure in a large dark frame. The style's self-awareness is part of it; parody and sincerity share the same motif kit, and the tension between them is the point.

## Era & Origins

Coined via Yung Lean's Sad Boys collective (2012); formed on Tumblr and SoundCloud in the early 2010s; peak popularity 2016–2019 across Tumblr, SoundCloud and YouTube, later migrating into gaming spaces such as Roblox hangout games. It reacts against two things: traditional hip-hop materialism (hence ironic cheap-consumable branding instead of gold and cars) and conventional masculine emotional restraint (hence tears as streetwear). It inherits Vaporwave's logo appropriation, breeds with Simpsonwave and Trxsh/AMV culture, and feeds Emo Rap and Cloud Rap. Iconic figures: Yung Lean, Lil Peep, XXXTentacion, Joji, BONES, $uicideboy$.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

- Degrade after building: finish the image, then subtract fidelity — compression crush, tracking lines, pixelation, chromatic fringing at edges.
- Conceal the face: censor bar, hand, bowed head, scribble, or crop at the mouth. Eye contact is rare and never confident.
- One saturated accent maximum; everything else monochrome or desaturated cool.
- Treat text as composition: Japanese glyphs, a frown emoticon, one short self-deprecating or melodramatic line, white on dark.
- Pair soft feeling with hard posture: roses against sportswear, tears against trap stance, vulnerability inside brand logos.
- Leave an irony valve open: an emoji, a fake UI button, a slogan that undercuts the grief.
- Default to vertical framing and night-or-dusk light unless using the flat greyscale anime mode.

**Variation axes.** A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

- *Subject*: photographed person, anime/manga figure, cartoon edit, still life (roses, sneakers, drink can), dusk landscape with clouds or power lines, zine collage, back-of-jacket slogan study.
- *Composition*: centered type lockup over figure; full-body flash snapshot; first-person feet-on-pavement view; mouth/hand close-up; gridded collage panels; tiny silhouette under a wide sky.
- *Lighting*: night flash, cool single-window interior, flat overcast, twilight backlight, directionless greyscale.
- *Color handling*: pure monochrome; grey plus rose red; desaturated blue-purple; rare pastel-neon vaporwave overlay variant; single orange garment accent.
- *Mood register*: anywhere from sincere anguish to deadpan parody, including both at once.
- *Medium*: flash photograph, manga ink with hatch texture, anime still with subtitles, vector edit, xerox zine scan.

**Avoid**

- Clean, sharp, high-resolution commercial photography with no degradation layer.
- Warm cozy saturation, golden-hour glow, cottagecore or pastel softness as ambient light.
- Openly smiling, engaged, eye-contacting faces (smiles only survive behind censor bars or ironic captions).
- Luxury flex: gold chains, watches, sports cars as status symbols (a mundane branded car as deadpan joke is fine).
- Sadgirl/Soft Grunge femininity as the primary register: lace, diary cursive, dried florals in pastel light.
- Gore or explicit self-harm depiction; distress is implied through text, posture and censoring, never shown as wounds.
- Pure vaporwave pastel optimism with no melancholy text or motif.
- Concert-crowd energy, punk/metal chaos, saturated multi-color palettes, more than one saturated accent.
- Crisp modern UI or sincere corporate branding; all branding here is borrowed and winked at.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Dead Inside**: shares greyscale anime anguish but drops the irony, emoji and brand play; adds Cyrillic text, hollow blackened eyes and literal nihilism. Sadboi keeps the wink, the streetwear and the VHS brand irony.
- **E-Boy**: TikTok-clean and saturated, groomed curtain hair, chains, layered skate fashion, face visible and posing confidently. Sadboi hides the face, crushes the file quality, and stays monochrome.
- **Emo Rap**: music-scene portraiture with face tattoos, split-dye dreads, punk-grunge layering and stage energy. Sadboi is quieter: minimalist monochrome athletic wear, digital edits, empty rooms.
- **Lo-fi Art**: shares grain and softness but sells cozy calm — study desks, rain windows, warm pastels, no anguish text. Sadboi's grain carries grief and irony over a colder, darker base.
- **Sadgirl**: feminine literary melancholy and Soft Grunge film grain, pastels, florals, handwriting. Sadboi answers with masculine streetwear, hip-hop posture, corporate-logo appropriation, harsher black-and-white and glitch/emoji layers.
- **Simpsonwave** (near neighbor): purple-tinted VHS Simpsons edits for chill nostalgia; Sadboi's Bart carries ERROR bars, frowns and melodramatic captions instead of relaxation.

**Quick recognition check**: degraded file + hidden face + monochrome cool palette + one red or neon accent + Japanese type or frown emoticon + streetwear or cartoon stand-in = Sadboi. Remove the degradation and the irony and it drifts toward Dead Inside or E-Boy; warm it and soften it and it becomes Lo-fi Art or Sadgirl.
