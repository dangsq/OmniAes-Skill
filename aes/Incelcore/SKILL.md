# Incelcore (E-Punk / Bedroom Punk / Losercore)

Incelcore is an Internet rock microgenre and visual aesthetic that emerged from 4chan imageboards (/r9k/ and /mu/) in the late 2010s; the term was coined in 2018 by Negative XP, who later disavowed it in favor of "E-punk." Visually it is "post-Internet": radically distinct from traditional punk or emo because its entire vocabulary is terminally-online — meme faces, MS Paint drawings, imageboard compression, and the cluttered bedroom of a NEET as both set and self-portrait. It rejects fashion-focused rebellion (Scenecore's costumed defiance) in favor of a "give up" / "Lay Down And Rot" posture: no styling, no design skill, no aspiration to taste. Ugliness is the authenticity signal. Irony is the load-bearing wall — provocation, self-hatred and shitposting blur until sincerity is undecidable.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette (rule, not recipe)

The declared core is monochrome: black, white, grey, rendered as checkerboard dither rather than smooth gradient. When color exists, it comes in two illegitimate kinds — *accidental* color (degraded domestic tones produced by cheap cameras and flash) and *pasted-in* color (clipart, flags, logos). Never a harmonized designed palette.

| Role | Range | Where it lives |

| Dither core | #000000–#1A1A1A, #7E7E7E–#9C9C9C, #F2F2F2–#FFFFFF | B&W filters, line art, yearbook grids |
| Accidental domestic | beige plaster #C9BDA6–#E0D6C2, wood veneer #6B4423–#8B5A2B, carpet grey #8E8A82–#A9A49B, yellowed plastic #CFC9B4–#E4DFC9 | walls, doors, CRT casings |

Rule of thumb: at most two or three saturated accents, each looking borrowed from somewhere else (a flag, a can, a WordArt gradient), sitting on a field of dither grey or flash-washed beige.

### Signature motifs and objects (ranked by textual centrality)

1. **Wojak-family meme faces** — Wojak/Feels Guy, Soyjak, Chudjak, Pepe; crude variants substituted for human faces in photos, yearbook grids and memorial layouts.
2. **Crude MS Paint drawings** — shaky black outlines, flat bucket fills: anime girls with clown facepaint, self-portrait line drawings placed beside their photo twin.
3. **The NEET bedroom / bedrotting set** — piles of trash and paper, energy-drink cans, ashtrays, old CRT monitors and beige towers, retro consoles and controllers, CD-Rs, tangled cables, pizza boxes, anime merchandise up to a dakimakura on the bed; the room is the lyric sheet.
4. **Dithered B&W filters and low-bitrate compression** — deliberate mimicry of early-2000s imageboard thumbnails.
5. **Energy drinks and cigarettes** — Monster-style cans, lit cigarettes, overflowing ashtrays as still-life props.
6. **Retro gaming/computing hardware** — CRT glass, six-button pads, low-poly FPS or PS1-grade graphics glowing inside the monitor.
7. **Provocative clothing** — "Criminal Chic" taboo tees, the "please be patient with me, I have autism" cap (black or blue), irony-poisoned political slogan shirts (e.g. MAGA box-logo tees) worn deadpan as contrarian signal.
8. **Canon references** — Scott Pilgrim's Ramona as the resented "Manic Pixie Dream Girl," Joker/Taxi Driver wojaks, stoic "Literally Me" male ideals, Postal 2 / Hotline Miami / Manhunt grit sampled into collages.
9. **Scene promo extensions** (documented in scene photography rather than the written canon): tactical-gear cosplay — balaclava, gas mask, patched vests — deliberately deflated by holding a plush rabbit or cats; split portraits half-photo, half-crude-drawing; yearbook grids with a skull or soyjak replacing one student.
10. **Typography and stickers as collage** — red or black blackletter/gothic titles, chrome beveled metal logotypes, vertical black bars with white sans, typed name lists, Parental Advisory stickers, skull-and-crossbones and silver angel clipart, checkerboard floors.

### Materials and textures

CRT glass curvature and phosphor glow; yellowed beige plastic; aluminum cans; cardboard pizza boxes; plasterboard and wood-veneer doors; carpet; plush fabric and tactical nylon; paper clutter; crumpled flag cloth. On the surface layer: checkerboard dither noise, JPEG blocking, rough magic-wand selection edges around photo cutouts, flat Paint fills, scan-line and webcam softness. Everything should feel touched by a cheap sensor or a free editor.

### Recurring subjects

Deadpan young men (and some women) in self-shot snapshots; masked or geared figures undercut by soft props; the cluttered room itself as protagonist; junk-food-and-hardware still lifes; crude anime/clown drawings; institutional formats (yearbook, memorial, ID) vandalized by meme substitution; collage album covers with the artist cut out and pasted over sky, flags or checkerboard voids.

### Composition and lighting

Composition is anti-design: centered amateur snapshots with clipped heads and tilted horizons; dense scrapbook collages with clipart, stickers and logos stacked at competing scales; split halves; rigid grids broken by one wrong cell; titles crammed at the top edge or run vertically down a black bar; misalignment left uncorrected. The only "clean" layout permitted is a single crude drawing floating on empty white. Lighting is always found and faulty: harsh on-camera flash in a dim room (hot face, black corners), flat yellow tungsten, monitor/RGB glow in darkness, flat overcast window light. Exposure mistakes are welcome; studio light is impossible here.

## Mood & Values

Social alienation, self-hatred and depression delivered through layers of irony so thick that sincerity becomes undecidable. Provocation and gatekeeping as community glue; DIY ethos as proof of authenticity; resentment toward modern dating projected onto pop-culture stand-ins; the "give up" / LDAR posture as anti-aspiration. Humor is a defense mechanism and a weapon simultaneously — the image should feel like a joke told by someone who might not be joking. Deliberate bad taste is the point: if it looks focus-grouped, it is wrong.

## Era & Origins

Coined 2018 out of a scene forming from 2016 on 4chan's /r9k/ and /mu/; genealogy runs through vaporwave archives and the Doomer wave, with early overlap into Scenecore. Offline attempts (2021's Virginfest, the Wizard Tower subset) failed; rebrands followed — "bedroom punk" (2020), "E-punk" (2022) — splitting artists who wanted distance from the name from those who saw rebranding as sanitizing. A 2023–26 TikTok revival (Fried by Fluoride, then the SoundCloud "losercore" strain) softened edges toward Olderbrothercore, femcel and hikikomori 2000s-revivalism. The aesthetic reacts against traditional punk/emo fashion rebellion, against Scenecore polish, and against mainstream PC culture — while visually reacting against professional design itself.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Amateur-first production: assume MS Paint, magic-wand cutouts, chrome WordArt-type logos, 480p exports. Skill gaps are stylistic features.
2. Dither, don't gradient; degrade, don't grade. Compression artifacts are ornament.
3. The room is biography: clutter, cans, CRTs and cables narrate isolation without a person present.
4. Ironic juxtaposition pairs: hard/soft (tactical gear + plush), sacred/profane (crucifix + body pillow), taboo text + deadpan face.
5. Meme grammar: wojak variants stand in for people; institutional formats (yearbook, memorial) get vandalized by substitution.
6. Type is collage: blackletter, chrome metal, vertical sans bars, typed lists, stickers — never a single coherent typeface system.
7. Found faulty light only: flash, tungsten, screen glow, overcast window.
8. Provocation delivered deadpan, through clothing slogans and pasted text, not through spectacle.

**Variation axes.** Subject: self-portrait snapshot, masked cosplay, empty room, junk still life, crude drawing, collage cover, grid layout. Composition: centered snapshot, dense collage, split half/half, rigid grid, lone drawing on white. Lighting: flash, tungsten, screen glow, window overcast. Color: full dithered B&W, degraded domestic color, monochrome plus one or two pasted accents, optional pastel vaporwave fringe. Mood: deadpan, melancholic, provocative, absurd shitpost. Era flavor: 2000s imageboard thumbnail vs. 2020s TikTok losercore softness. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image — a tidy-looking room photo with dithering and a wojak is as valid as a collage cover.

**Avoid**

- Polished professional photography, retouching, or graphic design; clean vector gradients, modern UI sleekness, coherent brand typography.
- Studio lighting, golden hour, cinematic color grading, lens flare glamour.
- Tidy, minimalist, or styled interiors; fashion coordination of any kind.
- Analog film warmth and nostalgic grain used sincerely (that is grunge / Midwest Emo territory); incelcore decay is digital, not chemical.
- Neon cyberpunk gloss or smooth vaporwave renders (Doomer-wave neighbor territory); low-poly game screens yes, neon noir sheen no.
- Bright saturated 3D cartoon trap-rap art (Meme Rap); celebratory geek badge-design (Nerdcore).
- Earnest emotional sincerity with no irony layer; wholesome or motivational messaging.
- Current-generation slim hardware presented attractively (hardware here must read old, yellowed, obsolete).
- Content guardrail: the scene's documented shock tactics stop at ironic slogan clothing, meme faces and edgy text. Do not generate hate symbols, school-shooter glorification, or imagery targeting real people; provocation in this skill lives at the level of deadpan meme logic, not real-world violence.

## Discrimination Cues

| Neighbor | Overlap | What separates incelcore |
|---|---|---|
| Doomer | Wojak faces, alienation, monochrome | Doomer is cinematic, smooth, wistful — desaturated photography and gradient melancholy; incelcore is domestic, crude, dithered, and shitpost-funny, with clutter and meme substitution |
| Grunge | Decay, cigarettes, anti-taste | Grunge decay is analog: film grain, flannel, thrift, stage photos; incelcore decay is digital: JPEG blocks, CRT glass, Paint fills, no analog romance |
| Meme Rap | Internet irony, meme literacy | Meme Rap is bright, saturated, 3D-rendered, trap-flavored absurdism; incelcore is monochrome, squalid, hand-crude, rock/bedroom DIY |
| Midwest Emo | Suburban alienation, self-pity | Midwest Emo is earnest: natural light, film snapshots, twinkly sincerity; incelcore adds meme faces, provocation and deliberate digital ugliness |
| Nerdcore | Games, consoles, geek references | Nerdcore celebrates geekdom with clean graphics; incelcore uses the same hardware as a metaphor for rot, wrapped in self-hatred and anti-design |
| Losercore / Olderbrothercore (descendant strain) | Cluttered rooms, 2000s revival | The revival strain softens into pastel TikTok nostalgia; core incelcore keeps the provocation, dither and offensive deadpan |

Quick tell: if the image looks like it was made in ten minutes by someone who wants you to know they could have tried harder — flash-blown face, dithered grey, a wojak where a person should be, a CRT under a pizza box — it is incelcore.
