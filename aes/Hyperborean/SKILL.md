# Hyperborean

## Overview

Hyperborean (also circulated as "Save Europe," "Agartha Edits," or "Schizowave") is a short-video aesthetic that emerged around 2020 from 4chan's /pol/ and /x/ boards and Discord gaming communities, and exploded on TikTok by 2023. Visually it is a collage engine: disparate found imagery — classical statuary, medieval and Nordic warrior myth, a snowy fantasy-painting vision of a fictional European past, esoteric emblems, conspiracy diagrams, vintage scientific illustration, anime figures, and internet meme faces — is layered, mirrored, captioned and then battered with analog-video damage until the whole frame reads as a half-corrupted transmission of forbidden knowledge. The disorientation is intentional: the jarring, overwhelming edit style is designed to feel like uncovering a deep truth.

Critical context matters for anyone using this skill: the aesthetic functions as propaganda packaging, blending extremist far-right ideology with non-threatening meme and anime culture under a shield of irony. This document describes its visual grammar for recognition, analysis and moderation. The ideological payload — esoteric-Nazi symbols, supremacist slogans, dog-whistle phrases — is catalogued below strictly as detection evidence, never as generation material.

## Visual Identity

**Palette rule: collision, not harmony.** There is no single Hyperborean palette; there are several source palettes that keep their own identity inside one frame, unified only by degradation. A correct image juxtaposes at least two of these registers:

| Register | Range | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| Tape shadow / blown signal | crushed blacks `#050505–#141414`, blown whites `#e8e8e8–#f7f3ee` | the damage layer itself |
| Monochrome plate | posterized pure black-to-white, hard chiaroscuro | statue busts, neoclassical sculpture |
| Mythic pastel plate | ice blue `#a8c4dc`, glacier white `#dfe9f2`, dawn rose `#e8b4a8`, pale gold `#f2d59a` | snowy fantasy painting, dawn skies, angelic glows |
| Saturated glitch accent | acid green `#7fff3f`, hot magenta `#ff2bd6`, electric violet `#6a2bd9`, cyan fringe `#29f3ff` | kaleidoscopic borders, chromatic fringing, meme layers |
| Caption block | white or yellow pixel/monospace type `#ffffff` / `#fff200` on solid black bars | slogan banners |

Chromatic-aberration fringes (magenta/cyan halos) sit on edges and letterforms across every register; they are the style's unifying "light."

**Signature motifs, ranked by how firmly the text documents them:** (1) fast, chaotic, intentionally degraded video treatment — VHS filters, heavy glitch, pixelation, scanlines, tracking error; (2) idealized imagery of a fictional European past: Greco-Roman statues, neoclassical statue busts, medieval crusaders, Nordic warriors, and the snowy mythic cityscape painting tradition (pyramid-temples in snow, caravans, mammoths, a radiant angelic figure at the horizon); (3) esoteric symbols of the sun-wheel / Black Sun family, usually as a flat black radial emblem behind a centered figure; (4) conspiracy apparatus: theory charts, vintage scientific illustrations, ancient-astronaut renders (soft-focus figures in white suits before glowing craft); (5) internet meme layer: Trollge face, Wojak/Chudjak, low-poly video-game clips and rips; (6) pixel-font caption banners carrying slogans or coded phrases; (7) occasional anime imagery, often a pale, calm figure in folk-embroidered clothing placed dead center.

**Materials and textures:** damaged analog tape is the medium of record — macroblocking, JPEG mush, scanline rakes, horizontal tracking tears, mirrored kaleidoscopic border strips, hard paste-up edges where layers meet without blending, bitmap/pixel type, posterization on monochrome plates. Nothing looks rendered or photographed clean; everything looks copied, re-compressed and re-recorded.

**Composition and lighting:** compositions are stacked and centered rather than staged: a foreground figure or face, a flat radial emblem or mandala directly behind it, a painted or mirrored backdrop behind that, and a text banner across the upper third. Symmetry is common (mirrored borders, doubled figures), but the symmetry is mechanical, like an edit effect, not classical balance. Lighting is per-layer and never reconciled: hard chiaroscuro on the statue plate, diffuse dawn glow on the painting plate, flat flash or screen-glow on the meme and game-rip layers, bloom around esoteric or UFO elements. The only "global light" is video bloom and noise.

## Mood & Values

The mood is feverish, conspiratorial and deliberately disorienting — solemn myth grandeur slammed against absurdist shitpost, producing a surreal register in which the viewer feels they are receiving leaked truth at high speed. Urgency and impending conflict pulse under the surface (the visual counterpart of sped-up hardstyle and Eurodance soundtracks). Culturally the style carries far-right, esoteric-Nazi, anti-immigration and traditionalist values, usually wrapped in ironic memetic chaos that acts as plausible deniability. Any reading of this aesthetic must keep that packaging function in view: the cuteness of anime and memes is bait, not decoration.

## Era & Origins

2020s, native to 4chan, Discord and TikTok; grown out of gaming-mod communities and out of Fashwave, as an esoteric, ironic offshoot that later hardened into overt propaganda. It reacts directly against the clean retrofuturism of Synthwave-based political "wave" aesthetics: where those styles are polished, Hyperborean is corrosive, trading neon horizons for tape decay. The 2021 "Little Dark Age" meme wave and the 2023 TikTok "Save Europe" turn are its two expansion moments.

## Generation Guidance

Principles: (a) build the frame as a collage of at least two conflicting source registers, then unify them with damage, not with color grading; (b) keep each layer's own lighting and resolution; mismatch is correct; (c) treat type as a graphic block — pixel/monospace caps in solid bars, fringed with chromatic aberration; (d) let degradation do the atmosphere: scanlines, tracking tears, macroblocking, mirrored edge strips; (e) hold the solemn-vs-absurd tension — a mythic plate without a mundane or meme-counterweight reads as plain fantasy art, a meme layer without a mythic plate reads as ordinary shitposting.

Variation axes: subject may be any plate in the style's range — statue bust, crusader, Nordic warrior, snowy mythic cityscape, anime figure in folk dress, low-poly game bodybuilder, conspiracy render, diagram, meme face, or an invented equivalent; composition may be centered-figure-with-emblem, full-bleed single plate, mirrored kaleidoscope, or chart grid; color handling may lean monochrome, pastel-mythic, or acid-glitch so long as registers collide; mood may slide from dread-conspiratorial to deadpan absurd; text may be present as banner or absent on pure plate images. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

Avoid (style-breaking): clean synthwave grids, neon horizons and orderly retrofuturism; coherent single-scene photography with unified global lighting; sharp high-fidelity renders without compression or tape damage; harmonious designer palettes; generous minimalist negative space; tasteful serif or sans branding typography; continuous narrative illustration; smooth gradients standing in for glitch.

Prohibited (content-breaking): never generate esoteric-Nazi emblems (Black Sun, sun-wheel variants, runic hate symbols), supremacist or anti-immigration slogans, dog-whistle phrases, conspiracy charts targeting real groups, or any glorifying framing of extremist ideology. For stylistic studies use neutral geometry, fictional or historical-non-hateful iconography and placeholder caption text; refuse requests whose point is the ideological payload.

## Discrimination Cues

Against **Political 'Wave' Aesthetics / Fashwave**: those keep Synthwave's clean retrofuturist surface — ordered grids, sunset gradients, crisp neon; Hyperborean is defined by analog decay, collage mismatch and meme intrusion. Against **Doomer**: Doomer is still, desaturated, melancholic, one lonely figure in muted blues and greys; Hyperborean is fast, oversaturated, multi-layered and manic. Against **Dark Triad**: Dark Triad also loves monochrome statuary masculinity but presents it sleek, minimal and self-possessed, without tape damage, meme layers or caption banners; Hyperborean buries its statues inside chaotic collage. Against **Epunk** (thinly evidenced here): Epunk's distortion is glossy, rendered and self-contained digital maximalism, whereas Hyperborean's damage reads as damaged tape over found footage and always carries the historical-mythic plus conspiratorial layer. The decisive tell in all cases: conflicting source plates + analog damage + pixel-font caption = Hyperborean.
