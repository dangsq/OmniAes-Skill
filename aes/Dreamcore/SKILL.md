# Dreamcore

Dreamcore depicts events inside dreams. Its settings are borrowed from liminal space — suburban streets, lawns, hallways, kitchens, pools, empty rooms — but recolored bright, hazy and pastel so that absence reads as comfort rather than dread. Into these softened stages the style inserts dream motifs: eyes that float or grow on mushrooms, winged angels, stitched and object-headed characters, voids, organs, cryptic red text that questions whether the viewer is real. The intended effect is double and deliberate: simultaneously comforted and unsettled, familiar and unverifiable. Born on Tumblr around 2018 with @gbrltvrst's surreal 3D landscapes, named and popularized on TikTok in the early 2020s, amplified by COVID-era compilation videos and later by Backrooms lore, Dreamcore is a kitschy, cheap-edit aesthetic by conviction — its haze, grain and clumsy overlays are the point, not flaws. It descends from Weirdcore but swaps alienation for anemoia and wonder, and it indirectly spawned Nostalgiacore.

## Visual Identity

**Color palette.** Two registers fused under a single veil of haze. The *sky register* is pastel: lilac-lavender, rose pink, peach-apricot, baby blue, mint. The *ground register* is early-CGI saturated: kelly-green lawn, cerulean-to-cobalt sky gradients, cream and beige siding, pure white cloud. Accents are small and candy-bright — signal red (cryptic text, mushroom caps, balloons), candy green and orange balloons, occasional teal pool water. Night variants swap the sky for a deep luminous blue while windows glow warm. Everything sits behind low contrast, soft grain and faint gradient banding; no color ever appears crisp or HDR-clean.

**Signature motifs, ranked by how firmly the style documents them.** (1) *Dreamy liminal settings*: suburban streets and lawns, a solitary house on a hill, domestic interiors with floral carpet and wallpaper, pools and poolrooms, Backrooms-like empty rooms — plus surreal substitutions: clouds or flowers replacing floors and walls, skies pouring through windows, rainbows arcing over rooftops. (2) *Eyes*: the style's recurring obsession — bloodshot eyes floating over cloudscapes and stairways, eyes embedded in mushroom caps, eyes on winged beings. (3) *Clouds, rainbows, sky as material*: cotton-solid cloud bushes crowding a yard, cloud floors under kitchen cabinets, rainbow arcs as set dressing. (4) *Mushrooms*: oversized fly agaric with white-spotted red caps, fungus groves in teal darkness. (5) *Characters and creatures*: object-headed original characters in cardboard-cutout masks, stitched plush-like figures, black silhouettes with pasted facial features, floating winged angels. (6) *Cryptic red text* questioning reality, identity or the rules of entry ("there exists a world…"), set sparse and plain over the image. (7) *CRT TVs and screen motifs*. (8) *Voids, organs, teeth, flowers*. (9) *Borrowed garnish*: yami kawaii pills, bandages and syringes; kidcore stickers and smiling faces; webcore pop-up windows. Sampled imagery confirms settings, clouds, rainbows, eyes, mushrooms, pools and balloons as the working core; CRTs, teeth, organs and pills are text-documented but appear less often in practice.

**Materials and textures.** Flat-shaded low-poly geometry with vertex-lit flatness — PS1-era, Sims-era, old web 3D; matte plaster and vinyl siding; grass as uniform carpet; floral carpet and patterned wallpaper indoors; mirror planes, portals and still reflective water; volumetric clouds with solid, sculptable mass; soft grain, VHS/JPEG softness, slight upscale blur and sky banding over everything.

**Composition and lighting.** Static, tripod-still cameras. One-point perspective down a suburban sidewalk or hallway; a single house centered on a horizon; high or low horizon lines; thresholds — doors, windows, mirrors, standing portals — framing an impossible view; scattered floating motifs arranged like dream debris; scale distortion (giant mushrooms, toy-small houses). Scenes are empty of candid human life; people appear only as silhouettes, masked characters or stickers. Lighting is diffuse and shadowless or soft-shadowed: flat midday brightness, hazy peach-pink sunset with a blurred sun disc, lavender dusk, or luminous night; interiors lit by window-light that is really sky-light.

## Mood & Values

Comfort first, unease second. Dreamcore sells anemoia — nostalgia for places you never lived — and the safety of a childhood room, then slips one wrong thing into the frame: an eye in the clouds, a void where the floor ends, a red sentence asking who you are. The dissonance is the value: dreams as both refuge and identity test. The community prizes self-expression through original characters, cosplay and fashion, and a grainy, deadpan humor (parodied "rules" narrations, stitched characters) that keeps the surrealism friendly.

## Era & Origins

Late 2010s Tumblr imagery (2018), named and exploded on TikTok in the early 2020s, peaking through COVID-era compilations, declining after 2021, resurging 2025–2026 with Kane Pixels' Backrooms film. It reacts to Weirdcore by keeping the dream-logic but replacing dread with comfort, and inherits a games lineage of dreamscapes: EarthBound, LSD: Dream Emulator, Yume Nikki, OMORI, Smile For Me, Superliminal. Its musical cousins are Jack Stauber's warped lullabies and hazy dream-pop.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.** Build a mundane stage first — suburb, room, pool, train car, hillside lawn — then dreamify it: haze the light, pastel the sky, substitute one surface with cloud, flower or sky. Keep the stage empty; add at most one class of dream intruder (eyes, angels, masked figure, mushrooms, void). Render in kitschy medium: low-poly flat shading or soft-grain photo edit, never crisp PBR photorealism. Let comfort dominate and let exactly one element quietly contradict it. If using text, use sparse red sentences that question reality or identity — never collage word-art.

**Variation axes.** *Subject*: any mundane place qualifies — street, kitchen, bedroom, hallway, pool, backyard, train interior, manor at night, mushroom grove, cloudscape; a correct image can use any subject within this range. *Composition*: wide horizon shots, one-point corridors, centered solitary objects, portal framings, floating-motif scatters, vertical or square formats. *Lighting*: flat midday, peach sunset, lavender dusk, luminous night — always diffuse. *Color*: anywhere from washed VHS pastel to candy-bright CGI, so long as haze unifies it. *Mood*: from pure comfort (rainbow over lawn) to gentle uncanny (eyes among clouds), never horror. A correct image should echo these rules, not any reference image.

**Avoid.** Blood, gore, decay, grime and jump-scare horror; heavy glitch corruption, datamoshing or aggressive JPEG-crush as the primary texture; mismatched word-art and caption bars dominating the frame; sticker-bomb maximalism with no empty dreamy space; sepia or faded-film photographic realism; plain desaturated liminal photography with no dream edit; sharp modern CGI with hard shadows and crisp materials; crowds, candid people, or realistic faces; neon cyberpunk or moody desaturated grading.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Kidcore** is awake, cluttered and playful — toys, stickers, primary colors at full energy. Dreamcore borrows its stickers but places them in empty, hazy, liminal dreamscapes with an uncanny undertow.
- **Liminal Space** is straight, unedited, often desaturated photography of empty transitional places, mood of eerie absence. Dreamcore takes the same spaces and recolors them bright and pastel, adds haze, rainbows and motifs, and aims at comfort.
- **Nostalgiacore** is melancholic analog memory — real childhood photos, home-video grain, no surrealism. Dreamcore is rendered or edited dream-logic; it has eyes, angels and voids where Nostalgiacore has only memory.
- **Traumacore** overlays childlike imagery with distress, grime and explicit trauma text. Dreamcore's unsettle is subtle and always wrapped in a comforting, bright frame.
- **Webcore** is UI-centric: old desktops, pop-ups, hyperlinked graphics as the composition itself. Dreamcore may include a pop-up as one motif, but its scene is a three-dimensional dreamscape, not a screen.
- **Weirdcore**, its closest twin, seeks confusion and dread with uniform low-fi grain, distorted objects and mismatched text; Dreamcore seeks wonder and comfort with hazy color, kitschy character edits and dreamy space.
