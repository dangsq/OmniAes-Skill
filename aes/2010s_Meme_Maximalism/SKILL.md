# 2010s Meme Maximalism

## Overview

2010s Meme Maximalism is a retrospective, internet-native collage style: Gen Z artists of the early 2020s rebuilding the visual noise of their "internet childhood" (roughly 2005–2014: 4chan, DeviantArt, Newgrounds, Tumblr, early YouTube) into dense, deafeningly bright compositions. The aesthetic is the *compositional method* — the maximalist meme collage — not any single meme inside it. It lives on TikTok, Pinterest and Roblox, carries the pejorative nicknames "Retroslop" or "Rainbowslop," and answers that criticism with a shrug: the goal is not faithful period recreation but transmuting low-fidelity nostalgic assets into something new and silly. Its emotional contract is "cringe culture is dead": excess is the point, randomness is a virtue, and polish is suspect.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette

The rule is **full-spectrum hue rotation at maximum chroma**. A correct canvas cycles the whole rainbow band — red `#FF0000`, orange `#FF7A00`, yellow `#FFE600`, green `#00C832` to lime `#A6FF00`, cyan `#00E5FF`, blue `#00A2FF`, violet `#7A00FF`, magenta/hot pink `#FF1FA0`–`#FF00FF` — either as a gradient field, as recoloring of a pattern (leopard/cheetah rosettes, stars, splatters), or as object-level coloring (hair strands, tutu panels, stockings each in a different band). Supporting field variants: flat hot pink (`#F45FA2`–`#FF3399`), electric purple, and black fields carrying neon self-lit accents. Black `#000000` is structural — thick outlines, checkerboard squares, studded belts — and paper-white `#FFFFFF` appears as character faces and macro panels, never as breathing room. Saturation has a floor: nothing muted, earthy, or dusty belongs here. Glitter adds an iridescent silver-grain overlay tone.

### Signature motifs and objects (ranked by how centrally the style documents them)

1. **Rage comic faces** — Trollface, Me Gusta, Forever Alone, "Y U NO" guy: the style's most prominent motif, stamped crude and aliased.
2. **Rainbows in every physical form** — gradient canvases, glitter-textured arcs, pixel trails behind flying cats, rainbow animal prints, rainbow hair.
3. **Chaotic background patterns** — rainbow gradients, galaxy print, brightly colored paint splatters.
4. **Nyan Cat** and pixel-art cats with Pop-Tart bodies.
5. **Epic Face and yellow smiley spheres**, repeated in clusters.
6. **Image macros and Advice Animals** — character head on colorful wheel background, top/bottom text in Impact font.
7. **MLG montage-parody elements** — hitmarkers, scope circles, pixel sunglasses, montage noise.
8. **Sparkledogs** — spiky anthropomorphic animals with checkerboard socks, beaded bracelets, patterned body panels.
9. **Kaomoji and emoticons** sprinkled as textural confetti.
10. **Anthropomorphized internet figures** (Derpina-type characters, Nyan Cat as a girl, the old Roblox Noob) dressed in exaggerated Scene/Scenecore/Kandi Raver wardrobe: bright dyed choppy hair with side-swept bangs, huge black or rainbow bows, studded belts, striped stockings, fishnets, tutu skirts, kandi beads, heavy makeup.
11. **Decorative text as object** — chrome/bubble-letter logo words, username watermarks and signature stamps, year stamps ("2009," "2010"), handwritten scrawls, speech bubbles, declarative cringe-positive slogans.
12. **Glitter and sparkle stamps** — star bursts, sparkle lens flares, glitter-grain rainbows (the Glitter Graphics inheritance).

### Materials, textures, medium

The style is digitally native and deliberately mixed-fidelity: aliased MS Paint linework and spray tool, low-resolution pixel stamps and soft-JPEG macro scraps, sit beside clean thick-outlined vector/airbrushed character art with glossy gradient shading and chrome lettering. Textures are screen textures, not physical ones: glitter grain, pixel dither, checkerboard fabric, fishnet mesh, studded leather, tulle tutu, rainbow leopard print, beaded kandi. There is no paper grain, no canvas, no film stock.

### Recurring subjects

Meme faces and meme characters; original sparkledogs and scene-kid personas; anthropomorphized animals and objects; blocky brick-avatar game characters; chibi screaming creatures; moe-style girls (the overlap with 2010s Soft Kawaii); character-sheet layouts showing one persona in multiple head studies and poses.

### Composition and lighting

Density is the composition: little to no negative space, sticker-stack layering with tilted overlapping elements, multiple competing focal points, borders crammed with stamps, corners claimed by watermarks. Accepted layout families include the full-bleed collage, the character sheet, the single figure on a patterned or flat saturated field with a glitter rainbow, the Advice-Animal macro panel, and the letterboxed animation-meme still. Lighting is screen-native and flat: cel flats or airbrushed glow, glitter emission, sparkle flares, neon self-illumination on black fields. No photographic light sources, no cast-shadow logic, no depth of field. (The wiki text does not prescribe lighting; this flat-render range is what the visual record consistently shows.)

## Mood & Values

Exuberant, defiant, deliberately "cringe." The stated values are "epicness," random humor, nostalgia, controlled chaos, and the death of cringe culture. Nostalgic authenticity outranks polish: a wobbly MS Paint line is more truthful here than a refined render. The style celebrates communal internet memory — watermarks, signatures and inside-joke stamps mark it as fan-made gift culture — and meets mockery ("Retroslop") with proud silliness rather than correction.

## Era & Origins

Source material: 2005–2014 internet culture (rage comics, Nyan Cat, Advice Animals, YTP, asdfmovie, Charlie the Unicorn, MLG montage parodies, Vocaloid mascots, MLP:FiM, Invader Zim's GIR). The revival itself crystallized in the early 2020s on TikTok, Pinterest and Roblox; the name was coined on the Aesthetics Wiki in 2025 to replace the pejoratives. It is frequently mislabeled as Scene, but Scene was a music-based subculture; this is a purely meme-focused visual style, though it genuinely overlaps with Scenecore, the 2020s TikTok revival of Scene fashion. A 2025 backlash cycle (e.g., the "Don't Touch My Pizza" mockery) and the counter-meme "Great Meme Reset" both orbit this style's loud nostalgia.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Fill the frame: density first, negative space only as a deliberate flat-field variant.
2. Run the full rainbow: hue-rotate across the spectrum in background, wardrobe and props; keep chroma at the ceiling.
3. Mix fidelities on purpose: crude pixel/MS-Paint stamps against clean thick-outlined vector characters and glitter overlays.
4. Treat text as decoration: Impact-font macro lines, bubble-letter logos, kaomoji, watermarks, year stamps, scrawled slogans.
5. Dress characters in the scene/kandi grammar: choppy dyed hair, bows, studded belts, striped stockings, fishnets, checkerboard, kandi beads.
6. Juxtapose for "random" humor: unrelated mascots, animals and objects colliding with equal emphasis.
7. Keep light flat and screen-native: cel flats, airbrush glow, glitter emission — never photographic.

**Variation axes.** Subject: any period meme, original sparkledog or scene persona, anthropomorphized animal/object, blocky game avatar, moe girl, screaming chibi — any subject is admissible. Composition: full collage, character sheet, single figure on patterned or flat field, macro panel, letterboxed animation still, sticker-bomb border. Color handling: rainbow gradient canvas, rainbow animal print, galaxy print, splatter field, flat hot pink or black field with rainbow accents. Rendering: pixel dither, aliased paint, clean vector airbrush, glitter grain, moe-soft shading. Mood: chaotic silly, defiant cringe, sweet kawaii overlap, aggressive scream-comedy. **A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.**

**Avoid.** Minimalist negative space and clean grid layouts; muted, earthy or desaturated palettes; photorealism, photographic lighting, depth of field, film grain; modern 2020s meme vocabulary and sleek corporate flat design; deep-fried mud saturation or heavy datamosh corruption as the primary treatment; vaporwave pastels and chrome retrofuturism; realistic anatomy and single-point perspective as the default; serif or tasteful modern branding typography; matte physical textures (paper, canvas, print grain); solemn or ironic-minimal tone.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Galaxy Print:** owns nebulae and starfields as subject and dreamy backdrop. Here galaxy print is only one optional canvas *underneath* meme stickers; without meme motifs and collage density, it is Galaxy Print, not this.
- **Glitchcore:** corruption-first (datamosh, slice-shift, channel split) with acid/dark cyber palettes and edgy mood. Maximalism keeps assets intact and cheerful; glitch marks are at most garnish.
- **Internet Awesomesauce:** the original-era (c. 2009–2012) sincere rainbow-unicorn-glitter scrapbook. Maximalism is the 2020s revival: retrospective meme stacking (rage comics, MLG, Advice Animals), higher density, self-aware cringe defiance.
- **MLG:** gaming-HUD grammar — hitmarkers, scope circles, noscope text, dubstep visualizer energy, deep-fried contrast. Maximalism may borrow MLG stickers but centers rainbow collage and rage faces; pure MLG reads darker and gamer-centric.
- **Scenecore:** the 2020s TikTok revival of Scene as fashion subculture — fashion portraits, selfies, music-scene signifiers, comparatively cleaner frames. Maximalism borrows the wardrobe but subordinates characters to meme collage; a fashion portrait without meme stickers is Scenecore, not this.
