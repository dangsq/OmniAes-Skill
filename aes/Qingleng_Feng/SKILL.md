# Qingleng Feng (清冷风, "clear-cold style")

## Overview

Qingleng Feng is a Chinese character aesthetic born from Wuxia and Xianxia storytelling: the aloof, quiet figure with pale delicate features, cool-toned ethereal costume, and a restrained, unapproachable, otherworldly aura. It began as a literary character trope — the reserved "Ice Queen" subtype, smart, independent, respectfully distant, and usually masterful in martial arts or extraordinary power — and was fixed into a visual language by the wave of 21st-century screen adaptations of Wuxia and Xianxia novels, comics and web fiction. In the 2010s the name broadened into a descriptive fashion label that rejects ornate decoration in favour of refined elegance marked by restraint, merging with Minimalism and Wabi-Sabi without ever becoming a standalone pure-design aesthetic: it always carries a person, a story and a weather.

The style's emotional core is *cold clarity*: white fabric as a moral symbol, empty air as composition, and withheld emotion as drama. A white robe without the aloof bearing reads as generic costume; the bearing without the cool palette reads as ordinary melancholy. Both halves must be present.

## Visual Identity

### Palette rule

Every hue sits in a cool, desaturated band. Value is high on the garments (whites, pale blues), mid on backgrounds (mist, grey foliage, stone), and the only deep value is ink black used structurally — hair, belts, collar edges, an outer robe — never as a dominant field. Warmth is permitted only as a faint cream or beige undertone in fabric, never as a light source. Saturation stays under roughly a quarter strength everywhere except one tolerated note: a soft matte crimson lip. A single clearer muted blue (a student uniform's sky-blue, a river-jade sleeve) may act as the deepest cool note; warm gold, if it appears at all, is a peripheral whisper in an illustrated backdrop, never on the body.

### Signature motifs, ranked by textual prominence

1. **Weather and emptiness (core, text-defined).** Falling snow, moonlit night, morning mist, overcast rain; quiet, empty scenes with no one around. The atmosphere is a motif in its own right — an unpeopled snowed-in courtyard or mist-laden wood is a valid Qingleng image.
2. **Costume grammar (core, text-defined).** White and pale blue; draping quality with clean lines; no elaborate lace, no prints; high collars; hanfu cross-collar robes; matte drapable cotton and linen; minimalist commuter wear in modern settings (school uniform, trench coat); ornament limited to a plain jade piece or slim silver bracelet. Winter adds a fur collar trim on cloak or robe edge.
3. **Settings (text-defined).** Ancient: the study (desk, paper, inkstone, lattice window), the woods (bamboo, pine, mist). Modern: library, streetside, riverside, the quiet corner of a cold café.
4. **Bearing and face (text-defined).** Aloof quiet mannerisms; pale, delicate, matte porcelain skin; fine straight brows; soft crimson lip; gaze downcast or directed past the viewer; upright, contained posture; composure even when armed.
5. **Props and hair (image-consistent, supporting the textual martial/scholarly character).** The straight sword (jian) held low or sheathed at the hip; the guqin zither; a white oil-paper umbrella in snow or rain; a single white flower (lily, lotus) held or potted; scrolls and books. Hair: long, straight, ink-black, half-bound with a simple silver hair crown (guan) or a plain ribbon/headband, loose strands framing the face. An occasional silver filigree half-mask appears on masked wanderer variants.
6. **Optional graphic layer (poster evidence).** Faint ink-wash washes, drifting petal or snow specks, and thin calligraphy columns behind the figure in promotional-poster treatments.

### Materials and textures

Matte above all: crinkled cotton-linen, washed gauze, low-sheen silk that absorbs light rather than reflecting it. Fur trim at collar and cuff in cold-weather variants. Brushed, non-glittering silver; plain jade with a waxy depth; paper — umbrella skin, book pages, rice paper. Backgrounds are soft-focus organic blur (foliage, snowfall, mist) or flat ink-dark grounds, never crisp clutter.

### Recurring subjects

A solitary figure, female or male: the cultivator, swordswoman, scholar, exiled strategist; in modern dress, the student, librarian, quiet commuter. Groups, when they occur, are sparse and non-interacting — two figures kept apart by distance and gaze. The figure may also be absent entirely, leaving only the cold setting.

### Composition and lighting tendencies

Vertical portrait bias, head-and-shoulders to three-quarter length, with full-body figures reserved for weather scenes. The face turns three-quarter or profile; the eyes leave the frame. Negative space is structural: pale void above the shoulder, mist swallowing the lower third. The only strong diagonals are carried by objects — a sword blade, an umbrella shaft, a ribbon in wind — while the body stays vertical and still. Two lighting modes are canonical, both cool in temperature and low in contrast: (a) **high-key diffuse** — shadowless overcast or mist light, white robe against pale ground, edges nearly dissolving; (b) **low-key moonlit** — a white figure isolated on a dark ground of night foliage or ink-black space, lit by soft cool frontal or rim light like moonlight. Interiors use dim, even window light. Hard shadows and warm key light do not occur.

## Mood & Values

Calmness and detachment are the declared values. The style prizes restraint as beauty: emotion implied by a lowered eyelid, not expressed; strength implied by a sheathed sword, not swung. Whiteness reads as purity and self-sufficiency; emptiness reads as dignity rather than loneliness, though a quiet melancholy is welcome. The Wabi-Sabi strand adds acceptance of the imperfect and transient — a falling petal, thin snow melting on a sleeve — without ever becoming rustic warmth. Above all the aura is *unapproachable but respectful*: cold clarity, not cruelty.

## Era & Origins

Rooted in Chinese literature's long line of reserved figures, the visual code was fixed when Xiaolongnü of *The Return of the Condor Heroes* tied white fashion to this personality, and consolidated in the 2000s–2010s by Wuxia/Xianxia film, drama, MV and animation adaptations. The 2010s turned the name into a fashion descriptor for restraint-led elegance merged with Minimalism and Wabi-Sabi. Post-2015, mass use of white costumes in Xianxia drama diluted the symbol — a caution for generation: the palette alone is not the style; the aloof character and cold atmosphere must carry it. The aesthetic reacts against ornate, saturated, busy costume-drama opulence: gold brocade, phoenix crowns, crowded court scenes.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Build the atmosphere first (snow, mist, moon, rain, emptiness), then place one composed figure inside it — or leave the scene empty.
2. Keep the palette inside the cool desaturated band; let white dominate and ink black structure.
3. Dress in matte, drapable, clean-lined garments: high collar, cross-collar hanfu, or minimalist commuter wear; ornament budget of one or two plain pieces (jade, slim silver, simple hair crown).
4. Light diffusely and coolly: shadowless overcast high-key, or moonlit isolation on a dark ground. No hard shadows, no warm keys.
5. Withhold emotion: downcast or distant gaze, still hands, upright posture; composure even when armed.
6. Reserve saturation for the lip; reserve diagonals for props; reserve clutter for nowhere.
7. In poster treatments, an ink-wash wash or thin calligraphy layer may float behind the figure.

**Variation axes.**
- *Subject:* swordswoman or cultivator, scholar at a desk, male strategist in a fur-trimmed cloak under an umbrella, masked wanderer, modern student in uniform, librarian in a trench coat, a potted white lily on a riverside wall — or no person at all, only a snowed study window or misty bamboo grove.
- *Composition:* vertical bust portrait, three-quarter with sword diagonal, full body small in vast mist, profile against dark foliage, back view walking into snow, seated at guqin or library desk.
- *Lighting:* overcast shadowless daylight, misty dawn, cool moonlight on dark ground, dim even window light in a café corner.
- *Color handling:* white-dominant with ice blue; grey-dominant with cream; beige-and-ink Wabi-Sabi leaning; dark-ground variant with a luminous white figure; one deeper muted blue garment as the coolest note.
- *Mood:* serene stillness → quiet melancholy → cold resolve.
- *Medium:* photographic drama-still grade, promotional poster with ink graphic layer, painterly digital illustration, flat 2D animation.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image. A modern librarian shelving books in misty window light, in a cream linen trench with a slim silver bracelet, is fully Qingleng despite matching no historical reference.

**Avoid.**
- Warm saturated color: vermilion, gold brocade, orange lantern glow, amber candlelight as key, golden-hour sun.
- Ornate decoration: phoenix crowns, rank badges, gemstone hairpins, layered pearl strands, cloisonné, elaborate lace, floral prints, dense embroidery.
- Glossy or flashy materials: satin shine, sequins, patent leather, metallic lamé.
- Hard lighting: noon sun, warm rim light, high-contrast studio strobe, neon.
- Crowds, festivals, markets, busy signage, cluttered interiors or desks.
- Hot expression and motion: laughing, shouting, weeping, flirtatious poses, mid-air action with motion blur and flying debris.
- Revealing cuts: bare shoulders, low necklines, slit-heavy silhouettes; the collar stays high.
- Heavy makeup: smoky eyes, glitter, draped blush, glossy lips.
- Cozy-cute props: plush toys, candy pastels, heart motifs, kawaii stickers.
- Rustic warm domesticity: terracotta, rattan, oat-toned bouclé, dried pampas, honey wood.
- Generic white-robed Xianxia emptiness: the costume without the aloof bearing and cold atmosphere is a diluted copy, not the style.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Vs Cleancore:** Cleancore is cozy modern hygiene — soap suds, white bedding, candy-soft pastel pink and mint, glossy freshness, domestic comfort. Qingleng shares whiteness but is cold, aloof and narrative: matte hanfu or commuter wear, snow and mist, sword or scroll, detachment instead of comfort; its blues are greyed, never candy.
- **Vs Ethereal:** Ethereal chases luminous glow — backlight halos, translucent chiffon, dewy skin, romantic rapture, often warm or iridescent light. Qingleng is matte and opaque, shadowless cool or moonlit dark, emotion withheld, clean tailored draping rather than floating shimmer.
- **Vs Minimalism:** Minimalism removes narrative: geometry, concrete, glass, steel, temperature-neutral emptiness, often object or architecture studies. Qingleng keeps a literary character, historical or refined-modern dress, weather and ink-wash softness; its emptiness is atmospheric, not geometric.
- **Vs Normcore:** Normcore is deliberately average Western casual — faded jeans, grey tees, sneakers, ironic mediocrity in mid-saturation denim. Qingleng's modern mode is poised commuter minimalism (crisp uniform, trench, high collar) in a cool pale palette with an unapproachable bearing; never sloppy, never generic.
- **Vs Sad Beige:** Sad Beige lives in warm greige domesticity — taupe nurseries, bouclé, honey wood, dried grasses, soft melancholy of home. Qingleng's beige is only a faint undertone inside a cool white-blue scheme, and swaps the domestic cocoon for snow, mist, weapons and books: cold elegance, not warm sadness.
