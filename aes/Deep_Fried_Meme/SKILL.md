# Deep Fried Meme

Deep Fried Meme (also "deepfried," "nuked memes") is a meme aesthetic and format from the mid-to-late 2010s built on the intentional destruction of image quality: filters, saturation and compression stacked until the picture turns crunchy, high-contrast and visually abrasive. It mimics generation loss — the decay of a file screenshot and reposted dozens of times — and weaponizes that decay as humor and as camouflage, producing images that read like a "system seizure" or a CAPTCHA hostile to content-moderation scanners. Originally a vehicle for surreal joke formats, it later fed the data-degradation visuals of 2020s horror-adjacent aesthetics such as Goreweb and Haunted Mound.

## Visual Identity

### Palette rule

The palette is not a fixed hue list but a treatment: whatever enters the frame leaves it maximally saturated, posterized into hard bands, and bleeding at the chroma edges. Muted tones, pastel mixes and smooth photographic gradients do not survive the fry. The documented core is **fried yellow, saturated red, laser blue**, with a witnessed extended range of hot magenta, acid chartreuse and flare-white:

| Role | Hex range | Behavior in the fry |
|---|---|---|
| Fried yellow / batter | #FFD400–#FF9A00 | Skin tones and midtones collapse here; the "battered" flesh look |
| Saturated red | #FF0000–#B00000 | Background washes, overheated faces, the 🅱️ box |
| Laser blue / cyan | #0000FF–#00E5FF | Eye beams, chroma bleed, cold counterpoint to the yellows |
| Hot magenta / pink | #FF00FF–#FF4FA0 | Solarized flesh and poster bands in acid variants |
| Acid chartreuse | #BFFF00–#7CFC00 | Background field in solarized / inverted-leaning variants |
| Flare white | #FFFFFF | Lens-flare cores, caption outer glow, blown highlights |
| Crushed black | #000000–#1A0000 | Contrast floor, hard outlines, shadow clots |

Any hue is admissible if it arrives oversaturated and banded; a red-yellow "thermal" fry, a green-magenta solarization, and a blue-pink wash are all correct members of the same style.

### Signature motifs (ranked by textual prominence)

1. **Compression decay** — excessive JPEG artifacting: macroblock smears, ringing halos around edges, grain and noise filters that make the image look cursed and half-illegible. This is the style's ground texture.
2. **The fried filter** — extreme HDR sharpening plus saturation pushed past clipping; edges get crunchy sharpening halos, highlights blow out.
3. **Lens flare eyes** — glowing points or four-point starbursts placed over the eyes (classically red; orange, pink and white cores also occur), sometimes emitting diagonal laser streaks across the frame, signaling aggression or power.
4. **The 🅱️ emoji** — the red-square B used as a consonant substitute inside words and as a corner sticker; a meaningless surrealist glyph appropriated from censorship culture.
5. **Distorted text** — captions warped, bulged, arched or set in deliberately unreadable fonts; typically white or neon with an outer glow halo, often laid diagonally or in curved bands rather than straight lines.
6. **😂 and 👌 emoji stickers** — pasted PNG-scale emojis floating beside the subject or in corners, formalized in the subreddit era.
7. **Meme caption grammar** — a setup line and punchline ("When your… / To get…"), top-text/bottom-text blocks, or a single shouted phrase; the joke text stays just legible enough through the distortion.
8. **Low-poly and pop-culture subjects** — early-3D cartoon characters, video-game figures, animal mascots, celebrity and stock portraits, all re-fried; occasionally parody packaging (VHS sleeves, game-box covers with rating logos) as the compositional frame.
9. **Radial / zoom blur** — occasional motion smear pulling toward the subject, adding to the sense of a seized frame.

### Materials and textures

Purely digital, purely abused: recompressed JPEGs, screenshot-of-screenshot stacks, filter chains (saturation → sharpen → noise → flare), glow layers, emoji PNGs. The texture vocabulary is macroblocks, chroma bleed, posterization bands, sharpening ringing, sparkle starbursts and soft outer-glow halos around text and flares. There is no paper, film, canvas or analog scan texture here; even grain reads as digital noise, not film grain.

### Recurring subjects

Anthropomorphic cartoon animals and game characters rendered in crude or dated 3D; human portraits pushed to thermal-mask extremes; mundane scenarios (church, reading time, driver's licenses) escalated absurdly by the caption; emojis acting as cast members; boxed-product and cover-art parodies. The subject is always a vehicle for the joke line and the fry, never a portrait study.

### Composition and lighting

No coherent natural light source exists. Illumination is emissive: flare cores, glow outlines, saturation bloom, blown highlights against crushed blacks. Composition tends to a centered or full-bleed subject on a flat saturated wash or noise field, with text as a diagonal banner, arched band or top/bottom block, and emoji stickers orbiting the subject. Crops follow social-feed squares and portraits. Depth, when present, is fake — blur and glow layers, not optics.

## Mood & Values

Irony, noise, absurdity, digital decay, anti-censorship. The style values loudness over polish ("loud equals funny"), illegibility as resistance to advertiser-friendly sanitization, and the cursed over the beautiful. It is a sneer at glossy corporate HD: the worse the file looks, the more sincerely it speaks. In its 2020s afterlife the same fry carries deranged, anti-society dread instead of punchlines.

## Era & Origins

Term coined on Tumblr on March 24, 2015 by user paparoachscarsmp3; the visual language spread through Black Twitter in mid-2015 via early compression-satire formats (some of which, per the style's documented criticism, normalized slur usage — a history a generator should not reproduce), and was formalized in October 2015 by r/DeepFriedMemes, which fixed the glowing red eyes, 🅱️ and 😂👌 vocabulary. The 2016–2019 "Band Kid" era tied it to dank-meme Reddit culture and template repetition; the 2020s saw artists such as Sematary and the Goreweb community repurpose the fry for horror atmosphere. Throughout, it reacts against the sanitized high-definition feed of corporate social media.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Degrade last, degrade hard: build or source the image, then run the fry chain — saturation to clipping, HDR over-sharpening, noise, posterization, JPEG macroblock artifacts — until edges ring and blocks smear.
2. Melt flesh to batter: skin and warm midtones land in fried yellow/orange; let backgrounds crush to red, acid green or a thermal wash.
3. Ignite the eyes: flare cores or starbursts over the eyes (or the eye-like center of the subject), optionally with diagonal laser streaks.
4. Treat text as artwork: warp, bulge or arch the caption, give it an outer glow, place it diagonally or in bands; keep the joke barely readable.
5. Collage the emoji layer: 🅱️ inside words or in a corner, 😂/ stickers floating at the subject's edge.
6. Light only from emission: flares, glows, blown highlights; no sun, no softbox, no ambient logic.
7. Keep the joke structure: a setup/punchline or shouted phrase is part of the composition, even in horror-leaning variants where the "joke" is dread.

**Variation axes.** Subject: any cartoon, game character, animal, human portrait, object or packaging parody. Composition: centered portrait, diagonal action smear, sticker collage, box-cover parody. Color handling: red-yellow thermal, green-magenta solarization, blue-pink wash, or any oversaturated banding scheme. Lighting: flare-driven, with background wash hue free. Fry intensity: mild crunch with readable photo underneath, up to total posterization where the subject is a color-map ghost. Mood: loud comedy through cursed horror-adjacent. **A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.**

**Avoid.**
- Clean HD rendering, smooth gradients, natural skin tones, accurate white balance.
- Pastel or desaturated palettes; matte film grain or analog scan texture standing in for JPEG decay.
- Straight, kerned, unglowed typography; elegant serif or minimalist caption bars.
- Photorealistic lighting: golden hour, studio softbox, coherent shadows.
- The unfried standard meme format (crisp image + plain Impact text) — that is a meme, not a deep fry.
- Pure analog glitch (VHS tracking lines, scanlines) as the sole texture; the fry is compression decay, even when a VHS box is merely the subject.
- Generous negative space, quiet minimalism, tasteful color grading.
- Slur-based or hate-harassment caption formats from the style's early history.

## Discrimination Cues

The wiki names the neighbors without describing them, so these cues contrast the fry's core practice against each neighbor's known center of gravity.

- **Arabfunny** also runs loud and saturated, but leans on ornate Photoshop collage, glitter, decorative gradients and bilingual lettering with comparatively clean edges; it lacks the macroblock crunch, flare eyes and 🅱️ substitution that define the fry.
- **Haunted Mound** inherits the fry but drops the punchline and the emoji sticker layer, substituting graveyard-country horror iconography and deranged dread; a captioned joke with 😂👌 stickers points back to Deep Fried.
- **Keller Synth** (thin evidence in the source) centers on analog synthesizer hardware and smooth, warm, cleanly lit product imagery; the fry never presents clean hardware — if the synth photo is crisp, it is not fried.
- **Meme Rap** uses trap-album iconography and cartoon grotesques but usually in crisp digital illustration or clean photo composites; the fry is what happens when that cover is then nuked with saturation, sharpening and JPEG decay.
- **Surreal Meme** shares the absurd ontology but typically keeps image fidelity intact — smooth neutral 3D heads, dreamlike spaces, deadpan muted palettes; Deep Fried is the degradation layer on top, and without crunch, flares and 🅱️, an absurd image is Surreal Meme, not fried.
