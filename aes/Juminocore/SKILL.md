# Juminocore

Juminocore is an internet aesthetic of collected cuteness: the artful, heavily staged display of small Japanese kawaii goods — capsule toys, blind-box figures, character plush, food and animal miniatures — arranged on shelves, desks, trays, bags and retro handheld consoles. It emerged around 2024 on Pinterest and TikTok and was named accidentally after the TikTok user and small-business owner JuminoJewels. Crucially, it is a Western gaze on Japanese childhood objects: it borrows Heisei-era (1989–2019) goods but restyles them through a muted, coordinated, social-media-native lens of "cute clutter" as interior design. The name remains contested by collectors who see it as a diluted offshoot of the authentic Japanese Heisei Retro movement, and that tension is part of the style's identity: Juminocore is the curated, color-filtered interpretation, not the lived-in original.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette

The palette rule is the style's spine: a subdued, desaturated quartet of **green, white, silver and blue** carries roughly four-fifths of every frame, while **beige, strawberry red and orange** appear only as small accents — a strawberry charm, a goldfish figure, a star sticker, a peach-shaped eraser. Nothing is neon; nothing is deeply saturated; whites lean warm cream rather than clinical; greens read as sage, pistachio, mint and jelly-green rather than grass or emerald; blues stay powdery and periwinkle; silvers come from pearl-grey device shells and metallic charm hardware.

### Signature motifs and objects (ranked by how centrally the style documents them)

1. **Kawaii trinkets and gacha toys** — gachapon capsule figures, blind-box minis, eraser-shaped sweets, tiny animal and food miniatures (goldfish, mushrooms, peaches, strawberries, stars). The defining object class.
2. **Character goods** — Sanrio (Hello Kitty, Keroppi the frog, Usahana, Hanamaruobake), San-X's Rilakkuma bear, Sonny Angel dolls, Peanuts/Snoopy, Pop Mart figures; round faces, dot eyes, animals in costumes (a bear in a cat hood is quintessential).
3. **Retro handheld technology, decorated** — Nintendo DS / 3DS clamshells and Tamagotchi shells plastered with stickers, screens showing character wallpapers, acrylic charms and bead straps dangling from hinge loops and strap holes; silver flip-style devices with clover or strawberry charms qualify.
4. **Phone and bag charms** — the lineage of the 2023 "Birkin-ified" bag trend: purses and cases hung with maximalist keychain clusters; sticker-covered phone cases in sage or clear shells.
5. **Curated clutter displays** — shelves, desks, trays, bins and tiered stands packed edge-to-edge with the above; whole room corners where curtains, bedding and plush piles share the palette.
6. **Plush and soft goods** — vinyl plush bears, pastel bun-shaped pillows, gingham and linen cloth used as display grounds.
7. **Media imagery as surface pattern** — Ghibli (Totoro, Kiki), Yotsuba&!, Kirby and Toro Inoue appearing on screens, stickers and collage paper rather than as poster art.
8. **Scrapbook and doodle overlay layer** (presentation device, strongly evidenced in the image record) — hand-drawn white stars and sparkles in frame corners, zine-style vertical layouts with text blocks, arrows and taped-down photo inserts, sticker-sheet collages of cut-out objects on white.

### Materials and textures

Glossy acrylic and plastic dominate: charm surfaces with small specular highlights, translucent jelly silicone (umbrellas, squishies, bead-filled bottles), matte vinyl figure skin, soft plush pile, pearl-finish device shells, paper stickers and washi tape, cotton gingham and wrinkled linen backdrops, metallic ball-chain and bead straps. The texture story is *clean synthetic softness* — nothing rusty, woven-rough, ceramic-antique or weathered.

### Recurring subjects

Collections themselves, not people: flat-lay scatters of trinkets on white cloth; an open clamshell handheld centered with charms hanging symmetrically from its hinges; a tray or bin overflowing with figures; a shelf wall of plush; a charm-laden bag or phone case; a scrapbook collage page. Human presence is limited to hands holding a device or charm, or tiny photo-strip portraits embedded in collages.

### Composition and lighting

Composition is maximalist but coordinated: the frame fills with many small handheld-scale objects, grouped in color clusters (a green pile, a pink row), with white cloth or paper as the only breathing room. Angles favor top-down flat lay or a slightly elevated three-quarter view; shelf and room shots are frontal. Density is constant; scale never exceeds a plush bear or a handheld console. Lighting is bright, diffused natural daylight — window-side, softly overexposed, low contrast, shadows faint or absent, matte highlights on glossy plastic. No flash hardness, no colored gels, no night.

## Mood & Values

Nostalgia at one remove: a tender, borrowed longing for a childhood mediated by Japanese toy culture, expressed through collecting as identity. Values are **collecting, curated clutter, youthfulness and positivity** — abundance that feels cared-for rather than hoarded, playfulness without irony, gentleness without melancholy. The emotional register is a quiet "look at my little treasures" pride: every object placed with affection, every charm dangling like a medal.

## Era & Origins

Coined in early 2024 on TikTok, grown on Pinterest; groundwork laid by the 2023 trend of maximalist keychain-decorated bags, which created a market for Japanese gachapon and 2000s blind-box stock. The objects themselves are Heisei-era (1989–2019); the aesthetic is a 2020s Western reinterpretation of them, deliberately distinct from Japan's own Heisei Retro movement. Recognizing Juminocore means recognizing that filter: same toys, softer palette, styled for a feed.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Begin from a collection, not a scene: populate the frame with many small kawaii objects before deciding anything else.
2. Enforce the palette rule: muted green/white/silver/blue ground, red-orange-beige only as accents, saturation capped at pastel-to-medium.
3. Any technology shown must be retro-handheld-shaped and decorated — stickers on the shell, charms on the strap loop, character wallpaper on screen.
4. Light everything with bright, diffused daylight; keep contrast low and highlights soft.
5. Clutter is curated: coordinate colors into clusters and rows; the mess is styled, never accidental.
6. Keep scale handheld; the largest tolerated object is a plush bear or a clamshell console.
7. Use the character language: round faces, dot eyes, frogs, bears, rabbits, cats, stars, strawberries, goldfish, mushrooms — as toys and prints, not as living creatures.
8. Optionally add the overlay layer: hand-drawn star doodles, washi-tape borders, zine text blocks.

**Variation axes**

- *Subject*: flat-lay trinket scatter; decorated handheld, flip phone or phone case; charm-laden bag; packed shelf, desk or room corner; tray/bin of figures; sticker-sheet collage; vertical scrapbook zine page; even a translucent pastel prop (jelly umbrella, bead-filled bottle) held inside the trinket world. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.
- *Composition*: top-down scatter, centered clamshell with symmetric charms, frontal shelf wall, grid collage, vertical zine — density and small scale constant across all of them.
- *Lighting*: morning window light through sheer curtain to soft overcast daylight; always bright, always diffused.
- *Color handling*: green-led, blue-led or cream-led frames; accents may be strawberry, peach or sparse; the muted ceiling and high-key value never move.
- *Mood/finish*: clean photograph, doodle-overlaid snapshot, or full scrapbook collage with text and arrows.

**Avoid**

- Neon or highly saturated color, gyaru vibrancy, rainbow candy maximalism at full chroma.
- Dark rooms, night scenes, candlelight, RGB strips, monitor glow as key light.
- Minimalist compositions, single hero product on empty backdrop, generous negative space.
- Undecorated modern technology: sleek black glass slabs, current consoles, bare laptops.
- Grime, rust, sepia aging, grunge textures, weathered wood-and-ceramic antique styling.
- Non-kawaii junk: ticket stubs, packaging, mundane keepsakes, paper ephemera of no character lineage.
- Living botanicals as subject: clover patches, ponds, meadows, real frogs.
- Desktop gaming setups: monitors, mechanical keyboards, desk mats, headset stands.
- Eclectic thrift knick-knacks: porcelain dolls, brass objects, non-Japanese vintage kitsch.
- Harsh direct flash, high-contrast editorial lighting, heavy vignettes.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Heisei Retro** shares the exact same objects but not the filter: Heisei Retro is vivid, saturated and lived-in — Japanese storefronts, stationery, gyaru fashion, personal memory — while Juminocore mutes the palette to sage/cream/silver/blue and stages everything as curated display for a Western feed. Muted + staged = Juminocore; vibrant + contextual = Heisei Retro.
- **Cacareco Girl** also hoards small things, but its clutter is personal, mixed-origin and uncoordinated — ephemera, packaging, any beloved junk — with a warmer, messier feel. Juminocore's clutter is exclusively kawaii character goods and is color-coordinated.
- **Clovercore** overlaps on green and frogs, but its green is botanical: real clovers, ponds, grass, outdoor light. Juminocore's frogs are Keroppi-style vinyl and plush; its green is plastic, plush and paint, indoors under window light.
- **Cozy Gamer** centers a gaming station — monitor, keyboard, ambient lamp glow, desk mat — often at night. Juminocore centers handheld retro tech as a decorated trinket among clutter, in daylight, with no desktop setup framing.
- **Trinketcore** is the broad maximalist display of trinkets from anywhere — thrifted ceramics, dolls, kitsch of any provenance and palette. Juminocore is the narrow Japanese-kawaii subset with its fixed muted quartet palette and Heisei character lineage.

**Quick recognition check:** many small kawaii objects, coordinated muted greens/crems/silvers/blues, glossy charms and plush, decorated retro handheld, bright soft daylight, styled density — if all five hold, it is Juminocore.
