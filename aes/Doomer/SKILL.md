# Doomer (Doomerwave)

Doomer is an internet subculture and mindset — deeply pessimistic, nihilistic, fatalistic — that grew a consistent visual language of its own, sometimes called Doomerwave. The term began in late-2000s "Peak Oil" circles describing people who expected industrial society to collapse under resource depletion; the aesthetic itself crystallized in 2018 on 4chan's /r9k/ board with the Doomer Wojak, a meme stand-in for a young man worn down by depression, alienation and dread about the state of the world. Visually the style feeds on bleak, decaying urban and industrial environments — above all the Brutalist architecture and Soviet-era Khrushchyovka apartment blocks of Eastern Europe — shown at night, in winter, or under overcast skies, filtered into gloom and often degraded to look like old tape. Its emotional register is not anger and not adventure: it is stasis, loneliness, and quiet introspection inside a world that is slowly running down.

## Visual Identity

### Colour palette (the rule, not a swatch copy)

The image is built cold and desaturated first, then allowed one atmospheric cast and at most a pinprick of warmth.

- **Base neutrals:** near-black and navy shadow masses, roughly `#0a0c10` to `#141a24`; concrete and sky greys from `#3c4450` to `#6a7280`, always blue-leaning and never clean white.
- **Atmospheric cast (pick one per image):** green-teal smog or fog (`#46544e`–`#5d6b64`), deep blue dusk (`#1e2c3c`–`#2a3a4a`), or dim purple twilight (`#2b2436`–`#453a55`). Flat overcast grey with no cast at all is equally valid.
- **Snow and haze:** snow reads as dirty blue-grey (`#8f9aa6` and below), never bright white; fog and smog milk out the blacks and lift distant forms into silhouette.
- **Permitted warm accents, small and practical:** amber window light (`#c9924f`), sodium streetlamp glow (`#a89358`), a teal-green porch or mercury lamp (`#4e8f88`), a red signal or tail light (`#7e2a26`). These occupy a few percent of the frame and function as lone signs of life, never as lighting design.

Any image whose dominant hue sits outside this cold family — sunny gold, saturated green, neon magenta, pastel cream — has left the style.

### Signature motifs, ranked by how firmly the text documents them

1. **Bleak, decaying urban and industrial environment** — the core subject: housing estates, factory chimneys with smoke plumes, freight yards, tram and trolleybus lines, power lines, dumpsters, snow-covered vacant lots.
2. **Brutalist architecture and Soviet-era Khrushchyovka panel blocks** — the named architectural signature: slab facades, repetitive window grids, concrete balconies, cramped stairwells and entry porches.
3. **Night, winter, overcast** — the mandated conditions: blue hour, full night with practicals only, falling or settled snow, fog, smog, leaden sky.
4. **Gloom filters in grey, navy, black** — the processing signature: desaturated, low-contrast, shadows crushed or fog-lifted.
5. **Old-media degradation:** VHS tape wear, glitch artifacts, scanlines, grain, compression mush, on-screen displays ("PLAY"), subtitle lines baked into the frame.
6. **The Doomer Wojak as stand-in viewer** — the crude line-drawn figure in black beanie and hoodie, cigarette, unkempt clothes, composited into the desolate scene; an overlay, not a rendered person.
7. **Nihilistic or depressive caption text** — short hopeless phrases, plain lowercase type, sometimes Cyrillic subtitles.
8. **Archetype props:** plain black hoodie or Adidas-style track jacket, blue jeans, worn sneakers, chain-smoking, night-shift loneliness, a worn-down car as refuge.
9. **Musical mood:** post-punk, synthpop, Sovietwave, shoegaze — the visual often feels like a cover for a slowed-and-reverbed edit: melancholic, reverberant, still.

### Materials, textures, medium

Concrete panel seams, stained plaster, frosted snow over dirt and grass stubble, bare tree branches, wet asphalt, chain-link and railing steel, drying laundry on a kitchen line, curtained windows glowing from within. The medium behaves like a cheap camera or a copied tape: soft focus, digital noise in the shadows, lens flare from a single porch lamp, handheld snapshot framing, vertical crops, slight chromatic fringing. Nothing looks professionally lit or retouched; the image should feel found, not produced.

### Recurring subjects and composition

Environment dominates and people are absent or negligible. Typical structures: a one-point perspective down an empty snow-packed street or tram track; an elevated or aerial view over rooftops dissolving into fog; a flat frontal facade whose window grid supplies the only rhythm; a vast empty lot with one small human trace (a playground frame, a drying rack, parked cars, a dumpster); a cramped interior — small kitchen, stove, clothesline — lit by one dim bulb; a lone vehicle idling in snow at dusk. When a human appears, it is a distant silhouette, a figure at a window, or the Wojak overlay. Horizon lines sit high or vanish into haze; symmetry and repetition replace focal drama.

### Lighting

Practical sources only: windows, streetlamps, porch lamps, vehicle lights, signals. Light scatters through fog, snowfall or smog instead of casting crisp shadows. Overcast days are shadowless and flat. A permitted variant is a cold twilight with a thin warm band at the horizon beneath a purple-grey sky. Direct sunlight, hard shadows and golden-hour warmth do not occur.

## Mood & Values

Pessimism, nihilism, alienation, despair, hopelessness, introspection. The mood is resignation rather than melodrama: nothing is screaming, nothing is burning dramatically — the world is simply cold, tired and continuing without you. Loneliness is expressed through empty space and lit windows belonging to other people. In Eastern European usage the same visual grammar has carried anti-war statements, grief for damaged cities rendered in the same muted register. The style romanticizes nothing actively; critics note it risks aestheticizing depression, and that tension — beauty found in hopelessness — is exactly its charge.

## Era & Origins

Term coined 2008 in Peak Oil communities; aesthetic and meme born 2018 on 4chan's /r9k/ with the "-oomer" Wojak series; spread through 4chan, Reddit and YouTube, especially via slowed-and-reverbed post-punk and Sovietwave mixes (Molchat Doma, Kino, Mr. Kitty, Mareux). Its visual reservoir is the post-Soviet housing estate and the post-industrial northern city, filtered through the tonal ancestry of films like *Taxi Driver*, *Stalker*, *La Haine* and *Joker*. It reacts against optimism itself: growth narratives, consumer brightness, curated social-media warmth.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Desaturate first. Build the frame in grey–navy–black, add one cold atmospheric cast (teal fog, blue dusk, dim purple), then place at most one or two tiny warm practicals as life-signals.
2. Let weather and hour do the emotional work: night, blue hour, overcast, fog, smog, snow. No clear skies.
3. Make the environment the protagonist. Human presence is a trace (lit window, laundry, parked car), a distant lone figure, or a crude Wojak overlay — never a styled subject.
4. Degrade the medium: grain, noise, scanlines, soft lens, compression artifacts, optional OSD marker, subtitle line or short nihilistic caption in plain type.
5. Keep it mundane. Entropy, not catastrophe: a stained panel block, an idling trolleybus, a snow-crusted lot. No heroes, no events.
6. Restrain contrast and highlights: milky haze or crushed shadow, never HDR crispness.

**Variation axes (what may change and still read as Doomer)**

- *Subject:* courtyard, stairwell, balcony, kitchen interior, tram stop, freight yard, chimney row, rooftop panorama, smog-bound aerial skyline, worn car at dusk, war-damaged concrete used as sober commentary — any bleak urban, industrial or domestic fragment.
- *Geography:* Eastern Europe is typical, but any post-industrial or northern city qualifies; the foggy high-rise aerial of a non-Soviet metropolis is equally valid.
- *Season and weather:* snow dominates, yet rain, fog, smog and plain night in other seasons all fit.
- *Palette handling:* anywhere inside the cold family — green-teal smog, navy night, purple-pink dusk, flat grey overcast.
- *Medium:* lo-fi photograph, VHS still, meme composite with Wojak, captioned image, rough digital illustration in the same palette.
- *Text and figure:* present or absent independently.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image. A foggy skyline with no Soviet architecture, or a summer night street in rain, can be perfectly Doomer if the palette, lighting, degradation and mood hold.

**Avoid**

- Saturated or warm-dominant colour: golden hour, neon cyberpunk signage storms, pastel dreaminess, vivid green nature.
- Sunny midday, clear blue sky, lush vegetation, blossoms, beach or tropical settings.
- Crowds, parties, smiling or gesturing people, fashion styling, glamour, luxury, branding.
- Crisp professional architectural photography, clean glass towers, minimalist Scandinavian daylight interiors.
- Rust-orange ruin porn and peeling-paint texture close-ups (Industrial Decay's territory).
- Post-apocalyptic narrative props: survivors, weapon caches, zombies, dramatic collapse spectacle.
- Cozy hygge warmth: candlelight, fairy lights, bokeh comfort.
- Aggressive shock iconography, gore, hate symbols, blame-directed rage (Incelcore's territory).
- Hopeful or motivational text, ornate typography, decorative framing.
- High contrast, HDR clarity, pristine new construction, manicured landscaping.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Brutalism** documents concrete architecture with appreciation: daylight, sharp geometric shadow, heroic angles, neutral or admiring tone. Doomer borrows the same buildings but never the admiration — always gloom filter, night or winter, decay, absent people, degraded media. If the concrete looks celebrated, it is Brutalism.
- **Feísmo** celebrates the mundane-ugly with ironic affection: daylight snapshots, vernacular signage, humour, unfiltered colour. Doomer removes irony and warmth, adds the cold cast, the night, and hopeless text. Feísmo laughs with the ugly; Doomer stares past it.
- **Incelcore** shares the Wojak lineage but directs anger outward: resentment, provocation, shock imagery, often harsher or brighter meme graphics. Doomer turns inward — introspection, resignation, environmental gloom, no target and no threat.
- **Industrial Decay** makes decay the subject: abandoned interiors, rust oranges, peeling paint, explored ruins lit for texture. Doomer keeps decay as background condition to life that continues — lit windows, a running trolleybus, laundry drying — under a cold blue-grey cast with a meme/media layer on top.
- **Northerncore** also loves snow and silence, but as landscape romance: clean air, nature, cabins, bright snow sparkle, national sublimity. Doomer snow is urban, grime-stained, framed by panel blocks and streetlamps, and carries despair instead of wonder.

Adjacent notes: Sovietwave shares the Soviet setting but keeps nostalgic, dreamy pastel warmth — Doomer subtracts the nostalgia; Post-Apocalyptic narrates an event with survivors — Doomer shows slow entropy with nobody left to narrate.
