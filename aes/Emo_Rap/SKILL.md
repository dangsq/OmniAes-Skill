# Emo Rap

Emo rap is the visual culture of the mid-2010s SoundCloud rap scene: early-2000s emo and grunge sensibility filtered through late-2010s internet meme culture and high-end streetwear. It is a DIY, phone-made aesthetic — album covers, bedroom selfies, flash snapshots and fan edits rather than staged campaign photography — built around a hybrid identity: hip-hop hardware (chains, dreadlocks, hoodies, snapbacks) worn with emo grief (roses, broken hearts, handwritten confessions, downcast poses). Fans call themselves "sadbois" and "sadgirls," and the imagery trades entirely in that register: melancholy worn openly as identity, memorialized in black cloth, photocopied paper and corrupted pixels.

## Visual Identity

**Color palette (rule, not recipe).** The ground is monochrome: matte black (`#0A0A0A–#161616`) or photocopied paper (xerox cream `#E9E0D0`, scan gray `#8C8C8C`). Line-work, logo type and highlights sit in stark white (`#F2F2F2–#FFFFFF`). Emotional accent is restricted to two families: red (dried oxide `#7A0C0C` through siren `#E00000`, often as a full duotone wash) and purple/magenta (deep violet `#3B0A45` through hot magenta `#C73E8C` and dyed-hair pink `#E86FA4`). Portraits may drift into desaturated gray-lavender midtones; sepia-bleached skin reads as xerox degradation. Yellow appears only as cartoon skin tone in subverted Simpsons edits; cool mint or teal may surface peripherally in surreal checkerboard collage backdrops but never leads the palette. The governing rule: one monochrome ground plus at most two saturated accents. A frame carrying a full rainbow is not this style.

**Signature motifs and objects, ranked by how centrally the culture documents them.**
1. *Streetwear and skate clothing*: Anti Social Social Club, Thrasher, Vlone and FTP hoodies and tees; thrifted oversized flannel shirts; band tees; black snapback caps bearing "SAD BOYS" wordmarks with a frowning-face glyph.
2. *Hair*: brightly dyed pink, purple or blonde; two-tone half-bleached dreadlocks (often split red/blonde); long unkempt Kurt Cobain-length falls.
3. *Metal and skin*: heavy silver Cuban-link chains, wallet chains, facial tattoos rendered as tiny handwritten words, numerals and teardrops.
4. *Angst iconography*: minimalist or withered rose line drawings, broken hearts, skulls, barbed wire — usually white-on-black, single-stroke, sparse.
5. *Subverted nostalgic cartoons*: sorrowful Bart Simpson edits and crying cartoon heads adrift on nebula backgrounds, the widespread "sadboi" emblem.
6. *Handwritten confessional text*: scratched capitals, diary scrawl, scribbled margins, cursive fragments ("it was all a dream"), vertical hand-lettered titles.
7. *Paper ephemera*: torn notebook pages, taped Polaroids, scrapbooked photographs, zine collages of line-drawn roses, wings, hands and crosses.

**Materials and textures.** Photocopy/xerox grain and paper fiber; torn edges and tape marks; VHS glitch, datamosh smears and chromatic fringing; JPEG compression cruft; direct-flash blowout with hard black falloff; matte cotton fleece of hoodies; cold silver of chains; reflective tabletops mirroring a slouched figure; flatbed-scan flatness on collages.

**Recurring subjects.** A lone young figure in a melancholic pose — head cradled in hands, gaze dropped, cigarette smoke curling, half-hearted hand signs; shirtless flash snapshots wearing only chains; hooded or masked avatars (smiley-face balaclavas, fishnets layered under black); object still-lifes of cap, hoodie, chain or rose; graphic album posters with a single centered icon on black; cartoon edits; diary and scrapbook spreads.

**Composition and lighting.** Portrait orientation dominates (album cover, phone frame). Defaults: a centered solitary icon or figure surrounded by heavy negative space; snapshot framing that crops, tilts and flattens; profile or three-quarter busts; text scrawled vertically or diagonally along an edge. Lighting vocabularies: on-camera direct flash in dark rooms and night parties; saturated single-color studio backdrops (especially red); red/black or purple/black duotone washes; flat scan light on paper; dim warm bedroom lamplight; overexposed bleached highlights that erase skin detail into paper white.

## Mood & Values

The style expresses emotional vulnerability, the open expression of sadness, and mental-health awareness — values the culture states plainly. Its images feel numb, confessional, memorial: grief for dead peers hangs over the late-2010s peak, and even party flash snapshots read as elegies. DIY anti-polish is a virtue; degradation signals honesty. Nostalgia (childhood cartoons, handwritten notes) is always subverted into sorrow rather than comfort. Belonging is expressed through collectives and crews — GothBoiClique, Sad Boys, Drain Gang — via shared logos and shared grief.

## Era & Origins

Emergent mid-2010s on SoundCloud from cloud rap, trap and witch house undergrounds (Bones, GothBoiClique, Yung Lean's Sad Boys), mainstreaming 2016–2020 with Lil Peep, XXXTentacion and Juice WRLD. Visually it reacts against mainstream hip-hop's luxury flex and emotional restraint, and against polished pop photography, by inheriting early-2000s emo and grunge dress codes and re-filtering them through meme culture, streetwear drops and phone-flash documentation.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Lay a monochrome ground first — black field or photocopied paper — then admit at most two saturated accents (red and/or purple-magenta).
2. Commit to one lo-fi process per image: flash blowout, xerox grain, glitch/datamosh, duotone wash, or scanned-paper collage. Clean renders break the spell.
3. Always fuse both lineages: a hip-hop signifier (chain, dreads, hoodie, snapback, flash snapshot) plus an emo signifier (rose, broken heart, scrawl, slumped pose). Neither half alone is emo rap.
4. Let type behave as handwriting, scratched capitals or tiny logo text — never as clean headline typography.
5. Keep the emotional register downcast, numb, drugged-hazy or tenderly raw; never triumphant or playful-glossy.
6. Treat portrait/album-cover aspect and centered solitude as defaults, not laws.

**Variation axes.** Subject may be any of: a portrait of any gender or ethnicity; a masked or hooded avatar; an object still-life; a graphic poster or album cover; a cartoon edit; a paper collage; an empty bedroom or night street. Composition may range from centered icon-on-black, to cropped snapshot, to scrapbook spread, to seated full-body slouch. Lighting may be direct flash, dim ambient, flat scan, saturated backdrop or duotone. Color handling may be pure black-and-white line-work, sepia xerox, red/black duotone, magenta nebula (cartoon edits) or gray-lavender studio. Mood may span numbness, mourning, defiant angst, tender confession. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

**Avoid.** Luxury flex (supercars, cash stacks, champagne, gold-as-wealth, boss postures, glossy magazine lighting); clean corporate minimalism (crisp vector logos, sans-serif headline grids, pristine white seamless); unsubverted pastel softness (cottagecore, kawaii cute, blush romance); TikTok e-girl/e-boy gloss (RGB LED strips, ring-light beauty, stamped cheek hearts); pure 2000s scene-emo band staging with no hip-hop element; mainstream trap polish (chrome 3D type, neon cyberpunk grids); upbeat lifestyle positivity (bright daylight smiles, stock-photo energy); more than two competing saturated hues; corpsepaint or black-metal occult excess as the dominant language; vaporwave mint-and-palm pastels leading the frame instead of merely backing a black streetwear focal object.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Sadgirl / Soft Grunge / 2014 Tumblr:** shares melancholy and film grain, but leans feminine-vintage (lace, dried flowers, tea cups, ballet flats) in pastel desaturation; lacks chains, face tattoos, streetwear logos and flash-rap snapshots. Emo rap adds hip-hop hardware and red/black punch.
- **E-Boy:** overlaps in dyed hair and chains, but is TikTok-performative — ring-light gloss, chain belts, ironic posing, clean digital finish. Emo rap is grittier, flash-lit, grief-register, paper-grain degraded, meme-edit heavy.
- **E-Girl:** blush makeup, heart stamps, plaid skirts, LED-lit rooms, cute performativity. Emo rap's fishnets and stripes appear only inside dark grunge layering, without makeup-stamp cuteness or ring-light sheen.
- **Emo (200s/2000s original):** band-merch tees, skinny jeans, studded belts, side-swept fringe, posed band group shots, Myspace angles. Emo rap swaps band tees for streetwear hoodies, adds dreadlocks, face tattoos, trap flash photography and cartoon meme edits; its context is SoundCloud, not the stage.
- **Hip-Hop (mainstream trap):** luxury flex, gold, cars, confident dominance, saturated gloss and chrome type. Emo rap keeps the chain but pairs it with slumped posture, dyed dreads, black fleece, lo-fi degradation and roses/skulls — vulnerability instead of dominance.
- **Near neighbors worth noting:** Trap Metal pushes into chaotic aggression and corpsepaint; Witch House into occult geometry and VHS haze; Drain into glossy Y2K futurism. Emo rap stays anchored in the black hoodie, the handwritten confession and the flash-lit sad face.
