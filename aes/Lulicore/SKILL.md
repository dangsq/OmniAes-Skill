# Lulicore (可露丽风 / Canelé Style)

Lulicore is a dessert-driven fashion aesthetic that emerged in China in 2025 on Rednote (Xiaohongshu). Its name transliterates the Chinese term for "canelé style," after the French pastry with a dark caramelized crust and soft pale custard interior — and that pastry is the entire design system: brown is the crust, pastels are the filling. The trend was coined through a collaboration between Rednote's REDGALA fashion gala, Vogue, and the art-toy brand POP MART (maker of LABUBU), forecast as a merger of Dopamine Dressing's color joy with Maillard Style's brown warmth, and legitimized by its overlap with Pantone's 2025 Colour of the Year, mocha mousse. It spread through hashtag fit checks (hundreds of millions of views) with celebrity Nana Ou-Yang as its iconic figure. Everything downstream — outfits, props, posters, café photo shoots — obeys the crust-and-filling logic.

## Visual Identity

### Color: the crust-and-filling rule
The palette is not a free assortment of browns and pastels; it is a ratio. Brown-family tones occupy the majority of garment or frame surface (roughly two-thirds or more), while one to three creamy pastels appear as bounded accents — a sock, a beret, an inner layer, a bag, a background band, a prop. Pastels are desaturated and confectionery-toned, never electric. Cream and butter sit between the two poles as bridge neutrals.

Rule of thumb: if the browns disappear, it is not Lulicore; if the pastels disappear, it is not Lulicore either. Both must coexist, in that proportion.

### Signature motifs, ranked by how firmly the written record documents them
1. **The canelé itself** — the fluted, ridged, caramel-glazed cylinder — appearing as a real pastry prop, an illustration, a color cue, or a shape echoed in ceramics and packaging. This is the style's namesake and its most canonical object.
2. **Desserts generally**: cream-topped cakes, cupcakes, frosting swirls, dessert-café table settings; sweetness made edible and visible.
3. **Brown-plus-pastel pairing via layering and accessories** — the text's core fashion mechanism: a brown outer garment over a pastel inner one, or brown tops and bottoms lifted by colorful accessories.
4. **Art-toy plush and pixel-playful objects**, inheriting the POP MART / LABUBU collaboration: fuzzy plush characters, pixel-heart cubes, toy-like staging in campaign graphics.
5. **Campaign graphic language** (strongly present in the circulating corpus): horizontally split two-tone color-block backgrounds, a retro cursive script wordmark, mixed Chinese/English caption blocks, and photos set inside rounded or blob-shaped frames.
6. **Styling extensions observed in the fit-check corpus** (not text-canonical but recurrent): slouch leg warmers and ribbon-tied knee socks, berets and bucket hats, pearl strands, lace cuffs and collars, sheer tights, structured mini handbags, loafers and retro sneakers.

### Materials and textures
Glazed and caramelized surfaces (patent leather, lacquered wood, ceramic glaze) answer the canelé crust; soft matte knits, wool cardigans and cotton twill carry the browns; fluffy knit leg warmers, plush toy fur, lace, and pearl strands carry the sweet accents. Recurring environmental textures: warm wood paneling, black-and-white checkered tile floors, cream plaster café interiors, painted metal shutters on street corners.

### Recurring subjects
Young women and men in full-body or three-quarter fit checks; seated, crouching or leaning poses rather than rigid standing; groups of friends in coordinated brown-and-pastel outfits; street snapshots against shutters, tile walls and park landmarks; café interiors with a dessert prop in hand; mirror selfies; and poster-style still lifes combining pastry, plush and wardrobe.

### Composition and lighting
The written record is largely silent on lighting and composition; the corpus converges on a consistent practice. Photography favors direct on-camera flash indoors (slight overexposure, candid snapshot energy) or soft diffuse daylight outdoors; warm interior light in cafés. Framing is straight-on and full-body, or intimately seated at floor level. Graphic layouts center a cursive logotype above a color-block split, with props floating in the field and photography confined to soft-edged inset windows. Neither any single backdrop nor any single framing is mandatory.

## Mood & Values

Sweetness and gentleness are the declared values: Lulicore wants to look edible, kind and a little childlike. It treats softness as strength — pearls, lace and plush toys worn without irony — and frames dressing up as an adventure in brown and bright color, in the words of its campaign manifestos. The mood is cozy optimism with a toy-box playfulness: composed and tender rather than loud, nostalgic rather than futuristic, communal (hashtag fit checks, friend groups) rather than solitary luxury.

## Era & Origins

China, 2025, native to Rednote. Coined by the REDGALA × Vogue × POP MART collaboration and forecast as the synthesis of two preceding Chinese social aesthetics — Dopamine Dressing (saturated joy) and Maillard Style (browned neutrals) — with extra credibility from Pantone's mocha mousse as 2025 Colour of the Year. It is therefore a platform-native, brand-seeded trend: young, commercial, graphic-aware, and inseparable from Chinese social-media caption culture.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**
1. Honor the crust-to-filling ratio: browns dominate surface area; one to three creamy pastels appear as discrete, bounded accents.
2. Make the dessert logic legible: include a canelé or dessert cue, or at minimum let the color story read as crust-plus-filling.
3. Layer deliberately: brown outer over pastel inner, or brown garments plus pastel accessories (socks, hat, bag, inner collar).
4. Keep pastels confectionery-muted and lighting gentle (flash snapshot, soft daylight, warm interior); avoid harsh contrast.
5. If typography or layout is present, use the campaign voice: cursive script wordmark, two-tone color-block field, mixed Chinese/English captions, rounded photo insets.
6. Let props and textures carry sweetness: plush toys, pearls, lace, glazed ceramics, checkered tile, wood panel.

**Variation axes (what may freely change)**
- *Subject*: solo fit check, friend group, street snap, café scene, mirror selfie, still life of pastry and wardrobe, plush-toy staging, poster graphic, amusement-park outing.
- *Composition*: full-body straight-on, seated or crouching at floor level, cropped detail of socks and bag, flat color-block poster, inset photo window.
- *Lighting*: direct flash indoors, soft overcast daylight, warm tungsten café light.
- *Color handling*: which pastel leads (pistachio, strawberry, butter, lavender, blueberry), how many accents (one to three), how much cream bridges; brown depth from chocolate to latte.
- *Mood*: playful and toy-like versus serene and tender.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image. A still life of a canelé on checkered tile with a lavender ribbon and a brown wool cardigan draped nearby is as valid as a full outfit portrait.

**Avoid**
- Neon or electric saturation; rainbow-everywhere color blocking (that is Dopamine Dressing).
- Head-to-toe brown with no pastel accent (that is Maillard Style).
- Stark monochrome minimalism, black-and-white austerity, cold grey luxury.
- Grunge distressing, heavy leather-and-metal aggression, night-city neon noir.
- Seamless gradients, chrome/Y2K futurism, sporty techwear.
- Pastels used as the dominant base with brown reduced to a token detail (inverts the ratio and drifts toward coquette or balletcore).
- Loud logomania; the only sanctioned type voice is the soft retro script wordmark.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Against Dopamine Dressing**: Dopamine Dressing runs on high-saturation clashing brights with no anchoring neutral and an energetic, loud mood. Lulicore always anchors on brown, keeps its brights creamy and portioned, and reads gentle rather than hyperactive. Test: remove the brown base — if the image still holds together as loud rainbow play, it was Dopamine, not Lulicore.
- **Against Maillard Style**: Maillard Style is brown-on-brown-on-brown (caramel, rust, chocolate, toasted orange), autumnal and savory, with no pastel interruption. Lulicore shares the brown base but must puncture it with dessert pastels and sweet props. Test: remove the pastel accents — if nothing is lost, the image is Maillard.
- **Quick identity check**: brown majority + bounded creamy pastel accents + dessert/toy sweetness + gentle light = Lulicore; fail any of the first two and the style collapses into one of its neighbors.
