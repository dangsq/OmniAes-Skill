# Ethereal

Ethereal names the feeling of being extremely delicate and light in a way that seems not of this world. It generalizes the imagery of fairy-tale films — Disney and Barbie princess movies, fantasy cinema completely without irony in their dreamy romanticism — into an aesthetic of wholesome, light, soft happiness: royalty, classical dancers, sweeping ballrooms, colorful gardens, pixie dust, fairies, angels and the beauty of true love. Codified by online aesthetic communities in the 2010s–2020s, it descends visually from animated and live-action fairy-tale cinema, from illustrated editions of Burnett and Montgomery in the Tasha Tudor lineage, and from Romantic-era painterly softness. Its governing values are kindness, love, tranquility, compassion and calmness; its governing technique is light itself, diffused until the world looks remembered rather than observed.

## Visual Identity

### Palette

The canon quartet is **green, pink, white and blue**, held at high value and low-to-medium saturation, with white or cream acting as the atmospheric base rather than a mere accent. Lavender, lilac and champagne gold are permitted supporting notes; iridescent spectral color (prism rainbows, holographic glints on glass or water) appears only as thin refractions, never as flat color areas.

| Role | Range |
|---|---|
| Atmosphere whites / creams | #FFFDF9 – #F6EFE6 |
| Blush, rose quartz, petal pink | #FBDCE6 – #F2B8C6 |
| Pale sky, periwinkle, powder blue | #DCE9F5 – #B9CBE4 |
| Lavender / lilac mist | #E6E0F2 – #C9BFE0 |
| Soft greens: mint, sage, sunlit moss | #E3EEDC – #AFCBA8 |
| Champagne / candle-gold accent | #F0E3C8 – #D9BE8C |

Rule: at least two of the quartet plus white should be present; dark values are allowed only as a small velvety backdrop that makes a luminous subject glow (a glowing figure against deep shadow, for instance). A saturated or black-dominated area of any size breaks the style.

### Light and atmosphere

Light is the primary material. Photography and rendering favor soft focus or outright blur to evoke a heavenly aura: bloom and halation around highlights, backlight and sun flare washing the frame, overexposed white skies, dappled garden light, glittering speculars on water, starburst sparkles, and floating particles — pixie dust, dust motes, snow, pollen. Edges dissolve; highlights bleed; the image reads as dream or memory. A gauze-like haze or tulle-soft overlay is typical, sometimes with faint film grain, never gritty grain.

### Motifs and subjects, ranked by textual prominence

1. **Nature landscapes** — the text's primary visual: ponds, forests, waterfalls, meadows; water carrying swans or koi; flowers in mass (blossom boughs, cosmos, tulips, roses).
2. **Fairy-tale royalty** — princesses and princes, ballgowns, spired castles, sweeping ballrooms.
3. **Classical dancers** — ballerinas, spinning figures, veils and hems caught mid-motion.
4. **Colorful gardens** — manicured, blooming, sunlit, sometimes with statues or follies.
5. **Pixie dust, fairies, angels** — sparkle particles, wings, white gowns, heavenly glow.
6. **True love** — tender couples, reaching hands, quiet intimate gestures.
7. **Clear and refractive objects** — the text's "clear accessories" extended in practice to glass globes, crystal prisms, mirrors, water-filled spheres, diamond jewelry.
8. **Fashion details** — long flowing dresses with lace, white mesh, pleated skirts, white fur, drop earrings, angel patterns.

### Materials and textures

Lace, tulle, chiffon, satin; petals and leaves; still or rippling water; glass, crystal, prism; pale marble and stone; pearl and diamond glint; white fur; in illustrated modes, watercolor washes and painterly blending. Surfaces should look touchable-soft or light-bending, never hard-plastic or matte-industrial.

### Composition

Recurring tendencies, not formulas: a luminous subject centered or slightly off against melting background; figures mid-motion (spinning, dancing, reaching) or in reverie with eyes closed; fabric and hair airborne; framing through flowers, glass or veil; reflective symmetry via water or mirror; vertical aspiration in spires, colonnades and shafts of falling light; generous negative space of white sky or mist; shallow depth of field. Intimate macro is equally valid — an eye wreathed in petals, a hand's shadow holding a flower stem on a sunlit wall — provided glow and softness persist.

## Mood & Values

Serenity, tenderness, safety, reverence. Ethereal embraces the happiest parts of fairy tales with complete sincerity: kindness, love, tranquility, compassion, calmness. There is no wink, no cynicism, no threat; even melancholy here is the sweet ache of longing, not despair. The mood says the world can be gentle, and light can hold you.

## Era & Origins

Named and codified in the 2010s–2020s internet aesthetic culture, but its image bank reaches back through fairy-tale cinema (Snow White 1937, Sleeping Beauty 1959, Cinderella, Beauty and the Beast, Barbie in The Nutcracker and Swan Lake, Maleficent), illustrated children's classics (The Secret Garden, A Little Princess, Anne of Green Gables, The Last Unicorn), and Romantic painterly softness. It reacts against irony, grit and hyper-sharp digital realism by asserting dreamy romanticism without apology.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Expose for heaven: push highlights toward white, let bloom and halation wrap the subject; light should feel like an substance poured over the scene.
2. Dissolve edges: soft focus, gentle motion blur, haze; nothing clinical or razor-sharp across the whole frame.
3. Keep the pastel quartet: pink, green, white, blue dominate; lavender and champagne accent; iridescence only as thin refraction.
4. Make gravity optional: floating veils, drifting dust and sparkle, gliding swans, spinning hems, falling light — weightlessness is the thesis.
5. Be sincere: wholesome fairy-tale emotion, no irony, no satire, no menace.
6. Use transparency: glass, crystal, water, mesh, lace — materials that bend or transmit light.
7. Stage in fairy-tale nature or architecture: ponds, meadows, waterfalls, forests, gardens, ballrooms, castles, colonnades, vanity corners.

**Variation axes**

- *Subject*: unpeopled landscape; still life; single figure; couple; animal (swan, koi, butterfly, bird); macro detail (eye, hand, shadow); architecture; illustrated or animation-style scene.
- *Composition*: centered luminous subject; diagonal reach; vertical spire; horizontal water plane; macro intimacy; wide vista; mirror symmetry.
- *Lighting*: midday bloom; golden dappled garden light; overexposed white sky; backlit silhouette with rim glow; luminous twilight; candle-soft interior; rarely, a velvety dark backdrop carrying a self-glowing figure.
- *Color handling*: pink-led, blue-led, green-led, or near-white monochrome; lavender or champagne accent; optional prismatic glint.
- *Medium*: soft-focus photograph with film haze; watercolor or gouache illustration; painterly oil-like figure study; vintage animation still.
- *Mood register*: serene stillness, joyful spin, tender longing, quiet reverence.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules — luminous softness, pastel quartet, weightlessness, sincerity — not any reference image. A pond with no figure, a prism on satin, or a shadow on a pink wall can be as fully Ethereal as a princess in a ballgown.

**Avoid**

- Saturated neon or primary colors; black-dominated frames; hard direct flash; deep chiaroscuro.
- Irony, satire, horror, gore, decay, grime, cluttered maximalist props.
- Modern urban nightlife: neon signs, traffic, crowds, phones, branding.
- Edge-to-edge clinical sharpness and glossy commercial contrast.
- Heavy gold Baroque/Rococo opulence, formal court stiffness, jewel-tone velvets.
- Sexualized or provocative posing; Ethereal is wholesome.
- Moody desaturated grading, teal-orange grade, grunge grain.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Angelcore** narrows to celestial iconography — wings, halos, clouds, church light, white-and-gold. Ethereal borrows angels as one motif among gardens, ponds, dancers and castles, and keeps the multi-hue pastel quartet; if every element is heaven-iconography with no earthly nature or fairy-tale courtliness, it is Angelcore.
- **Delicate Sweet** is small-scale tender still life — soft trinkets, intimate domestic pastels — without otherworldly glow. Ethereal demands transcendent lightness: bloom, blur, sparkle, the not-of-this-world aura. A cozy pastel object photo without heavenly light is Delicate Sweet.
- **Princesscore** centers princess identity and regalia as fashion statement — gowns, tiaras, courtly dress — and may be crisp and saturated. Ethereal uses royalty as one motif inside an atmosphere of haze and light; if the sharp outfit is the point rather than the dream, it is Princesscore.
- **Royalcore** is opulent court life: Baroque interiors, gold leaf, formal portraiture, weight and ceremony. Ethereal is weightless, pastel, outdoor-leaning and blurred; gold-heavy throne rooms with crisp ornament belong to Royalcore.
- **After Hours** is night-bound: dark streets, neon or lamplight, liminal emptiness, melancholy insomnia. Ethereal's rare darkness is a velvety backdrop for a glowing figure, never neon urban loneliness; if the frame reads as late-night city solitude, it is After Hours.

**Quick recognition check**: heavenly diffused light + soft-focus blur + pastel pink/green/white/blue + weightless fairy-tale sincerity = Ethereal.
