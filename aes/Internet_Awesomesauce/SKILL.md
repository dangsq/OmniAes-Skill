# Internet Awesomesauce

Also known as **Epic Bacon**, Internet Awesomesauce is the visual arm of the late-2000s-to-mid-2010s internet's "random" humor: a chaotic, saturated, surreal collage culture born on Tumblr, YouTube and DeviantArt and named by Alex Edwards of the Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute. Its images paste together internet memes, pop-culture cut-outs, junk food and animals against space backdrops, then treat the whole absurd assembly with over-the-top "epic" poster grandeur — rainbows, explosions, laser beams and chrome lettering applied to subjects as trivial as a waffle. It is a pastiche quoted ironically: 1980s airbrush album art, retro arcade graphics and early Flash-game visuals rerun as jokes by a generation that found sincerity in excess.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette

The palette rule is simple: **maximum saturation, full-spectrum rainbow, and either a void or a candy field behind it.** Rainbow gradients are structural, not decorative — they fill text, trail behind flying subjects, and stripe the frame. Backdrops split into two families: deep-space near-black indigo and violet nebulae, or flat, bright candy fields (cyan, pink, yellow) pierced by radial sunburst rays. Neon emissive accents (laser lime, hot magenta, electric cyan) sit on top as light sources.

| Role | Range |
|---|---|
| Rainbow gradient core | #FF0000 → #FF7F00 → #FFFF00 → #00FF00 → #00FFFF → #0000FF → #FF00FF |
| Space void / nebula | #050014, #1A0033, #2A0049, magenta clouds near #660099 |
| Neon emissive accents | laser lime #39FF14–#66FF00, hot magenta #FF00CC, electric cyan #00FFE0 |
| Candy fields / sunburst | cyan #00CFFF, pink #FF99CC, yellow #FFE600, burst orange #FF5A00 |
| Chrome type | silver-lavender gradient #E6E6FA → #7F7F9F, often over rainbow fill |
| Star sparkle | pure #FFFFFF specks and four-point flares |

Muted, earthy or desaturated color is outside the style entirely; even its "dark" images glow.

### Signature motifs (ranked by how strongly the source text documents them)

1. **Galaxy and space backdrops** — described as near-ubiquitous, the canvas on which everything else is pasted: starfields, nebulae, planets, sparkles.
2. **Rainbows and rainbow gradients** — the style's heaviest color emphasis: arcs, trails, stripe ribbons, gradient text fills.
3. **Augmented animals** — cats first, then unicorns and dogs, humorously modified: laser eyes above all, plus wizard hats, taco shells, wings, crowns, party hats.
4. **Internet meme quotation** — Nyan Cat, the Shooting Stars meme, Doge, rage-comic faces; recognizable meme bodies pasted in as-is.
5. **Junk food as epic protagonist** — pizza, waffles, tacos, bacon, mac and cheese, birthday cake, elevated to heroic status.
6. **Beams, explosions and epic energy** — eye lasers, cone beams, fireball blasts, lens flares as punctuation of "epicness."
7. **Retro game graphics** — pixel art, 8-bit sprites, arcade HUD lines (score/high counters), Flash-game brightness.
8. **Pop-culture cut-outs** — celebrities and cartoon characters clipped from photos and dropped into the chaos.
9. **1980s airbrush / chrome type** — neon-glow lettering, rainbow WordArt bubbles, metallic gradient titles, mock-poster credit lines.

### Materials and textures

This is a **digital photo-collage medium with visible seams**: hard-edged cut-outs with faint selection halos, source photos of mismatched resolution and grain, airbrushed soft spray glows around outlined shapes, glossy chrome and bubble lettering, aliased pixel sprites, flat vector cartoon fills, starfield photography, lens-flare streaks, glitter specks, and gentle JPEG-era compression softness. Mixed resolution and rough masking are honest features, not flaws to correct.

### Composition and lighting

Composition follows the principle of "random" juxtaposition: unrelated elements stacked for surreal humor, with no consistent perspective or scale between layers. Recurring structural tendencies (not templates): a centered emblem with radial sunburst rays and title text above and below (mock poster / mock ad grammar); an all-over horror-vacui collage with stacked planes and scale collisions; a diagonal flight leaving a rainbow trail across the frame; and deadpan layouts (advertisements, birthday cards, campaign posters) invaded by beams and memes. Lighting is **emissive rather than natural**: beams, outer glows, flares, explosions and sunbursts supply light, while each pasted layer keeps the lighting of its source photo — the mismatch is the point. Backdrops are either dark starfield or flat saturated color; there is no ambient, coherent, single-source illumination.

## Mood & Values

Humor, irony, randomness, epicness, online culture. The emotional core is **ironic grandeur**: mundane subjects treated with cathedral-level visual ceremony, and genuine affection disguised as joke. It values maximalist joy over taste, in-group meme literacy over clarity, and DIY democratized tools (paint buckets, outer glow, WordArt) over professional polish. Excess is sincerity; chaos is community.

## Era & Origins

Emergent in the late 2000s, dominant through the mid-2010s, on Tumblr, YouTube and DeviantArt. It reacts against minimalism and "good taste" while recycling older visual vocabularies ironically: 1980s airbrush album art, retro arcade and Flash-game graphics, epic fantasy poster language. Its contemporaries and relatives include Scene, Frutiger Aero, Galaxy Print and Webcore; its tone survives in chaotic-cartoon television lineage (Uncle Grandpa, The Amazing World of Gumball, Unikitty!, Teen Titans Go!) and chiptune-driven viral music. Note honestly: the style's music and TV associations document mood and kinship more than visual rules, and text-cited props like wizard hats and taco shells appear less often than laser eyes and rainbows in surviving imagery.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**

1. Use space as canvas: starfield or nebula backdrop by default; a flat candy field with radial sunburst rays is the licensed alternative.
2. Make rainbow structural: gradient text fill, trail behind a moving subject, arc across the sky, stripe band through the layout.
3. Augment the mundane: give an ordinary animal or food item one absurd upgrade (laser eyes, wings, shell, hat) and one epic context (explosion, beam, cosmos).
4. Collide registers in one frame: photo cut-out + flat cartoon + pixel sprite + novelty typography. At least two, ideally three or four.
5. Treat the trivial mock-heroically: poster titles, chrome lettering, credit lines, HUD counters applied to cats, snacks and memes.
6. Keep the seams: hard cut-out edges, mismatched lighting and scale between layers, mixed resolution.
7. Hold the saturation floor: every hue near maximum chroma; glows and flares instead of shadows.
8. Let emissive elements light the scene: beams, bursts, explosions, sunbursts — never a single coherent key light.

**Variation axes.** Subject matter: any animal (cat, dog, unicorn, panda, tiger, shark), any junk food, any meme-era artifact, celebrity or cartoon cut-out, or everyday object granted epicness. Composition: centered emblem poster ↔ all-over collage ↔ diagonal flight ↔ deadpan layout invaded. Lighting: dark void with neon emitters ↔ bright candy field with sunburst. Color handling: rainbow-dominant on black, neon-on-space, or candy field with rainbow accents — saturation always maximal. Mood: pure absurd joke ↔ mock-heroic grandeur ↔ nostalgic arcade play. Finish: rough JPEG collage ↔ cleaner vector-cartoon brightness ↔ pixel-art-led. **A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.**

**Avoid**

- Muted, earthy, pastel-dusk or desaturated palettes; beige minimalism; generous tasteful negative space.
- Unified photoreal rendering, consistent scale and perspective, seamless compositing, coherent single-source lighting.
- Corporate flat design: limited palette, clean grids, restrained sans-serif typography.
- Post-2015 meme vocabulary as the whole joke (deep-fried compression artifacts, emoji mosaics, template caption boxes) — that belongs to the later Meme Maximalism.
- Sincere epic fantasy or sci-fi without an absurd punchline.
- Handmade craft textures: paper grain, watercolor, film grain, analog photography.
- Single-medium purity (all-photo, all-vector, all-pixel) and dark cinematic teal-orange grading.

## Discrimination Cues

| Neighbor | Tell that separates it |
|---|---|
| 2010s Meme Maximalism | Successor style: template memes, deep-fried artifacts, emoji/vine-era vocabulary, flatter digital-native shitpost layering. Awesomesauce keeps the older toolbox — galaxy canvas, rainbow gradients, airbrush chrome, Nyan-Cat-era motifs — and its irony is mock-heroic poster grammar, not deadpan compression. |
| Hipness Purgatory | Muted mustards, teals and olives; flat matte vector figures; deadpan cool. Awesomesauce is the opposite: maximal saturation, glow, chaos, uncool-on-purpose. |
| Snackwave | Food as tidy product hero on retro neon grids and Memphis patterns, pastel-neon restraint. Awesomesauce's food is one chaotic motif among memes and galaxies, with no grid discipline. |
| Superflat Pop | Art-world flatness: clean vector planes, composed graphic design, limited bright palette. Awesomesauce is rough mixed-resolution collage with glow, depth clashes and visible masking. |
| Avantropop | Glossy surreal 3D/CGI collage with gallery polish and fashion-editorial uncanny. Awesomesauce is lo-fi cut-out, joke-first, paint-bucket energy. |

The fastest positive check: starfield or sunburst backdrop + rainbow gradient + at least one pasted animal or snack granted lasers, wings or royalty + novelty chrome/pixel type + visible collage seams. If all five appear, the image is Internet Awesomesauce regardless of subject.
