# Teethcore

Teethcore (also Toothcore) is a surreal internet aesthetic in which human teeth are removed from the body and handled as strange, collectible, decorative objects. It grew out of the Morute and Gurokawa "morbid cute" scenes on Tumblr in the mid-to-late 2010s, isolating and expanding a single element of those styles — the tooth — into a whole visual language. Its defining operation is juxtaposition: the clinical, visceral, faintly disgusting reality of extracted teeth placed inside sentimental, delicate, even precious contexts (lace, satin, gold lockets, blush-pink paper). Much of it functions as vent art for dental anxiety and body horror, which keeps the mood confessional and tender rather than shock-driven.

## Visual Identity

### Palette

The palette works in two required registers plus two optional ones. The **sentimental ground register** runs from pale lace pink through blush to dusty rose (roughly `#F6D7E0` → `#E9B8C9` → `#C78CA4`), sometimes substituted by warm linen and beige neutrals (`#D9CDBB` → `#BFB09A`). The **biological object register** is ivory, bone and enamel cream (`#F7F2E7` → `#E2D8C3`), never pure optical white: real teeth carry ochre stain (`#C6A163`), tobacco brown (`#8A6B4A`) and grey decay (`#9A8F85`) on roots and crowns. Optional register three is a **stark void ground** — near-black, grungy charcoal (`#191512` → `#2E2622`) — used for specimen-style isolation shots. Optional register four is **small metal accent**: antique gold and brass filigree (`#B99646`, `#C9A227`) or dull silver/steel (`#B9BCC0`) in chains, caps, locket rims and frames. Saturated color is absent; even at the grotesque pole, red appears at most as a desaturated oxblood trace, never as bright blood.

### Signature motifs, ranked by how centrally the style documents them

1. **The loose tooth as isolated object.** The primary motif: extracted teeth presented not as anatomy but as relics, specimens, gems. Molars with twin splayed roots, single-rooted incisors, baby teeth with short resorbed roots, cracked and cavity-pitted crowns.
2. **Curated collections in ritual or symbolic arrangements.** Teeth laid out in outlines — hearts, pentagrams, circles are the canonical shapes — on a flat ground, with generous negative space inside and around the shape.
3. **Teeth as jewelry.** Necklaces and earrings: a tooth banded with a metal cap and hung from a fine chain, teeth as charms and pendants.
4. **Teeth housed in sentimental containers.** Lockets (heart-shaped, satin-lined), music boxes, small boxes and shallow trays, bowls and gilded frames; the container converts a body part into a keepsake.
5. **Tooth Fairy folklore, reinterpreted dark or surreal.** Implied or literal fairy-tale framing: offered teeth, hoarded payments, childhood myth gone uncanny.
6. **Dental-anxiety vent text.** Handwritten confessions ("I'm afraid") in pencil or ink on pink paper, surrounded by sketched teeth — the illustration branch of the style.
7. **Damaged and morbid anatomy.** Decayed, broken, stained teeth as the body-horror pole; the grotesque is carried by caries and calculus, not by blood.
8. **The mouth as staging device (rare, marginal).** Lips biting or holding a tooth-adjacent object (a heart charm, a gem on a tooth). Teethcore is explicitly *not* about mouths or smiles; when a mouth appears it is a prop holder, never a portrait of a smile.

### Materials and textures

Enamel with a soft wet-look specular sheen against matte, chalky root cementum; porous stain lines and decay pits. Against the teeth: crochet and lace doilies, satin and velvet locket linings, matte paper and cardstock, linen or velvet cloth, ornate gilded filigree, fine cable and ball chains, cardboard and wooden specimen trays, pencil and ink line work on paper. The contrast of hard mineral tooth against soft textile and paper is the tactile core of the style.

### Composition and lighting

- **Top-down flat lay:** arrangement centered on a flat matte ground (paper, cloth, lace), shot straight from above, shadowless or with faint soft shadows; the empty ground is compositional, altar-like.
- **Macro still life:** a single tooth or pendant filling the frame, shallow depth of field, one gentle highlight sliding across the enamel.
- **Contained collection:** open box, tray, locket or bowl viewed from above or three-quarters, teeth clustered or loosely gridded inside.
- **Specimen-on-void:** isolated teeth floating on black, evenly lit, catalog-plate feel.
- **Paper illustration:** ink or pencil teeth scattered around small handwritten text.
- Lighting is diffuse and flat — window daylight, softbox, or flattening on-camera flash — and the photographic mode welcomes lo-fi Tumblr-era degradation: slight blur, phone-camera grain, muted contrast, square-ish crops. Nothing is professionally glossy.

## Mood & Values

Uncanniness first: the tooth is familiar and wrong, mine and not-mine. The style values surrealism, a fascination with the grotesque, bodily autonomy (keeping and ornamenting one's own discarded parts), and coping with anxiety by making it an object on pink paper. The emotional dial runs from **delicately creepy** (lace, locket, baby teeth) to **overtly grotesque** (rotted crowns, dark grounds), but almost always stays quiet, intimate and handmade rather than aggressive.

## Era & Origins

Tumblr, mid-to-late 2010s, in niche morbid-cute communities. A direct offshoot of Morute (which blended death, dolls and vintage medical imagery) and cousin to Gurokawa; Teethcore narrowed Morute's breadth to one object and built a language around it. Source material: the folk practice of keeping baby teeth, Tooth Fairy folklore read through a darker lens, and the vent-art culture of anxious self-expression.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Decontextualize: teeth out of mouths, handled as objects, relics, gems.
2. Juxtapose registers: visceral/clinical object inside sentimental/delicate staging.
3. Hold the muted palette: pink + ivory dominant, dark ground only as stark contrast, metal only as small accent.
4. Arrange with intent: symbolic outline or curated container; negative space is part of the composition.
5. Material honesty: enamel gloss versus matte root, stain, wear, resorbed baby roots; no veneer-perfect white in photographic mode.
6. Light soft and flat; lo-fi analog degradation welcome; macro for jewelry.
7. Keep gore low: decay and stain carry the grotesque; blood is essentially absent.
8. If text appears, it is small handwritten anxiety confession on paper.

**Variation axes.** Subject: one tooth, a handful, dozens; baby teeth, molars, damaged crowns; pendant, earring, ring charm; locket, music box, jewelry box, specimen tray, bowl, frame; heart, circle, pentagram, spiral, star or row arrangement; drawn vent illustration; Tooth Fairy implication (a coin, a note, an offering). Ground: pink-dominant, linen-dominant with pink accent, or stark black void. Grotesque dial: delicate-creepy to overtly decayed. Medium: lo-fi photograph or pencil/ink paper illustration. Scale: macro single tooth to tabletop altar of dozens. **A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.** No reference composition is canonical — a teeth spiral on linen under flash, or a drawn tooth chart with a confessional line, is as valid as a heart flat lay on pink.

**Avoid.**
- Smiling mouths, dental-hygiene contexts: toothpaste, brushes, orthodontic charts, bright clinical promo photography.
- Cartoon tooth mascots with faces or limbs; cute dental-education imagery.
- Neon, candy-saturated or vaporwave palettes; glossy commercial 3D rendering.
- Blood pools, splatter, open wounds (this slides into Gurokawa or Meatcore).
- Forest-floor foraging clutter: moss, mushrooms, animal bones, earthy browns and greens (Goblincore).
- Liminal rooms, hazy skies, nostalgic empty spaces with no tooth object (Dreamcore).
- Dolls, graves, vintage medical instrument overload without a tooth protagonist (Morute / Medicalcore).
- Raw meat textures, wet viscera, saturated reds (Meatcore).
- Heaped, uncurated clutter: Teethcore collections are arranged or contained, never randomly strewn across a whole frame.

## Discrimination Cues

| Neighbor | Their center | Teethcore tell |
|---|---|---|
| Morute | Broad morbid-cute: dolls, death, vintage medical, pastel goth | Teethcore has one protagonist — the extracted tooth as object — on pink/ivory grounds; dolls and graves without teeth are Morute |
| Dreamcore | Liminal spaces, surreal skies, nostalgic unreality | Teethcore is tabletop-scale object still life; no vast empty rooms or dream haze |
| Goblincore | Foraged natural clutter: moss, animal bones, mushrooms, earth tones | Teethcore's collections are human teeth, curated into symbols or keepsakes on sentimental pink/white, not woodland trove |
| Gurokawa | Kawaii fashion and character art with blood, bandages, pink-red gore | Teethcore is photographic object still life with little or no blood; no fashion coordinate, no cute character styling |
| Meatcore | Raw flesh, viscera, wet gloss, saturated red | Teethcore is dry, hard, mineral enamel in ivory on pink; abject wetness is absent |

Adjacent notes: Medicalcore overlaps when clinical tools appear, but Teethcore never centers instruments — only the tooth itself. Weirdcore shares the lo-fi degradation but lacks the tooth object entirely; degradation alone is not Teethcore.
