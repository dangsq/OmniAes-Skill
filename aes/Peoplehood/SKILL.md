# Peoplehood

Peoplehood is the aesthetic of everyday kindness made visible: the small, unheroic acts of care between people — feeding, grooming, holding, walking together, sharing a table — elevated into subjects worthy of attention. Popularized on Tumblr around 2021 as a cousin of "slice of life," it grew out of a community habit of binding images into moodboards, compilations, parallels and web-weavings, where a photograph of two hands passing an orange sits beside a line of Wendy Cope, an Ocean Vuong poem, or Laura Gilpin's "The Two-Headed Calf." It inherits the yearning of Dark Academia and Cottagecore and the TikTok-era conviction that romanticizing one's own life is a mental-health practice — but it turns that romanticization outward, toward other people, rather than toward the self or the pasture. The style's documentary impulse has a documented shadow: many beloved candid images were captured without the subjects' consent, so the aesthetic carries an internal tension between tenderness and extraction that a faithful image should resolve on the side of warmth, not voyeurism.

## Visual Identity

**Palette rule.** The wiki names orange, green and beige as the key colours; the sampled images confirm how they distribute. Beige is the ground — oat wool, paper walls, cream curtains, bread crust (#C9BBA0 to #F2E9D8). Green is the mass — olive coats, hedge walls, leafy stems, kitchen plants (#3F5233 to #8FA05C). Orange is the spark, never the field: tangerine peel, marigold or sunflower heads, crosswalk paint, a persimmon sweater (#C65A2E to #F0A33A). Supporting neutrals are urban: asphalt grey, wool brown, transit cream. The painted strand licenses Fauvist non-local colour — cobalt tablecloths, lemon backgrounds, rose-red skin — as flat saturated shapes; this saturation belongs to illustration mode only and should not leak into the photographic register, which stays desaturated with lifted, filmic blacks.

**Materials and textures.** 35mm grain and slight motion blur; wool coats, fur collars, knit beanies, patchwork quilted jackets; leather handbags; ceramic bowls, enamel pots, tile grout; bread crusts, flower stems, sunflower heads; aluminium canes, paper shopping bags; steel conveyor belts and transit poles; in illustration mode, gouache on paper with visible brush edges and unpainted margins.

**Composition tendencies.** The camera is a warm bystander: eye-level, off-center, bodies cropped by the frame edge. Walking pairs are taken from behind; tables from overhead; hands from close across. Faces may be averted, asleep, or omitted entirely — gesture carries the narrative. Figures are embedded in their environment rather than isolated from it: a couple inside the geometry of bus poles, elders inside the stripes of a crossing, cooks inside the clutter of a small kitchen. Crowds read as depth layers, not backdrops. Apartment windows appear as pattern and promise of life behind glass.

**Lighting.** Available light only. North-facing window light through sheer curtains indoors; hard midday sun casting crisp shadows on pavement outdoors; fluorescent green-grey cast in transit halls; a single warm bulb over a kitchen table. Exposure behaves like film: lifted blacks, rolled-off highlights, no strobe, no glamour golden hour.

## Mood & Values

Intimacy in mundane life, love for humanity, togetherness. The style asserts that nothing needs to happen for a moment to matter: soup simmering, hair being braided, a bouquet carried home are sufficient events. Its feeling is tender, unhurried, quietly humorous, reverent toward strangers and elders. Yearning is present but social rather than solitary — the ache is for connection already happening nearby. Underneath sits an ethic: look at people the way a friend would, not the way a surveillance camera or a paparazzo would.

## Era & Origins

Emergent in 2021 on Tumblr, in continuity with the yearning cultures of Dark Academia and Cottagecore and with the TikTok "main character" sentiment that frames romanticization as self-care. It reacts against hustle culture, doomscrolling, and the isolation of pandemic-era life by collecting proof that ordinary people keep touching, feeding and keeping each other company. Its literary canon — Cope, Vuong, Gilpin — supplies the caption voice: plain-spoken poems about oranges, calves with two heads, and small mercies.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Gesture before face: let hands, arms and posture tell the story; faces optional, averted or cropped.
2. Always imply an other: a second cup, a shared pot, a hand reaching into frame — never pure solitude.
3. Set care in mundane venues: transit seats, small kitchens, crosswalks, market stalls, stairwells, windowsills.
4. Include a shared vessel or portion: one pot, one plate of cut fruit, two cups from one kettle.
5. Light as found: window, noon sun, fluorescent, bulb; film exposure character.
6. Palette discipline: beige ground, green mass, orange spark; Fauvist saturation only in illustration mode.
7. Choose a register and stay in it: grainy analog candid, or flat gouache Fauvist illustration.
8. Keep imperfection: tilt, crop, clutter, wear, grain, slight blur.
9. Populate publicly: mix ages, ethnicities and cultural backgrounds in shared space.
10. Frame with participant warmth, at a respectful distance — tenderness, never extraction.

**Variation axes.** Subject matter may be any ordinary act of care across any age, culture or relationship — lovers, elders, friends, parent and child, strangers helping strangers. Composition may range from tight hand crops to wide crowd vistas, overhead tables to rear-view walks. Lighting may range from dim fluorescent to bright noon, always unmotivated by studio equipment. Colour handling may sit anywhere from near-monochrome beige-green film mutedness to full Fauvist flat colour, provided the orange-green-beige logic or its Fauvist extension holds. Mood may slide from quiet melancholy to bright joy, but must remain grounded in specific human contact. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

**Avoid.** Studio strobe, ring light or glossy commercial finish; posed fashion-editorial styling, makeup-forward faces, brand logos; solitary introspection with no trace of another person; rural pastoral costume, prairie dresses and harvest tableaus; vintage kitsch props as the subject (doilies, rotary phones, floral teacups curated for nostalgia); motivational skies, sunsets, lens flares and text overlays; HDR or teal-orange blockbuster grading outside the illustration register; empty, symmetrical architecture with no human trace; sexualized or fitness-coded bodies; wealth signifiers such as designer interiors, champagne or luxury cars; sterile minimalism — clutter and use-wear are required; hidden-camera voyeurism or cold surveillance angles.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Thought Daughter** centers solitary interiority: one figure reading, journaling, thinking in moody stillness. Peoplehood always implies a second person and outward-directed touch; its quiet is shared, not introspective.
- **Art Hoe** centers the artist's self-expression: paint on skin, sketchbooks, gallery walls, saturated creative clutter as identity. Peoplehood's painted strand borrows Fauvist colour but its subject is relationship, not self-authorship; its photographic strand is documentary, never self-portrait.
- **Cottagecore** is rural and pastoral: meadows, farmhouse kitchens, candlelight, prairie dresses. Peoplehood is urban and semi-public — buses, crosswalks, apartment windows, street markets — and its cooking is communal soup on a small stove, not a harvest tableau.
- **Grandmacore** curates vintage domesticity as décor: floral wallpaper, knitting baskets, retro appliances, cream-and-rose palettes. Peoplehood includes elders but in motion through public space, with functional props (canes, shopping bags, bouquets) and urban grey-greens plus orange in the palette.
- **Hopecore** delivers abstract uplift: sunsets, open skies, montage optimism, often captioned. Peoplehood's hope is embodied and specific — fingers braiding hair, a forehead kiss in transit — and keeps its grain, clutter and ordinary weather.

Where the sampled evidence is thin — apartment windows appear chiefly as a textual motif, and the exact hex behaviour of the Fauvist strand is inferred from a small painted subset — the rules above state the documented intent rather than over-fit to few examples.
