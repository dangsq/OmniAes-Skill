# Maillard Style (Maillard Feng)

Maillard Style is a fashion microtrend launched in China in August 2023 by Douyin E-commerce's fashion trend release and popularized overseas through the autumn of 2023–2024. It takes its name from the Maillard reaction — the 1912-described chemical process by which amino acids and sugars brown food under heat, as in a searing steak or a baking loaf — and turns that reaction into a wardrobe system: monochrome outfits and matching accessories assembled from multiple shades of brown at once. It borrows the restraint of Old Money / Quiet Luxury but inflects it with Chinese heritage fabrics and a playful, almost edible richness, and it arose as a direct reaction against the vibrant Dopamine Dressing of summer 2023, partly driven by a slowing economy and a youth preference for timeless, heritage-coded clothing.

## Visual Identity

### Palette: the sear gradient

The palette is not "brown" but a *spectrum* of browns, ordered like the cross-section of a cooked steak — rare interior through seared crust — plus two neutral accents. The governing rule: a frame should hold at least three distinct rungs of this gradient in simultaneous, tonal layering; a single flat beige is not Maillard.

| Gradient rung | Families | Example hexes |
|---|---|---|
| Plate accents | off-white, cream, khaki | #F2EAD9, #B9A87F |
| Light sear | tan, warm yellow-brown, camel | #C99B6A, #C08A3E |
| Medium sear | caramel, chestnut | #A9713C, #8A4B2A |
| Rare interior | rust, terracotta, burgundy, oxblood | #A34F2E, #5E2430 |
| Deep crust | chocolate, espresso | #4A2B1A, #2C1A10 |

Saturation stays medium-low to medium; warmth is constant. Khaki and off-white appear as collars, cuffs, trousers or backgrounds, never as the lead. Near-black (patent belt, tights, boots) is permitted only as a small punctuation inside an otherwise brown chord.

### Signature motifs, ranked by textual prominence

1. **Tonal monochrome brown outfits** — the defining feature: several shades of brown worn together, head to toe, including the accessories.
2. **Leather and fur** — the infobox's key motifs: smooth or patent leather jackets, coats and skirts; shearling, teddy and fur collars, sometimes combined ironically so the outfit looks "delicious, just like steak."
3. **Shade-and-texture experimentation** — deliberate layering of tan, reddish-brown and warm yellow-brown in one silhouette.
4. **Khaki / off-white accents** — the complementary break in the brown chord.
5. **Chinese heritage fabrics** — satin brocade and gambiered canton gauze, the Chinese-old-money element that separates this from Western Quiet Luxury; visually, jacquard/brocade wide-leg trousers and lustrous traditional weaves.
6. **Structured brown leather accessories** — wide corset-style belts, boxy totes and clutches, slouchy knee boots, fedoras and beanies in the same family as the clothes.
7. **The food metaphor itself** — seared steak cross-sections, bread crust, latte and bakery browns; the namesake still life and the logic behind brand tie-ins (brown packaging drops, latte-shade lipstick).
8. **Maillard makeup** — dewy natural base, full natural brows, matte muted-brown / coffee / terracotta eyeshadow with minimal shimmer, low-saturation earthy blush, contour in the blush family, nude-brown to chocolate lips with a blurred upper lip line.

### Materials and textures

Texture contrast carries as much meaning as color: teddy and shearling fur against smooth or patent leather; chunky and Fair Isle knits against satin brocade jacquard; suede, velvet and quilted cloth against crisp off-white cotton. A correct look pairs at least two opposing surfaces — plush with sleek, matte with lacquered.

### Recurring subjects, composition and lighting

Subjects cluster around fashion communication: full-body lookbook figures on seamless warm-off-white or grey studio backdrops; street-style candids against sun-warm urban walls; multi-look collages and moodboard grids (often captioned in Chinese); runway and event shots; food still lifes on dark slate; makeup close-ups. Evidence beyond fashion, beauty and the food metaphor is thin — interiors, landscapes and product-only scenes are undocumented and should be treated as extrapolation. Composition favors the whole silhouette, centered and upright, or the texture close-crop (belt, bag, knit, sear); lighting is soft and diffused in studio, low golden and ambient on street, and moody side-lit against dark grounds for food. The medium reads as digital fashion photography and social-media moodboard, never illustration or film still.

## Mood & Values

Warmth, comfort and appetite: earthy browns that evoke fall and cooked food. Underneath sits a value statement — timelessness and heritage over fast, flashy consumption; quiet luxury with a Chinese accent; restraint as a response to economic anxiety and to Dopamine excess. A thread of irony runs through it: textures combined to look good enough to eat.

## Era & Origins

China, August 2023, via Douyin; spread on Douyin and Xiaohongshu (hashtag views in the billions), then to Western influencers in autumn 2023–24. Named for Louis Camille Maillard's 1912 reaction. Commercial echoes: luxury-bakery co-branded brown packaging, a Shanghai latte-lipstick campaign. It reacts against Dopamine Dressing and aligns with, but distinctively sinicizes, Quiet Luxury.

## Generation Guidance

Principles: build the frame as a brown chord of three or more gradient rungs; clash at least two textures (plush vs sleek); keep every hue warm and every accent in-family; accessorize in the same leather/brown language; light as if in soft studio daylight or low autumn sun; let still lifes lean "appetizing" (sear, crust, latte foam); if a face is shown, use the matte smoky-brown makeup with blurred chocolate lip.

Variation axes: **subject** — outfit lookbook, street candid, runway, accessory or texture close-up, makeup portrait, food still life, collage moodboard; **composition** — centered full-body, walking crop, multi-panel grid, overhead still life; **lighting** — diffused studio, golden street light, dark moody side-light; **color handling** — camel-dominant, burgundy-dominant, cream-accented, or deep-crust dominant, anywhere on the gradient; **mood** — quiet-luxury polish, cozy warmth, or playful edible irony. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

Avoid: Dopamine neons and saturated cool colors; cool pastels, blues, silvers; grayscale or black-dominated frames; a single uniform beige with no value contrast or texture play (that is minimalism, not Maillard); loud logos and non-brown graphic prints; athleisure or beach/summer context; academic or preppy signifiers (crests, tweed blazers, library props) as the subject; rustic harvest or grocery props (pumpkins, leaf piles, paper market bags) as the subject; cool fluorescent or blue-hour lighting; pale Scandinavian wood interiors as the focus.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Grocery Girl Fall** centers on the errand narrative — paper bags, baguettes, coffee cups, market produce — in candid motion; Maillard centers on tonal outfit construction and leather/fur richness, and appears equally in studio and runway contexts with no props at all.
- **Japandi** is interior-led minimalism in pale wood, beige-grey and matte ceramic, sparse and gloss-averse; Maillard is fashion-led, deeper and warmer (through burgundy and chocolate), plush, layered and texture-maximal.
- **Lulicore** is wellness athleisure — leggings, sports bras, matcha, clean-girl brightness; Maillard is outerwear-heavy tailoring in leather, fur and knit, autumnal and low-key even when leggings appear under an oversized sweater.
- **Old Money / Quiet Luxury** spans navy, cream, forest green and tweed with Western preppy codes; Maillard restricts itself to the brown gradient, adds Chinese heritage weaves (satin brocade, gambiered canton gauze) and a foodish playfulness Old Money never permits.
- **Autumn Aesthetic** is a broad seasonal mood — leaves, pumpkins, cozy drinks, landscapes in orange-red; Maillard is a wardrobe system, urban or studio-set, head-to-toe brown including bags and boots, where autumn is the season but the outfit is the subject.
