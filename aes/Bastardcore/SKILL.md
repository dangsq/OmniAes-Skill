# Bastardcore

Also called Badboycore, Feralcore or Mischiefcore, Bastardcore is a chaotic internet aesthetic that crystallized on Tumblr in the late 2010s out of the broader "cursed image" meme. It fuses three inheritances: the low-fidelity, high-flash look of early-2000s compact digital cameras; documentation of youthful petty delinquency; and the aggressive, dark, deliberately offensive humor of novelty graphic T-shirts. Where a generic cursed image merely confuses, Bastardcore intends mischief — its followers describe it as "rebel without a cause," "feral," and "supposed to strike your fight or flight response," while remaining "tastefully hilarious." The shock is always ironic and deadpan: friendly or mundane imagery welded to something unsettling and out of place, presented as if it were ordinary evidence.

## Visual Identity

**Palette and light.** The style's only color law is contrast, not hue: any colors are permitted, organized as a stark opposition between a low-light scene and one or more bright light sources. The dark register is asphalt gray, tar black, muddy puddle brown, night-green foliage, and the degraded domestics of beige vinyl tile, cream plaster, gray shag carpet, motel pink. The bright register is the puncturing source: blown-out on-camera flash white, fire orange and yellow, sodium-vapor amber, headlight glare, fluorescent green-white, candle flame, and the saturated red of marker ink on paper or tile. Typical anchor values run from near-black `#141414`–`#2e2a26` shadows through mud `#4a3b2a` and carpet gray `#8a8a86` to flash-hot `#f2efe8`, with fire `#ff6b1a`–`#ffc24a` and marker red `#c1272d` as recurring accents — but a daylight overcast image with no fire is equally valid if the mundane-is-corrupted logic holds. Unity comes from tonal violence and degradation, never from harmonic palettes.

**Capture quality and medium.** Grainy high-ISO noise, motion blur, soft focus, harsh direct flash with rapid falloff and a hard shadow thrown onto the wall behind the subject, square or vertical phone crops, tilted horizons, limbs cut by the frame edge. The image must read as an accidental snapshot or evidence photo, not a photograph made by a photographer. No grading, no compositing, no clean edges.

**Signature motifs, ranked by how centrally the style documents them.**
1. Poorly lit urban and suburban liminal settings: underpasses, dark streets, parking lots, graffiti-tagged concrete, signs of neglect.
2. Acts of chaotic mischief: people riding or standing on shopping carts, lying on the ground with traffic cones on their heads, mild vandalism in progress or its aftermath.
3. Fire and arson: objects alight (skateboards, pots of spaghetti, cigarette teepees), structures burning while figures watch from a safe bystander distance.
4. Mundane objects in bizarre contexts: a skateboard floating in a muddy puddle, candles and cockroaches arranged like a séance, mannequin hands grafted onto a lizard.
5. Grotesque humor of food and vermin: cockroaches, possums, pizza cut into a pentagram, food on fire or in wrong places.
6. Head-substitutes and masks: skeleton masks, traffic cones, buckets, cardboard boxes with drawn faces — anything that anonymizes the head.
7. Novelty graphic T-shirts and hoodies with offensive, nonsensical or hyper-specific ironic slogans (the style's essential garment, inherited from the "Shirts That Go Hard" vendor trend), plus ripped jeans, combat boots, platform shoes, secondhand accessories.
8. Arresting statements as physical props: handwritten marker notes in notebooks or on tile ("Queen of Trash"), engraved spoons, embroidered patches, official signage defaced or riddled with bullet holes; deadpan declarative voice, including community in-jokes like "zoo wee mama."
9. Badly lit domestic rooms: ceiling fans, rumpled beds, shirtless groups, beige interiors flattened by flash.

**Materials and textures.** Asphalt, wet mud, chain-link, concrete, shag carpet, vinyl tile, cardboard, molded plastic (cones, carts, children's ride-on toys), bare metal (spoons, shopping carts, stove pots), notebook paper, plush fur, cigarette paper, candle wax, grease. Everything is cheap, worn, or stolen from a household.

**Subjects and composition.** Anonymous amateurs — faces hidden by masks, cones, boxes, distance, blur or turned heads; groups of friends caught mid-act; authority figures (cops) confronting absurd behavior. Composition is snapshot-casual: overhead phone flat-lays of table or floor arrangements, bystander-distance street shots, cropped verticals, off-center subjects centered only by where the flash happened to land.

## Mood & Values

The declared core value is shock value. The mood is feral, anti-establishment irony: making the viewer uncomfortable while landing as "tastefully hilarious," triggering fight-or-flight by inserting the unsettling into the friendly. There is an affectionate self-identification with trash and failure — the deadpan boast, the committed straight face. Nothing winks. The humor works because everyone in frame treats the absurdity as completely normal.

## Era & Origins

Late 2010s, creator unknown, born on Tumblr and sustained by Reddit (r/Bastardcore), Newgrounds, YouTube and novelty-shirt vendor sites. It is a deliberate extension of the cursed-image meme, differentiated by its explicit focus on mischief and delinquency. Its humor lineage runs through 2000s novelty-shirt culture (It's Happy Bunny, David & Goliath as precursors; Shirts That Go Hard, Wicked Clothes, T-Shirt Hell and similar vendors as core suppliers), and its photographic grammar borrows the high-flash digital look of early-2000s point-and-shoots even though the aesthetic itself is later.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles.**
1. Build the image around one harsh light source inside murk; contrast is the palette.
2. Degrade the capture: grain, noise, blur, flash falloff, hard shadow, tilted or cropped frame.
3. Stage the absurdity physically, before the shutter — no digital surrealism.
4. Apply the displacement rule: take one mundane, friendly object (skateboard, spoon, plush, pizza, sign, head) and place it where it does not belong.
5. Keep figures anonymous and amateur; the act is documented mid-mischief or as aftermath, never posed.
6. Use text as a prop in the world: marker handwriting, engraving, embroidery, defaced official signage, in a deadpan declarative voice.
7. Commit to the straight face: nothing in frame acknowledges the joke.

**Variation axes.** Subject matter may be anything domestic, urban, edible, animal or bureaucratic — a burning pot, a bullet-holed placard, a creature collage on carpet, adults on toddlers' ride-on toys. Setting ranges from night underpass to overcast daylight puddle to beige bedroom to motel hallway. Lighting may be flash, fire glow, sodium lamp, headlights, fluorescent tube or candle. Color handling is free in hue, fixed in contrast. Composition ranges from overhead flat-lay to bystander snapshot to cropped vertical. Mood ranges from quiet deadpan absurdity to open arson chaos. A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules, not any reference image.

**Avoid.** Clean, sharp, correctly exposed professional photography; studio or ring lighting; drone polish; cohesive graded palettes and tasteful minimalism; digital compositing, dream fog and existential text overlays; empty, agent-less liminal tableaux with no trace of mischief; performer-centered swagger, hero logos or album-cover styling; warm nostalgic affection for childhood props; celebratory frat-party confidence; image-macro meme captions in impact font; gore, genuine cruelty or glamorized serious crime — the transgression must stay petty, staged and ironic.

## Discrimination Cues

- **Liminal Space** shares night architecture and parking lots, but worships absence and even ambient light; Bastardcore always leaves an agent's trace — a cone head, a fire, a bullet hole — and its light is harsh and local.
- **Weirdcore** unsettles through amateur digital manipulation, dream logic and overlaid dread text; Bastardcore's weirdness exists physically in the world and is merely recorded badly.
- **Meme Rap** centers a performing ego with saturated graphics and cartoon luxury; Bastardcore's figures are faceless amateurs whose only vehicle is a shopping cart.
- **Olderbrothercore** uses similar low-fi domestic snapshots and turn-of-the-millennium props but with affectionate nostalgia; Bastardcore is the same house after the kids set the kitchen on fire.
- **Bro Culture** celebrates group masculinity in confident, often daylight party poses; Bastardcore's groups are caught by a flash at 2 a.m., self-degrading rather than self-aggrandizing.
- Against its parent **cursed images**, Bastardcore is the species with intent: mischief, delinquency and novelty-shirt irony, not mere accidental uncanniness.
