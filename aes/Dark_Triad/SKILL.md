# Dark Triad

Dark Triad (interchangeably called Blackpill, Lookism, or — for its soundtrack — TikTokTekk) is a 2020s internet aesthetic and TikTok-born subculture that surfaced around 2023. It takes the psychology term for the trio of narcissism, Machiavellianism and psychopathy and recasts it as a visual archetype: the cold, high-status, predatory male. The aesthetic is aggressive, monochromatic and heavily templated — less a photographic tradition than an editing grammar designed for vertical short-form video, where high-contrast black-and-white filters, a recurring pyramid logo overlay and a clinical vocabulary of facial ranking ("PSL score," "sub-5," "mogging") combine to glorify discipline, detachment and dominance. Beneath the gym-motivation veneer sits a contested culture of shock value and irony: the same template that edits Hollywood antiheroes and marble busts into menace is also used, deadpan, on absurd mundane subjects, so sincerity and satire are visually indistinguishable.

## Visual Identity

### Palette
The spine of the style is a value axis, not a hue axis: pure black (`#000000`) through charcoal and gunmetal greys (`#141414`–`#4a4a4a`) up to blown paper-white highlights (`#f0f0f0`–`#ffffff`), with chrome/silver (`#c0c0c0`–`#e6e6e6`, specular and metallic) as the only "prestige" finish. Hue is allowed only as a whisper: circulated edits sometimes carry a desaturated cold cast — teal-cyan (`#1e3a3a`–`#2f4f4f`), sickly green (`#22302a`), bruised violet (`#332a3a`) — or reserve one saturated accent, almost always blood red (`#8a0303`–`#b00000`), for a single element such as eyes. Rule: contrast carries the image; color never leads it. A fully saturated, warm, or evenly exposed color image is outside the style regardless of subject.

### Signature motifs (ranked by how centrally the culture documents them)
1. **The Dark Triad pyramid logo** — the definitive symbol: an outlined triangle containing stacked condensed caps ("THE DARK TRIAD"), its three edges labeled NARCISSISM, PSYCHOPATHY, MACHIAVELLIANISM. It appears as a glowing watermark, corner stamp, or flashing center overlay on top of footage, never as an in-scene physical object.
2. **High-contrast monochrome face treatment** — crushed blacks and blown highlights used to accentuate harsh facial shadows, sharp jawlines, cheekbone planes and "hunter eyes" (low, shadowed brow; direct unblinking gaze).
3. **The sigma-male archetype** — a conventionally handsome, groomed male subject: slicked or tousled dark hair, symmetrical features, expressionless or smirking-without-warmth.
4. **Looksmaxxing before/after and "mogging" narrative** — in stills this survives as split frames, ghosted transition doubles, or caption strips comparing jawlines; the implied cut from "weak" to "maxxed" is the genre's plot.
5. **Tailored suits and leather** — pinstripe or black suiting, crisp white shirt, tie; alternatively a black leather jacket as night-street armor.
6. **Cigarette smoke** — haze or a lit cigarette as the sole soft element in a hard image.
7. **Statue imagery** — classical busts and figures (Marcus Aurelius, David) standing in for the ideal face; marble read as monochrome flesh.
8. **Gym steel** — barbells, plates, chalk, sweat-sheen skin, progress-shot framing.
9. **Hardtekk / drift-phonk audio culture** — invisible in a still, but signaled by gym or night-club context and by track-slang typography ("CLAVICLE," "MAXILLA," "BLACK PILL").
10. **Archival villain footage** — the culture controversially splices criminals and movie predators (serial-killer mugshots, joker-faced villains, cold-eyed financiers) into montages; visually this reads as grainy archival monochrome inserted against cleaner footage.
11. **Satirical mundane subjects** — the parody wing applies the identical grave template to robots, animals, food, or cartoon characters; the template, not the subject, is the style.

### Materials and textures
Digital grain and phone-compression noise; posterization and thresholding that flatten midtones into graphic black-and-white shapes; halation around the logo's glow; smoke haze; concrete, cinderblock and brick walls; wool suiting cloth and glossy leather; cold marble; sweat and chrome in gym scenes; specular metal on the emblem. Surfaces should look processed — filtered, compressed, re-uploaded — rather than optically pristine.

### Recurring subjects
Men (photographic, cinematic or anime-stylized) in suits, leather, or gym wear; classical statues; night streets and parking structures; cinderblock stairwells and office interiors evoking finance-thriller cinema; crowds seen from behind a dominant figure; and, in the satire register, any ordinary object or character granted the same funeral-grade treatment.

### Composition and framing
Vertical 9:16 short-video framing dominates, with letterboxed horizontal variants. The subject sits center or offset while the logo occupies a corner or the center as a separate graphic layer; caption strips of condensed all-caps sans-serif appear top or bottom. Portraits run from extreme close-up (jaw, eyes, profile) to medium and full-figure night shots; low angles confer dominance; rear views and profiles deny the viewer warmth; large fields of pure black negative space isolate the figure. Symmetry is fetishized — centered faces, mirrored lighting — because facial symmetry is the culture's currency.

### Lighting
Single hard key from side or above, throwing half the face into shadow; rim light cutting the silhouette out of a black void; harsh on-camera flash against concrete; low-key night exteriors lit by one distant streetlamp; or a blown-white background silhouetting a dark figure. Light is a weapon used to carve bone structure, never to illuminate a room evenly.

## Mood & Values

The emotional register is cold dominance: ruthless discipline, emotional detachment, social manipulation, physical perfectionism and a "dark" charisma performed for the camera. Nothing is cozy, funny-warm, or vulnerable; even the smirk is a threat. The culture values self-optimization framed as conquest — the body as project, the gaze as weapon — and wraps insecurity in the costume of predation. A persistent layer of deadpan irony means the same image can be sincere self-mythology or shitpost; the style deliberately refuses to tell you which.

## Era & Origins

Emerged circa 2023 on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels as the most aggressive evolution of 2010s incel-forum looksmaxxing and blackpill communities, rebranded from appearance anxiety into a psychology-flavored superiority complex. Its soundtrack, hardtekk ("TikTokTekk"), overtook Brazilian Phonk as the dominant edit audio by 2026 — one-minute, hammer-beat tracks engineered for virality. Cinematic touchstones are antihero films and series: *American Psycho*, *Nightcrawler*, *The Wolf of Wall Street*, *Peaky Blinders*, *The Idol*. The aesthetic reacts against soft, therapeutic self-care culture by recasting self-improvement as warfare, and it carries documented contamination from far-right and misogynist pipelines — context an agent should know but never reproduce.

## Generation Guidance

**Principles**
1. Monochrome first: build the image on the black–grey–white–chrome axis; add hue only as a cold cast or one red accent.
2. Contrast as aggression: crush shadows to solid black, blow highlights to paper white, let midtones collapse (posterization welcome).
3. The face is the argument: jawline, brow shadow, symmetrical frontal or profile geometry, unwarm eyes.
4. Layer the emblem: the triangle logo with stacked caps text and three edge labels belongs on top of the image as watermark or glowing overlay, not inside the scene.
5. Use short-video grammar: vertical or letterboxed frame, condensed all-caps caption strips, grain, compression artifacts.
6. Light to carve, not to reveal: one hard source, half-shadow, rim against void.
7. Keep it cold: detachment, discipline, menace — or the exact same treatment applied deadpan to an absurd subject for the satire register.

**Variation axes (what may change)**
- *Subject*: suited men, leather-jacket night figures, gym athletes, classical statues, anime-stylized characters, movie-archetype villains, or — in satire mode — animals, objects, cartoon characters, food. Any subject within this range is legitimate.
- *Composition*: extreme close-up to full-figure; frontal, profile, or rear view; low-angle dominance; split/before-after frames; crowd-behind-figure; logo corner-stamped or center-flashed.
- *Lighting*: hard side key, top-down carve, on-camera flash, single streetlamp at night, blown-white backlight, near-total darkness with rim only.
- *Color handling*: pure black-and-white; monochrome under teal, green or violet cast; black-and-white plus one red accent; chrome highlights.
- *Mood*: sincere menace, gym-discipline triumph, or ironic deadpan parody — visually identical template.
- *Medium*: phone-screenshot photo, cinematic film still, posterized illustration, or clean anime render.

A correct image can use any subject within this range; it should echo the style's rules — monochrome spine, carved contrast, emblem layer, short-video grammar, cold affect — not any reference image. A statue alone in correct light, or a housecat given the funeral-grade template and pyramid watermark, reads as Dark Triad as faithfully as a suited portrait does.

**Avoid**
- Warm, saturated, or pastel palettes; golden hour; balanced naturalistic exposure.
- Soft diffuse lighting, high-key brightness, cozy domestic warmth, genuine smiling.
- Ornate serifs, handwritten scripts, playful rounded type; the style's type is condensed industrial caps.
- Neon carnival color and favela/funk energy (Brazilian Phonk); JDM cars, drift smoke and purple night neon (Drift Phonk); tweed, libraries, candlelit sepia and romantic scholarship (Dark Academia); sunlit marble, pagan/Nordic landscape and ethnic mythography (Hyperborean); tracksuits, squats, fluorescent club color and Cyrillic hardbass signage (Krushclub).
- Real identifiable criminals, dictators, or far-right symbols: documented in the culture's worst edits, but an agent should substitute fictional archetypes and keep the menace stylistic, not celebratory of real harm.
- Pristine optical clarity: no grain, no compression, no filter feel breaks the processed-edit identity.
